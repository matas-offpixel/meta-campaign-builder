A quote stays whole

Matas, 12 Nov: keep the artist team's approved quote intact.

Q13 of 2026-11-12-creative-lab-quote-style.html capped a quote at 96 characters, the writer's long-caption limit. That cap was invented in that record, not asked for, and it would have made Luke Dean's 98-character quote lose a sentence. This record replaces Q13. Q10–Q12, Q14 and Q15 stand.

Ruling

#Ruling
Q16 As long as the box holdsA quote may be as long as the caption box holds at the long size: 5 lines of 24 characters, 120 at most.
  • The test is the wrap, not a count. Q12's word-boundary wrap at 24 must make 5 lines or fewer, and every line must fit O99's box (x 120–944, ≤ 824 px measured). 120 is the most that can fit. A shorter quote whose words wrap to a sixth line doesn't fit.
  • Only past that does the Lab say This quote is too long — drop a sentence from the start or end. (Q13's sentence, kept) and offer Q1's sentence drop. The person drops the sentence; the Lab never does.
  • Approved words are never trimmed to fit: no characters cut, no ellipsis, no smaller size to squeeze the text in. C2's size steps are only for keeping off a face, and they run after the quote has fitted at its own size.
  • Captions are unchanged. The writer's captions keep the validator's own limits (18 a line and 4 lines at the standard size; 73–96 characters, 24 a line and 5 lines at the long size). The 120 applies to quotes only, because a quote's words aren't Off/Pixel's to shorten.
  • Luke's quote (Back in Southampton for this one… Closing out the year with Puzzle on 27th December. See you there, 98 characters) wraps to 5 lines of 24 and runs whole (CLQ6a).
