Overlay templates — issue 14: every video makes the 9:16

Matas, 10 Nov: video ads always run on Reels and Stories, so every video asset must make the 9:16. A feed-only video isn't run as a dark ad; the only feed-only video is a boosted existing post. O63 ruled the resolution floor per format, so a clip too small for 9:16 could still make a 4:5 or 1:1. This issue corrects that. The import already refuses such clips (#1176), so this is a record correction only, with no build. Issues 1–13 stand except where a row says otherwise.

Rulings

#Ruling
O93 Every video makes the 9:16This amends O63. Its 1.6× ceiling, the 675 px window, the filter (O69) and the render copy (O68) stand. What changes is where the floor applies.
  • The 9:16 decides. A clip is imported only if its 9:16 crop window, the largest 9:16 window in the source, is at least 675 px wide. Otherwise it is refused at import: "Clip too small for 9:16 — {w} px wide in the crop, needs 675". It isn't kept for 4:5 or 1:1 only.
  • A clip that makes the 9:16 makes its 4:5 and 1:1 too wherever their windows reach 675 px. A clip that makes the 9:16 but not a 4:5 or 1:1 skips only that format, with O63's line for it, and still runs.
  • This applies to every video template: T1 on a clip, T2 on a promoter clip (O82), and UGC caption renders. All of them run on Reels and Stories.
  • Stills are unchanged. They keep O19's no-upscaling, per format (O82). A feed-only still still runs in feed.
  • Clips already in the Lab that are under the floor are kept, never deleted. Their tile shows one status line, "Too small for 9:16", and they take no template, round or render.
  • Examples superseded:
    • H63's "Luke 1 makes the 1:1 only" becomes "Luke 1 (956 × 718; its 9:16 window is 404 px) is refused".
    • Issue 9's data note on Luke 1's 1:1 stands as a measurement, not an outcome.
