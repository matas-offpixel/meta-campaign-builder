Overlay templates — issue 15: video is one 9:16

Matas, 11 Nov: "I don't need 4:5 and 1:1 edits of videos, only a 9:16 edit that is fit for feed and 9x16 placement safe zones." So every video template renders the 9:16 only, and that one file has to read in two places. Feed shows about the centre 4:5 of it (y 285–1635). Reels and Stories show all of it, under their own controls, so O43's safe area applies (x 120–960, y 269–1248). This issue names the band both crops share, checks every current video slot against it, and moves the slots that fall outside. It also records O94 from the #1177 verdict: the T2 clip's spacing. Stills keep all three formats. Issues 1–14 stand except where a row says otherwise.

Rulings

#Ruling
O94 The T2 clip's spacingFrom the #1177 verdict (11 Nov), recorded here. On #1177's 9:16 roll-call clips the lineup cleared the block by 18 px, and the claim cleared the date by 18 px. The details' own line gap is 9 px, so the claim read as part of the date. Matas's example leaves 153 and 204 px.
  • On the 9:16 clip, at least 0.04 H (77 px) of clear space sits between the lineup's last line and the block, and between the block's last line and the date's cap top.
  • When the block doesn't fit, the lineup moves up first, no higher than 353 px (the verdict said 0.16 H; O98 changes it). Then the block steps down as one, through T2's steps (0.0485 H, then 0.042 H).
  • If it still doesn't fit at the last step, the run is refused: "No room for the claim on the 9:16 clip — shorten it".
  • The block is centred in the space left between the lineup and the date.
  • The 0.005 slot check (H86) reads the moved lineup top whenever the lineup has moved.

  • T1 on a clip, T2 on a promoter clip and UGC caption renders (quote mode included) render the 9:16 only. No 4:5 or 1:1 video is proposed, rendered, put in a round, filed or launched.
  • A round makes one render per clip × caption pairing.
  • At launch, the one 9:16 goes to Feed, Reels and Stories. Feed shows its centre 4:5.
  • Stills keep 9:16, 4:5 and 1:1: T1's artist card, T2 on the panel and T2 on a photo, all unchanged.

O96 The band
  • Feed shows the 9:16 as its centre 4:5, which is y 285–1635 at the full width.
  • Reels and Stories keep type inside O43's safe area, x 120–960, y 269–1248.
  • The band is their intersection: x 120–960, y 285–1248. Every type ink box sits inside it, including the claim, its backing's ink edge, the logo and the caption's stroke.
  • The top keeps a Feed inset. No type ink sits above y 353. That is 0.05 of the 4:5 view (68 px) below Feed's crop edge, the same inset O54 gave the lockup in a 4:5 render. Type 3 px under the crop edge reads as clipped in Feed.
  • The bottom keeps a descender's room. The last line of type has its baseline on 1238 (0.645 H), so a comma or descender still ends by 1248.
  • Bands and backings may run outside the band. They aren't type.
  • Below 1248, Feed still shows 387 px of the footage, with no type over it. That is intended.
