Dashboard v2 HE2: the words for CIRQLIN's page audience

This record amends 2026-11-13-dashboard-v2-phase-2.html (E1, E2, E3, EV2), which stands except where it's named here. CIRQLIN's GET /api/partner/page-audience (cirqlin #631, merged 15 Nov) returns counts for one published page, and three of them differ from what E2 assumed:

  • Consent is one marketing yes or no per fan. There's no separate email, SMS or WhatsApp consent. channels_offered lists the channels the page offers, and it's the same on every signup.
  • community_joined is exact: signups who joined the community, counted by the day they joined.
  • community_link_clicks counts every click on the page's WhatsApp invite code, from every source. When community_code_shared is true, other pages use the same code.

I read the merged code. whatsapp_with_phone counts signups who said yes to marketing and left a phone, and only on a page that offers WhatsApp; otherwise it's 0. partner_opt_in counts partner consents that haven't been revoked. Spam is excluded from every count. HE2's ingest PR is judged against this record. No new frames.

Rulings

#Ruling
PA1 · Marketing opt-in
  • One figure: "Marketing opt-in". It reads "{n} · {pct} of signups".
  • Beside it, the channels the page offers: "offered: email · SMS · WhatsApp". It names only the channels in channels_offered, always in that order.
  • E2's "consent snapshot counts per channel (email, SMS, WhatsApp)" is withdrawn. Those counts don't exist, so nothing on the panel splits consent by channel.
  • The consent wording on the page stays fixed platform-wide, as before. This rule only names the figure.
PA2 · Reachable on WhatsApp

whatsapp_with_phone is a figure, not only a denominator. It replaces E2's step 2:

  • Step 2 is titled "Reachable on WhatsApp", with the subline "said yes to marketing and left a phone". EV2's "WhatsApp opt-ins" and "said yes to WhatsApp" are replaced, because there's no WhatsApp-only yes any more.
  • On a page that doesn't offer WhatsApp, the step reads "WhatsApp isn't offered on this page", not 0.
  • E2's cross-check against the show's CQ-{slug} Bird list stays. When the two differ by more than 2 %, both are shown.
  • It's also the join rate's denominator (PA3).
PA3 · Community, the headline

Amends E2's community step and its join rate. The tile's headline comes from the first of these that exists:

  1. A hand-entered member count, as E2 rules: "{n} members · entered {date}". Under it goes a second line: "{j} joined from signups · CIRQLIN".
  2. community_joined: "{j} joined from signups", with the source "CIRQLIN · exact". It carries no "est.".
  3. The link-click estimate, labelled as E2 rules ("community link clicks · est."). It's used only for a page whose read has no community_joined.

The join rate is community_joined ÷ Reachable on WhatsApp, both from CIRQLIN, so it compares the same people. An entered member count can include members who never signed up, so it's never the numerator. This replaces E2's "uses whichever figure the tile shows".

  • Over a period other than "Since launch", the join rate isn't shown. community_joined counts by the day of joining and signups by the day of signing up, so in a window they aren't the same people. The tile shows "{j} joined {period}" instead.
  • E3's "Community at presale": the entered count nearest before presale, else community_joined up to presale open (exact, no "est."), else the link-click estimate.
PA4 · Invite link clicks
  • Never the headline once community_joined exists.
  • When the code isn't shared, it's a third line on the tile, internal and client view: "{n} invite link clicks · every source".
  • When community_code_shared is true, it's hidden on the client view and the share link. Internally it reads "{n} clicks on an invite shared with other pages", because the clicks aren't this page's alone.
  • Never added up. Venue and client totals leave link clicks out, so a shared code is never counted twice.
PA5 · Partner opt-in
  • Shown, internal and client view, as "Partner opt-in · {n}", with the subline "agreed to hear from the page's partner promoters".
  • It's a count of consents collected on the client's own page, with no person data, so it stays on the share link too.
  • The row is left out on a page whose form has no partner tick box, rather than showing 0.
PA6 · Geography

As E1 rules:

  • cities are named at 10 or more signups;
  • "Everywhere else" and "Couldn't tell" are always shown, and "Couldn't tell" is never spread across the others;
  • the three add up to the page's signups.

If a read doesn't add up, the geography block is withheld, with "Signup places don't add up for this read yet". An internal note names the page.

PA7 · Which signups
  • Every figure here counts CIRQLIN's counted signups, with spam excluded. So it can sit below the page's raw total in CIRQLIN's own editor.
  • The source line reads "CIRQLIN · counted signups, spam removed".
  • A window follows the page's own timezone (daily_timezone).
