Purchase campaigns, issue 3: halving to whole £5, and PF1's spend

This record amends 2026-11-14-purchase-campaigns.html (PC4) and 2026-11-14-purchase-campaigns-2.html (PC11). Both stand except where they're named here. It answers the one question from the #1240 review: halving a rate to whole £5s can't always land on half, so which way does it round? No new frames.

Rulings

#Ruling
PC12 · A step down rounds up

Amends PC4's "Down, halving the daily rate, never below £5 a day". The new rate is half the old one, rounded up to the next whole £5, and never below £5. A step down should never cut more than half.

  • At £5 a day it holds, as PC4 rules: "£5 a day is the floor".
  • The commitment it gives back is the change in rate × the days left. It's bounded by the Purchase campaign's own 1.1 × spend (PC7).
  • The line's words don't change: "Scale Purchase down: £{a} → £{b} a day".
  • A step up is unchanged: double, or the largest whole £5 the cap and the floor allow (PC4).
PC13 · PF1's spend is the run's

Corrects PC11's last line. PF1's Budget line is "£4,500 for this event · £2,025.66 spent to 12 Nov · £2,474.34 left in the plan", and at 393 "£4,500 · £2,026 spent · £2,474 left". Here's why:

  • The run reads data to Thu 12 Nov. Its spend to date is summed over the same rows, and P26-BDAY's spend to 12 Nov is £2,025.66.
  • The first record's £2,051.30 was my read to 13 Nov, a day the run doesn't cover.

The rule PC11 set stands: the day shown is the last day the figure covers. Only PC11's "stands as drawn" is withdrawn.

The floor (PC7) is different. It uses the campaign's live spend, because Meta's 1.1 × counts what's actually been spent. That's why PF2's floor uses £272.41, Locklead's spend to 13 Nov.
