Rulings added in issue 4

#Ruling
O26 The claim slot is the panelOn a kit whose T2 draws a panel (Puzzle's taped panel), the claim sits on the panel, so the slot is the panel, not 86 % W. A line that runs past the panel's edge changes ground mid-word and reads as a mistake.
  • Width: each line within the panel's ink width less 0.04 W (43 px) on each side, and never wider than 86 % W.
  • Height: the claim block (first cap top to last baseline, qualifier included) within the panel's ink box less 0.02 H (38 px) top and bottom.
  • The panel is measured by its ink box at its placed size, never its canvas (O11). In the 1:1 the panel and the slot are both at 0.8×.
  • O8's steps and drops, the fit meter and "can't shorten" all measure against this slot; the meter's figure becomes "{widest} of {slot} px".
  • The panel never grows or stretches to fit a claim (O11). A kit with no panel keeps O4's 86 % W.
  • The claim box tested by O19 is this slot.
O27 The roll-call's shorteningNo — O8's number form was not meant for the roll-call. Line 1's single RELEASE is A14's shared noun for the list ("EARLY BIRD, 1ST & 2ND RELEASE SOLD OUT"), so the number form never removes it.
  • Line 1 is never shortened. Line 2 takes O8's TICKETS and NOW drops; it takes the number form only while line 1 keeps RELEASE on the frame.
  • Line 2 reads "{live release} SELLING FAST" at least (A21). claim-slot.ts on #1156 must follow the generator fixed on #1158; no RUNNING LOW anywhere.
  • A roll-call that still doesn't fit at step 1 is flagged "can't shorten" and has no Approve on T2, like any claim (rule 14). It stays available on T1, where it has its own slot. It is not swapped for line 2 alone: that would be a different claim, and the ladder proposes claims, not the template.
O28 Breaking a one-line claimA one-line claim wider than the slot breaks onto two lines at the same step (accepted: T2's steps are two-line steps, O8). Two limits:
  • The break never falls inside a release name ("2ND RELEASE", "GENERAL SALE", "EARLY BIRD"); among the allowed breaks the one with the narrowest wider line wins.
  • A split never makes a number token: "FINAL 50 / TICKETS" draws both lines at the step's cap. A number is enlarged only when the approved claim itself puts it on its own line (O4).
  • Colour belongs to the claim, not the line: a "FINAL {n} TICKETS" claim is kit red on both lines when split.
O29 The bands on T2O25's scope left T2 to its own draft; H26 is that draft. T2 carries O25's bands unchanged, over the ground and panel, under all type and the lockup, so a T2 beside a T1 in one set reads as one kit. Card (d) shows T2 passes without them, so they are for consistency and the corners, not the claim. Matas judges them on the drafts with the artist card's (H25). The 18.4 % sample is accepted as the one-pixel artefact reported; H19's ±1 % acceptance is read on a 3 × 3 average, not a single pixel.
O30 No bare-ground T2Card (e) is dropped, not a template option. Without the panel the details corner fails at 31.2 % on Puzzle's paint, and a T2 option that fails its own corner test on the kit it was made for isn't one. A T2 on a panel kit always draws the panel. A kit that wants a panel-free message is a kit question for its designer, ruled when one asks.
O31 The video questionsVIDEO-READINESS.md's ten questions go into overlay-templates issue 5, ruled after the T2 and artist-card statics pass — Matas's order is statics perfected before video. The file did not reach the design thread: send its ten questions with the issue-5 request.
