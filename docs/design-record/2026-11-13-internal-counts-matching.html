Internal Counts: how the emails are matched

This record re-rules D8 of 2026-11-13-dashboard-v2.html, which stays as merged. Here it rules how an email becomes a count. The rest of D8 stands: paid vs comps, show, tier and outlet rows, the priority, and one source per show-day.

Rulings

#Ruling
M1 · Which emails are counts

changedOps' rule, with the pattern widened by two cases. An email is a count when all four hold:

  • the sender's address is at electric-group.co.uk (that domain exactly, not its subdomains);
  • it carries a PDF attachment;
  • its subject doesn't start with a reply or forward prefix, matched by /^\s*(re|fwd?|aw|sv)\s*:/i
  • its subject matches /^\s*internal\s+(shows?\s+)?(\(?ticket\)?\s+)?counts?\b/i

The widening adds "Shows" and "(ticket)". All ten real subjects since 30 Oct match, and so do the D8 wordings "Internal Counts" and "Internal Show (ticket) counts". "Internal comms update" doesn't match. Checked 13 Nov.

  • Replies are not counts. A reply from the domain in the same thread ("Re: Internal Show counts 06.11.26") is stored as a note on that day's count. Its text runs M3's check, so an inline "sold another 40" still reaches the review list. It is never parsed into the counts.
  • Near misses. An email from the domain with a PDF and "count" in its subject that fails the pattern doesn't become a count. It goes to the review list as "Looks like a count but wasn't read as one".
  • Corrections. A second count email for the same count date replaces the first for that date. Both are kept, the newer one is used, and a review note says so.
  • Idempotent on the message id, as D8 already rules.
M2 · The count date

passThe PDF's TIER TOTALS date is the count date. If it disagrees with the subject's date, that sheet's counts are held, not stored, and the review list asks which date is right.

  • Dates are always read day first (UK): dd.mm.yy in the subject; dd/mm/yy and dd/mm/yyyy in the PDF.
  • With no date in the subject, the PDF date stands alone.
  • With no date in the PDF, the sheet is unreadable (M5). The subject's date is never used on its own.
M3 · Sales mentioned only in the email

passThe body is stored with the count. A body (or a reply in the thread) that mentions tickets, sold or total next to a number puts one review item on that count, quoting the sentence. Matas confirms it as a manual entry, typing the figure himself. It's never parsed into the counts.

  • A confirmed entry is manual, so it outranks the sheet for that show and day (D8's priority).
  • Example: 30 Oct's "Grand total sold for this event is 1974". If he confirms it, Enzo reads 1,974 from manual for 30 Oct.
  • A sale for a show we don't run ("Dusky Bristol") gets "Not a show we run" (M5), so it never asks again for that name.
M4 · Sheet errors

changedOps' checks, with the £150 ceiling made per event:

  • Price looks wrong when a tier's price equals that column's top-row count (IPC on 12 Nov: 1st £200.00 with 200 sold, 3rd £363.00 with 363, 4th £579.00 with 579). It's also flagged when a price is above the event's highest ticket price: the dearest paid tier in its allocations, or £150 when none is set.
    • A flat £150 would flag a real VIP table, so it's only the fallback.
    • The counts are still stored. That show's revenue from that sheet is withheld, and the review list says so.
  • −£1.00 is an unused tier, not a refund. It's left out of counts and revenue, with no review item.
  • Sums don't add up when a show's tier counts don't sum to its TIER TOTALS count. That show's count from that sheet is held, and the review list asks which to use. Other shows on the same sheet are stored as normal.
M5 · The review list

An internal-only list on the dashboard (D11: never on a client link), headed "To check · {n}". When there's nothing, it reads "Nothing to check".

  • Every item shows the email's subject, sender and received time, and opens the email.
  • Resolving an item moves it to "Checked" with who and when.
  • Nothing resolves itself.
  • Held counts enter the dashboard only when their item is resolved.
