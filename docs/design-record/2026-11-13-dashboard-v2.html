Dashboard v2: the sales-vs-spend model, made general

Matas's "NX Newcastle sales vs spend" (artifact 1WbbWGeA1hvTfx7dkS9duj, v13) is the model for the internal dashboard and the client share links. The Electric Brixton thread wrote down exactly what it does (functional spec, 13 Nov). D1–D7 take its definitions as they are, changed only where D8–D13 say. D8–D13 rule what the model can't do: ticket truth in the database, per-event config instead of constants, a reference library instead of the Enzo case, client links, all channels' spend, and the consistency fixes.

One fact the record turned up: the model hard-codes a capacity of 2,000 per show, but events.capacity stores 1,500 for every NX show that has a value, and NX26-LOCKLEAD, NX27-EWANMCVICAR and NX27-JULIANFIJMA have none. D9 makes the stored value the only source, so which is right is open for Matas before the build reads it.

D1–D7 · The model, as it is

#Ruling
D1 · Shows and campaigns
  • Scope: the client's events from config (D9), never a code regex. A show leaves the default view the day after it plays. "Include finished shows" brings it back (D13).
  • Campaign → show: linked (the campaign id is in events.meta_campaign_id) beats tagged (the name contains [EVENT_CODE], first match). A tagged-only campaign carries "Not linked to event" on the internal view.
  • The umbrella (D9's flag) is its own chip and its own row. It is never in "All shows" spend, tickets, cost per ticket or the trajectory. The header shows it as "Separate {client} umbrella spend £X".
D2 · Spend
  • Per show, per day, per platform: the larger of the rollup's figure and the sum of that platform's linked and tagged campaigns. Then summed over days, and over platforms (D12).
  • That catches campaigns the rollup misses without double-counting. The rollup-only and unlinked figures are kept for audit, never shown.
  • Today is excluded everywhere: spend runs to yesterday, as in the model.
D3 · Engagement
  • From ad_daily_insights for mapped campaigns: link clicks, landing page views, and initiate checkout and add to cart, summed from the actions.
  • Meta's default attribution (7-day click, 1-day view).
  • TikTok and Google engagement aren't added to these sums. Their clicks are a different count, so blending them would make the figure no platform's (D12 covers their spend only).
D4 · Period figures

The model's definitions, with D13's fixes:

  • A window is two consecutive ticket counts for a show. Its tickets are the difference between them, its days are the calendar days between, and its spend runs from the day after the first count through the second. A window whose second count is later than the last spend day is incomplete.
  • Sold is the latest count (paid tickets, D8). For All shows, it's the sum over shows that have counts.
  • Tickets in a period is the sum of window tickets, with a window that straddles the period start pro-rated by its days inside (D13). Since launch, it's the latest count.
  • Cost per ticket is blended, not ad-attributed: spend over tickets on complete windows. Since launch, spend is capped at the latest count date (D13).
  • Checkout actions: initiate checkouts plus add-to-carts, labelled as actions (D13). Also £ per checkout action, LPVs and £ per LPV, and clicks and CPC.
  • Registrations are CIRQLIN signups on the show's linked page (D9), or "No CIRQLIN page". £ per registration uses spend between the first and last signup day, because signup pages close at on-sale.
D5 · The daily chart
  • Total sold is a straight line between counts, with a dot on each real count. Before presale, or before a first count of 0, it's 0. Before a non-zero first count it's empty. For All shows it's empty on any day a show lacks a value.
  • Tickets a day and cost per ticket hold flat across each window, drawn as a step.
  • The hover names days between counts as estimated: "Estimated between counts on 5 Nov and 12 Nov".
  • Clicks, LPVs, checkout actions and their costs are same-day sums and ratios.
  • Daily £ per registration is only drawn on days inside the signup window (D13).
D6 · Trajectory
  • Pace: count-to-count, from the latest count back to the latest count at least 7 (or 14) days earlier, falling back to the first count.
  • Projection to show day: the latest count, plus 7-day pace for the days before the final week, plus 7-day pace × f for the final week's days.
  • Cases: holds (f = 1), and the reference library's average and best (D10). This replaces "Like Enzo Siragusa" and "Like DJ EZ".
  • Range: "Projected on show day: {holds}–{high} of {allocation}". It's always capped at the show's sellable allocation (D9), for one show or all shows. The model left one show uncapped, which drew past the room.
  • Single show: the dots, a dashed pace line to D−7, then the three cases fanned out, the allocation line, a dotted "needed" line and a show-day rule. Tiles give "needed {n} a day" and "sells out {date} at this pace", or "{n} short at this pace".
  • All shows: one bar per show, layered sold → holds → high, against each show's allocation. Clicking a bar selects that show.
D7 · Controls

As the model has them:

  • Show chips: All shows, each show by date, the umbrella last. Kept in the URL (show), and selectable from the by-show table and the trajectory bars.
  • Period presets: since launch, 30, 14, 7 and 3 days, and yesterday (period). They drive the tiles, the chart's range, the campaigns table's "in period" column and the by-show table. The trajectory and header always run to date.
  • Twelve metric tiles that toggle chart lines. Spend and Total sold are on by default. All / None, and "Pick at least one metric" when none is on.
  • Axes: "Index to own peak" is forced on when three unit types are picked. Two types share dual axes.
  • Line pairs: volume solid, its cost dashed in the same colour.
  • Campaigns table: in-period spend, with "Spending", "Stopped {date}" and "Not linked to event". Hover shows real values.

D8–D13 · What the model can't do yet

#Ruling
D8 · Ticket truth: the Internal Counts ingest
  • What it reads. Emailed PDFs whose subject is "Internal Counts" or "Internal Show (ticket) counts" (the second since 30 Oct). Subjects match exactly as written; nothing is normalised.
  • What it writes. One row per show, tier and outlet into tier_channel_sales, with the count date as snapshot_at. The show's total goes into ticket_sales_snapshots with a new source, internal_counts.
  • Idempotent on the message id. The same email read twice writes nothing new. A corrected email the next day is a new count, not an overwrite.
  • Paid only. "Sold" is paid tickets. Guest list and comps are stored as their own tiers, flagged as comps. They never count as sold, never enter cost per ticket, and show only as a context line ("plus 218 comps"). This is the model's DJ EZ arithmetic made general: 523 − 129 guest list − 89 VIP comps = 305 paid.
  • Show matching. A PDF's show name matches an event through a stored alias on the event (D9). Anything unmatched, or a page that doesn't parse, goes to a review list with the message id. Nothing is half-written.
  • Backfill. The inbox's history is read once, so finished shows (NX26-DJEZ, NX26-MF, NX26-FOLAMOUR) have counts for D10.
  • Priority. Today it's manual > xlsx_import > fourthefans > eventbrite. internal_counts goes second: manual > internal_counts > xlsx_import > fourthefans > eventbrite.
    • A manual entry is a person correcting a figure, so it still wins.
    • Internal Counts is the promoter's own box-office total across every outlet, so it beats a periodic xlsx export and any single platform's count.
  • One source per show-day. Sources are never summed or blended. The tile names the one it used: "Sold 812 · Internal Counts, 12 Nov".
  • Other clients keep their sources: 4TheFans and Eventbrite connections, xlsx imports and manual entries, under the same priority. A client without counts gets D4's ticket figures as "no ticket counts yet", and the spend and engagement half still works.
  • Which inbox it reads is ops' question for Matas. The record doesn't pick one.
D9 · Per-event config

These fields replace the model's constants. They're set on the event page, and nothing is inferred from codes or slugs.

  • Capacity, from events.capacity.
  • Sellable allocation, when it differs from capacity: the sum of tier_channel_allocations for paid tiers, else capacity. The trajectory and "needed" use the allocation.
  • Umbrella flag, a yes/no on the event. An umbrella has no trajectory, no tickets and no cost per ticket.
  • CIRQLIN page link, the page id (or the CIRQLIN event's external_event_code). This replaces the hard-coded slug list.
  • Reference set, optional: by default the client's library (D10). An event can name its own shows to compare against.
  • Count aliases, the show name(s) as Internal Counts writes them (D8).

Missing values say so. With no capacity, the trajectory says "Set capacity on the event to see the sell-out line", and there's no 2,000 or any other default. With no page link, Registrations says "No CIRQLIN page".

D10 · The reference-lift library
  • One library per client. It holds that client's finished shows that have counts at D−21, D−7 and the final count.
  • The lift: tickets a day over the final 7 days, over tickets a day over the 14 days before that. Counts are interpolated to D−21 and D−7, paid tickets only (D8), and the lift is floored at 1.
  • Excluded: any show that reached its allocation before D−7 ("sold out before its final week"), because the room capped its finish, not demand. A show with no final count is excluded too. This is why Enzo goes: it sold out early, and its counts stop on 14 Oct.
  • Stored with its evidence: show code, show date, the three counts used, comps excluded, the lift, and when it was computed. It's never recomputed silently.
  • The two cases on the trajectory: "Like your average finish (×{avg}, {n} shows)" and "Like your best finish (×{max}, {show})". The hover lists the shows.
  • Fewer than 2 shows in the library: only "Holds" is drawn, with "Opens after your 2nd finished show with counts".
  • Never another client's shows.
  • Electric Brixton today: DJ EZ (×3.32 in the spec), with Modern Funktion (NX26-MF) and Folamour (NX26-FOLAMOUR) once D8's backfill gives them counts. That's the 28 Oct report's average-and-max, made standing.
D11 · Client surfaces
  • Server-side calcs. The same calc module serves both views, run on the server and handed out behind the existing client share token (/share/client/[token]). The page never queries the database from the viewer's connection, so a client needs no access.
  • Scope. The token reaches its own client's shows only.
  • Internal only: the campaigns table's "Not linked to event" pill, the audit figures and the review list. The client sees the same tiles, chart, trajectory and campaigns by name.
  • Freshness: the same stamps as internal (D13). The figures are cached for at most 5 minutes.
D12 · Spend across channels
  • Per day, per platform: the larger of the platform's own figure and its rollup column. That's ad_spend for Meta, tiktok_spend for TikTok and google_ads_spend for Google, summed per day.
  • The channel split shows under the Spend tile ("Meta £1,840 · TikTok £220") and as a stacked spend chart on the Spend line.
  • A platform with no data for the show isn't shown, rather than drawn as £0. A platform with real £0 spend is shown.
  • Cost per ticket uses total spend across channels.
D13 · Fixes and additions
  • Since-launch cost per ticket: spend is capped at the latest count date.
  • Windows that straddle a period start are pro-rated by their days inside the period.
  • Daily £ per registration is windowed to the signup window, as the period figure is. Outside it, the day is empty, not divided.
  • "Checkouts + carts" becomes "Checkout actions", with the hover note "Checkouts and add-to-carts. One person can do both."
  • "Include finished shows" is off by default. When on, finished shows join the chips greyed, and their final count and actual finish show against their library entry.
  • An allocation band per tier: behind the Total sold line, a stepped band of the released tiers' cumulative allocation, from tier_channel_allocations. "Needed a day" uses what's still on sale, not the whole capacity.
  • Freshness stamps per source in the header: "Counts to 12 Nov · Spend to 12 Nov · Signups to 12 Nov". A stamp turns amber when its source is more than 2 days behind yesterday.
