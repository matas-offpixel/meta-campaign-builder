Overlay templates — issue 2: the PSD corrected, the artist card, photo rules, extension, the cut-out

Extends 2026-11-08-overlay-templates.html (issue 1, rulings O1–O17, hand-back H1–H12). Issue 1 stands except where a row below says otherwise. This issue records what the Puzzle 4th Birthday PSD audit and the #1147 artist-card prototype settled: the PSD's real layer structure, the artist card's slot table, the rules a photo must pass, when a photo may be extended, and the cut-out variant on a plain ground. Every rule here was ruled in the #1147 verdicts (rounds 1–4, 9–10 Nov) and is gathered here unchanged unless the row says it is new. The frames are the three variant-A cards that passed.

2 · Rulings added in issue 2

#Ruling
O18 The artist cardAn artist card is T1 with the lockup slot added and the claim slot empty (O10 amended: "a T1 with its claim set" becomes this). No PART 1, no lineup list, no CTA, no claim. On the floating kit the type stays at the top, as on the poster and the Locklead cards; a frame kit's own layout (Ironworks puts the name low) is that kit's, not this one's. Slots on the 1080 × 1920 master, the 4:5 rectangle being the band y 285–1635:
  • Insets. Nothing sits closer than 76 px (7 % W) to the 4:5 rectangle's top or bottom edge — the side minimum of O2 applied to the top and bottom. This replaces O3's 0.027 H slack for every floating-kit T1.
  • Lockup. The kit's lockup piece, centred, placed by its ink box (O11): ink width 0.28 W (302 px), ink top at y 361 (0.188 H), height from the piece's own aspect (≈ 92 px at that width). The #1147 prototype placed the piece by its canvas (ink 269 × 82); the cards stand, the template build uses the ink box.
  • Name. Cap 0.1146 H (220 px), cap top 28 px under the lockup's ink (≈ y 482). O3's steps: down until it fits 80 % W, floor 6 % H, two lines below the floor, never condensed or scaled horizontally.
  • Info. The PSD's three lines, from the live layer, coral on ">" only. Cap 0.031 H (60 px), pitch 0.043 H (83 px), first cap top 36 px under the name. The block ends near 0.51 H.
  • Shadow. O6 once on the whole text group, lockup included.
  • Formats. The 4:5 is a pure centre crop of the master — every element inside it with the 76 px insets. The 1:1 is O1's re-sit at 0.8× (lockup top 61, name and info at 0.8×), the photo extended at the sides (variant A) or the ground re-covered (variant B). Nothing below 0.812 H.
  • Face band. Top of the head at or below 0.53 H; the eyes between 0.58 and 0.64 H; the chin at or above 0.76 H. No face, head or hand inside the text box (0.188–0.512 H).
O19 Photo rulesA photo is used as variant A only when every rule passes, each printed per card; a photo that fails is never covered with a scrim on the floating kit.
  • Contrast. In the text box (0.188–0.512 H × 0.07–0.93 W), measured on the placed photo before any type: no more than 10 % of the pixels below 3:1 against white (WCAG relative luminance).
  • One subject. The face detector's count is printed and must be 1.
  • No upscaling. The crop is used at 1.0× source pixels or less (at least 1080 × 1920 of source for the 9:16).
  • Face size. The detector's face box is at least 0.20 W wide (216 px). (Ruled in round 2 to replace a 270 px floor that the face band made impossible: a face box is about half the head's height.)
  • Eyes measurable. Detector confidence 0.5 or more, both eye centres inside the face box, at least a quarter of its width apart. A photo whose eyes can't be found can't be checked against the face band and is out (a pure profile, Enzo 46).
  • Face alpha (variant B, and the person mask in O20). At least 99 % of the face box at alpha ≥ 0.9, and no pixel below 0.5 inside the inner 80 % of the box. A share rule, not a strict minimum: zeros in the box's outer border are allowed (Enzo 11 copy (2): 100.0 % share, minimum 0.00 at the corner).
  • Order. Variant A (the photo, extended per O20 if it needs it), then variant B (O21), then skip.
O20 ExtensionA placed photo may be extended where the face band or the format needs more than the source gives.
  • Edges. The top and the sides only, never the bottom (it almost always cuts the body).
  • The strip. The 48 px strip along that edge of the placed photo, at output scale.
  • Plain tests (luminance, 0–255): the high-frequency residual — luminance minus a 31-tap Gaussian (σ 5) of itself — has a standard deviation of 4 or less; and no more than 2 % of the strip's pixels have a normalised Sobel magnitude (the raw 3 × 3 response ÷ 8, in levels per pixel) above 20.
  • Colour tests (CIELAB, on the strip after the 31-tap blur) — all three must pass: (a) column profile: take each column's mean Lab along the strip's length; the largest ΔE between that profile and its own 31-tap blur is 3 or less — smooth gradients pass, foliage, brick and crowds fail; (b) drift across the strip: per column, ΔE between the strip's outer 16 px and inner 16 px; the 95th percentile is 3 or less; (c) seam after fill: on the rendered card, per column, ΔE between the 8 px either side of the join; the 95th percentile is 2 or less.
  • Not through the subject. A person-segmentation mask (alpha > 0.5, computed at source resolution and placed with the photo) has no pixel in the strip. On the top edge the head's top is at least 0.05 H below the strip, and the extension ends at least 0.05 H above the head's top, so the face band is always real pixels. A column-around-the-eyes proxy is not a mask.
  • Limits. Top: up to 0.30 H (576 px). Sides: up to 0.12 W per side in 9:16 and 4:5; the 1:1 re-sit's side extension as O1.
  • Fill. Only the strip's own colours, stretched along the extension direction and blurred, with the strip's own grain (noise measured on the strip) added back. Never mirrored, cloned or generated.
  • On failure the photo goes to variant B, not to skip.
  • Disclosure. The card's approval line reads "photo extended {top / sides} by {n} px".
  • Measured. Enzo 1 _v4 passes: 540 px at the top (0.281 H), residual 2.15, Sobel 0.00 %, refilled from 48 px with grain. Reeshy 17 37 02 fails (b) at 19.98 — (a) read 1.07 — and its _v4 card showed the seam Matas rejected.
O21 The cut-out on a plain ground — matte pendingVariant B: the artist cut out of the photo and placed on a plain ground, the type in the same O18 slots. Matas's rule (10 Nov): when a background can't be blended, the cut-out goes on a plain ground; it must look photographed, not pasted. The paint-silhouette variant (B1 in the round-3 ruling) is withdrawn — "too harsh / too rigid" — and is not built.
  • Ground. Flat charcoal #32322f (the taped panel's own colour; re-sample it from the smart object), or a soft radial lift: centred on the face's x at 0.60 H, radius 0.70 W (756 px), the centre the charcoal raised 8 L* (≈ #454541), #1e1e1c at the radius and beyond, smooth, no visible ring. Below 0.85 H the ground darkens linearly to 25 % black at the frame's bottom edge. Kit red and coral are not grounds (coral is 2.8:1 with white).
  • The figure. The photo's own pixels through the segmentation alpha. Nothing generated or inpainted.
  • Matte edge. Refined at source resolution (guided filter, radius 8). Feather 2–3 px on hair (the soft-alpha region) and 1–1.5 px on body edges, at output size.
  • Colour decontamination. For every pixel with 0.05 < alpha < 0.98, the foreground colour is solved against the local source background (the median of the alpha < 0.05 pixels within 24 px). Spill test: in the 6 px band just inside the edge (alpha ≥ 0.5), at most 2 % of pixels within ΔE 12 of that local background. No-halo test: in the 4 px band just outside the edge, at most 2 % of pixels within ΔE 10 of the source's own background.
  • Light wrap. The ground behind, screen-blended into the figure's edge: 25 % at the edge falling to 0 at 6 px (8 px on hair).
  • Shadow. An ambient shadow from the figure's alpha — blur 0.04 W (43 px), offset 0.01 W (11 px) down, black at 30 %, multiplied onto the ground only — plus the ground's bottom darkening. O6 is not used on the figure; it stays on the type.
  • Unify, bounded and printed per card. Contrast change within ±10 %; saturation change between −15 % and 0; a white-balance shift toward the ground's neutral of ΔE 4 or less on the figure's mid-greys; the face box's median colour moves ΔE 3 or less; no relighting. One monochrome grain layer over the whole card (ground and figure, under the type): σ the photo's own measured luma noise, clamped to 2–5 levels, 1 px grain.
  • Where the figure ends. Bottom-anchored, or ending anywhere from 0.80 H to the frame's bottom with its lowest 0.12 H (230 px) faded to alpha 0 on a smoothstep, landing in the darkened ground. Never a hard horizontal cut inside the frame.
  • Side cut. Allowed when the photo's own edge cuts the person and that cut sits flush on the frame's matching side; the face's centre may then sit between 0.38 and 0.62 W (otherwise 0.45–0.55). The cut never shows as a line inside the frame; the head is never cut. In the 1:1 the figure layer moves out by the re-sit offset (108 px) so the cut stays flush on the square's matching side; the lockup and type keep their 0.8× positions; the face's centre stays within 0.38–0.62 W of the square.
  • Kept from O18–O19. The slot table, the face band, the 216 px face box, the face-alpha share rule, no upscaling.
  • Disclosure. "artist cut out onto a plain ground".
Matte pending. The current segmenter leaves a band of about 30 px of the photo's own background around hair, so no B card has passed the spill and no-halo tests yet. A matte evaluation is running. O21 stands as the rule the B build is judged against; its frame comes in a later issue, after a B card passes.
