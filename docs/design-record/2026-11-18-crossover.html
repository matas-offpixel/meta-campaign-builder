Crossover: the shows a show shares fans with

CIRQLIN is adding a partner read that returns counts only. For each show, it gives the show's neighbours: the client's other shows whose signup lists overlap with this one, each with its shared count and both shares. It also gives the show's share of signups that are new to the client, and how many consented fans of its neighbours aren't on its list yet, per channel. This record rules where those counts show up in the app and what they say: the on-sale refresh, the Retargeting campaign, the dashboard's show view and D2C. It also names a learning layer, which has no UI.

A real read, for Ewan McVicar at NX Newcastle: 18.4 % of Locklead's list also signed up for Ewan, as did 14.7 % of Robbie Doherty's, 6.6 % of D.O.D's and 2.4 % of Folamour's. 67 % of Ewan's list is new to Electric.

  • Builds on: R2 (the on-sale refresh's audience lines), RT2, RT12 and RT14 (Retargeting's lists, buyers gate and windows), PA1–PA7, SO1 and JR1–JR5 (the Signups & communities panel), WB1 and PB1 (the WhatsApp rate words), GK1–GK8 (the genre key), and the D2C rules: drafts only, the 3-of-3 gate and per-send approval. All of them stand.
  • Frames: XO-A to XO-D, each at 1280 and 393, on a fixture show, XMPL-EWAN.
  • Invented: every threshold, every new word, and the fixture's list sizes, counts and costs are invented. The four shares and the 67 % are the real read.

Rulings

#Ruling
XO1 · What crossover is
  • Within one client only. A show's neighbours are that client's other shows with a CIRQLIN event page, past or upcoming. Umbrella events (PT3) and crowd pages aren't shows here. Overlap between two clients is never computed, read or shown. Consent is given to the client named on the form, so the client's own lists are the only ones that can meet.
  • Counts only. The app reads counts and shares and stores nothing about a person. Every audience that needs people is built inside CIRQLIN or inside the client's own provider (XO6), never in the app.
  • Floors.
    • Overlap is read only between two lists that each have 100 counted signups or more (PA7's count, spam removed).
    • A shared count under 10 is never shown or used.
    • A neighbour is a show where at least 5 % of its own list also signed up for this one (share_of_that), with at least 50 shared.
  • The words for a share always name whose list it's a share of: "18.4 % of Locklead's list also signed up for Ewan McVicar". A bare "18.4 % overlap" is never shown, because the two directions differ (574 shared is 18.4 % of Locklead's 3,120 but 26.8 % of Ewan's 2,140).
  • Freshness. Read once a day at the refresh's 06:00 UTC run (C7). Every surface uses that read, and the dashboard tile names its day.
  • Closest first. Neighbours are always listed by share_of_that, highest first.

XO3 · On-sale refresh: a lookalike seed
  • The line. R2's audience lines gain a LOOKALIKE line for each seed neighbour, up to 2 a proposal, closest first. It isn't proposed when the show already has a lookalike from that neighbour.
    • Title: "Lookalike from {neighbour}'s signups"
    • Why: "{share} % of {neighbour}'s list also signed up for {show} ({shared} of {list})."
    • Writes: "Uploads {neighbour}'s signup list to Meta · a 1 % lookalike in {country} · into {ad set}". {ad set} is the main campaign's prospecting ad set, chosen as R2 places audiences. The venue's country is the lookalike's country.
    Accept writes through the existing audience-write gate. The list's people go from CIRQLIN to Meta as hashed email and phone only, and never through the app's database.
  • The gate (as RT12). Signup lists go to Meta only for a client whose "Signup lists in Meta audiences" setting is on. It's a per-client switch on the client page, off for every client. Matas turns it on only once the client confirms their privacy notice covers using signups for advertising, recorded in RT12's four fields. One confirmation may cover both switches when the notice names both.
  • Gate off: the alternative. The line seeds from the neighbour's page instead, with no list uploaded:
    • Title: "Lookalike from people who signed up on {neighbour}'s page"
    • Why: as above, plus "{client}'s signup lists can't go to Meta until their privacy notice is confirmed for advertising."
    • Writes: "Registrations on {neighbour}'s page · 180 days · a 1 % lookalike in {country} · into {ad set}". These are the pixel's CompleteRegistration on that page's landing_url (RT13's URL rule).
    If that page has under 1,000 registrations in 180 days, it isn't proposed, and the alternative tries the neighbour's own Facebook or Instagram page engagers (180 days) when the client has a role on it. With neither, no line.
XO4 · Retargeting: Similar lists by crossover
  • Mid Bottom's Similar lists (RT2) are proposed from crossover: every neighbour's signup list, closest first, up to 4. Hand-uploaded lists with the Similar role keep their place after them.
  • The row gains one line: "Similar lists, closest first: {neighbour} {share} % · …". Under it, once: "Each % is how much of that show's list also signed up for this one." Mid Bottom already leaves out Bottom, which holds this show's signups (RT2), so these lists reach the neighbours' fans who haven't signed up for this show.
  • On Create (RB), they're part of Mid Bottom. On a running campaign, a neighbour that newly qualifies is offered once a week on the 08:00 read: "Add {neighbour}'s signups to Mid Bottom · {share} % of their list also signed up for this show". It's an audience write, so it has Accept and Reject, and it isn't a Move or Cut (RT5's one-a-day limit doesn't count it).
  • Gate off: the same order, as registrations on each neighbour's page (180 days): "Similar, closest first: registrations on {neighbour}'s page {share} % · …". A brick RT8 line under the tier says: "{client}'s signup lists can't go to Meta until their privacy notice is confirmed for advertising, so Mid Bottom uses registrations on these shows' pages."
XO5 · Dashboard: new to the client
  • The tile. The show view's Signups & communities panel (SO1) gains "New to {client}", with the share to the whole percent and the subline "{new} of {signups} signups · since launch". Under the panel's tiles, one explainer: "New to {client}: signed up for this show without having signed up for another {client} show's page before."
  • Where. Internal and the share link. It's the client's own data about their own fans. At 393 it takes a full-width row under the funnel's tiles, which stay two by two (JR5).
  • States.
    • Under 100 signups: "—" and "Too few signups to say".
    • The client's first show with a CIRQLIN page: "First {client} show on CIRQLIN", and no figure.
    • Since launch only, as JR2, so it's left out on other periods.
    • Venue and client views add nothing in this record.
  • {client} is the client's name as the dashboard header shows it ("Electric").
XO6 · D2C: a cross-promo before the presale
  • The card. The D2C tab shows one CROSS-PROMO card from the announce until its send time.
    • It's offered when the matching shows' fans who said yes to {client}'s marketing and aren't on this show's list number 100 or more. Anyone reachable on at least one channel counts once.
    • Title: "{n} fans of {A} and {B} said yes to {client}'s marketing and aren't on {show}'s list". With three shows: "{A}, {B} and {C}".
    • One row per channel, each with a tick, its count and its cost:
      • "Email · {n} · no send cost"
      • "WhatsApp · {n} · {n} × £{rate} = £{cost}" (with no rate: "Rate not set", PB1)
      • "SMS · {n} · {n} × £{rate} = £{cost}"
      Email is ticked by default; the paid channels start unticked.
    • The send: "Sign-up nudge · {Ddd d Mon}, 17:00 · SIGN UP HERE → {show}'s page". The default time is 17:00 London two days before the presale, so new signups get in for it. The time can be edited.
    • Action "Create send". Under it: "Creates drafts only. Nothing goes out until it's approved."
  • Create send asks CIRQLIN to build the audience inside the client's own provider: the matching shows' consented fans, less this show's list. Then it makes one draft per ticked channel through the existing pipeline. That's a Mailchimp campaign saved as status=save, and a Bird broadcast draft with no audience attached. A WhatsApp template is submitted the day before, as always. The 3-of-3 gate and per-send approval apply. Nothing is ever sent automatically.
  • The copy is the announce-to-non-signups kind: it never assumes the fan signed up for this show, and its single CTA is SIGN UP HERE. Language follows the venue (Spain → Spanish), body and button alike. The scarcity rule stands: no claims the counts don't support.
  • Rest. A matching show whose fans had a cross-promo from {client} in the last 14 days is left out of the count and the audience, so a fan gets at most one every 14 days. This is tracked by show, not by person.
  • Sender limit. When the WhatsApp count is over the sender's 24-hour limit, the row adds " · over {sender}'s {limit} a day, so it goes in two parts".
XO7 · The learning loop
  • Taste neighbours are a pooling layer between a client's own results and its genre (GK5). When a show is short of its own evidence, the refresh's learned defaults read its taste neighbours' results first, then the genre's, then everyone's. Taste neighbours are the shows that pass XO2's ≥ 10 %. There's no new UI. Where a line says where its evidence came from, it reads "From {n} shows that share fans with this one".
  • The genre flag. When two shows pass ≥ 15 % but carry different genre keys (GK1), the learning-refresh report gets an operator line, internal only, with nothing auto-changed: "{A} and {B} share {share} % of {A}'s fans but are filed as {genre A} and {genre B}. Check the genre."
XO8 · What it never does
  • It never compares clients, shows a person, or names a fan.
  • It never uploads a list without the client's switch, and never sends anything on its own.
  • It never says "overlap" without naming whose list the share is of.

Acceptance

  • Same client only: a test feeds the read a show of another client and asserts it never appears anywhere.
  • Floors: lists under 100, shared counts under 10, and neighbours under 5 % or 50 shared are absent everywhere.
  • Words: every share names whose list it's a share of, and the strings are exact.
  • Gate: with the switch off, no line uploads a list. The page alternative or nothing is offered, and the brick line shows on Mid Bottom.
  • No people in the app: no table gains a person-level column. Create send hands CIRQLIN the show ids, the channels and the time, and receives draft ids.
  • Drafts only: Create send makes status=save campaigns and audience-less broadcasts, and a test asserts nothing is scheduled or activated.
  • The 14-day rest leaves a recently cross-promoted show out of both the count and the audience.
  • No banned words.
  • Frames: XO-A to XO-D at 1280 and 393 with the figures above.
