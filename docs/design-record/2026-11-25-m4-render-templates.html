Off/Pixel · design record · M4 · render templates · 25 November 2026 · before M4 (built after M3)

Four recipes, rendered as templates, every file owned and checked

In M4, the Creative Lab renders the four recipes that passed QA as templates on Lambda:

  1. the 15 s montage, which comes first because it’s the most reused;
  2. roll-call;
  3. top tracks;
  4. artist card.

Every render is recorded with its owner and rights, named so the learnings can read it, and can be started in the Lab or asked for by Claude (render_package) and approved on Today.

The spec is claude/creative-video-system-v2.md:

  • T1’s slot table;
  • O12’s roll-call rung;
  • artwork in a 9:16 (never shrunk with a blur);
  • CTA pairing;
  • the 8 checks and each recipe’s checklist, which become M4’s acceptance tests;
  • the 25 Nov file name, {EVENT}__{owner}__{recipe}__{variant}__{cta-slug}__r{round}__{ratio}.{ext}.

Rights follow the artist library record (AL1). Words appear only where Matas sees them.

Rulings

#Ruling
RT1 · The template picker
  • “Render a package” on the event’s Creative Lab, with “{code} · {stage}” under it. The stage (Signup, Presale or On sale) is the one every claim is written for: check 3.
  • Under that: “Only material this show can use is offered: shareable files, and {client}’s own.” This is the artist library’s rule, so another client’s footage is never offered.
  • One card per recipe, in this order: Montage (15 s), Roll-call, Top tracks, Artist card. Each card shows:
    • the recipe and one line on what it makes;
    • Owner;
    • Needs;
    • Render.
    A recipe not built yet shows the same card, muted, with “Coming after the montage” and no Render button.
  • Owner is who filmed or made the footage, as the file name will carry it:
    • the artist (their own posts or press kit);
    • the client (any promoter footage, even one clip);
    • “Off/Pixel, from {artist}’s material” (made by us from artist material only).
    Before any clip is picked, it reads “Set by the clips you pick”.
  • Needs, per recipe:
    • montage: 16 clips, track and drop, lineup, overlay kit, claim;
    • roll-call: 1 raw 9:16 clip, overlay kit, claim;
    • top tracks: press shot, 3 tracks and their drops, 9:16 story artwork;
    • artist card: press shot, 2–3 track snippets, name, date and venue.
  • Each need shows one state:
    • ready (olive);
    • “{n} of {m} picked” or “not picked” (ochre), with Pick;
    • missing (brick), when there’s nothing to pick from.
  • Claim is read from the tiers, never typed. It shows when the tiers were read, so a stale piece can’t slip through (O12’s note on the 25 Nov overlay). A roll-call shows both of its lines.
  • Render reads “Render {n} files”, with “{versions} × {ratios}” under it, for example “No text, teaser, announcement × 9:16, 4:5, 1:1”. While any need isn’t ready, the button is off, with “Pick what’s missing first.”
  • Starting a render here needs no approval: Matas is the one starting it. Rendering spends no ad money, so the client ceilings don’t apply.
RT2 · A render’s row
  • The full file name, in mono, never shortened. It may break only after a “__”, never inside an event code.
  • Under it: “{recipe} · {variant} · {cta} · {ratio} · round {round}”, read from the name.
    • no-text, teaser and announcement print as words;
    • no-cta prints “No CTA”;
    • any other slug prints as written;
    • a ratio prints as 9:16.
  • The rights badge, per file, with AL1’s words.
    • Owner and rights aren’t the same thing.
    • A file carrying the client’s kit, artwork, venue or date is “This client only · {client}”, with its AL1 reason, even when the footage is the artist’s. Every roll-call (the client’s kit) and every version with text is one of these.
    • A No text version made only from artist material is Shareable, and only that kind joins the library’s Renders.
  • Checks, in one line:
    • “Passed the 8 checks and the {recipe} list” (olive);
    • or “Failed: {checks}” (brick), naming the checks and recipe items that failed, by name only. A failed file offers “Render as round {round}”.
  • Checks with no machine test (Voice and Style; the builder lists the rest and sends them to design) read “To look at before upload: {checks}” (ochre), with “Looked at it”. Once ticked: “Looked at by {name}, {date, time}”.
  • A render nobody has looked at can’t be uploaded. The tree’s Add creatives to… and M3’s add_creatives_to_stage refuse it: “Not looked at yet, so it can’t be uploaded.”
  • “Drive ↗” opens the file in the event’s folder, where renders live (AL4).
  • While rendering: “Rendering”. If the render didn’t finish: “Didn’t render, so there’s no file.”, with Try again.
RT3 · What Claude is told
  • On request, before anything renders:
    • the package line;
    • one line per recipe with its needs, as the card shows them;
    • then TD2’s “Waiting for {name} to approve this on Today.”
  • When something is short or missing: it’s refused before Today: “Can’t render {recipe} yet for {code}: {needs} missing. Nothing was sent to Today.”
  • When the package finishes:
    • the counts;
    • one line per file (name, result, badge, Drive link);
    • the look line: “{name} looks at {checks} in the Lab before anything is uploaded. Nothing goes to Meta until then.”
  • Claude only ever gets names, counts and links. It gets no other error text.
RT4 · Today
  • A package is one TD1 row:
    • “Render package · {code}”;
    • “{recipes}: {files}”;
    • Approve and Reject, as TD2.
  • Approve starts the render. It doesn’t approve upload, because nobody can look at a file before it exists.
  • Done:
    • “Done · {files} rendered · {p} passed every check”;
    • then “{f} failed: {checks}” in brick, when any failed;
    • then “To look at in the Lab before upload: {checks}” in ochre, with “Open in the Lab”.
  • Refused before Today: “Refused · {recipe} is missing {needs}”.
  • Expiry is TD’s 24 hours.

Acceptance

  • Each recipe’s checklist and the 8 checks are tests on a fixture render. The row shows their result word for word.
  • File names match the 25 Nov convention exactly. The owner field follows the footage: any promoter clip makes it promoter-….
  • Badges: a version with the client’s kit, artwork or text is “This client only · {client}”; a No text version from artist material only is Shareable. Only Shareable renders become library shortcuts.
  • Upload: a render not looked at can’t be added by the tree or by M3 (a test checks both).
  • Claude: render_package writes nothing until Approve, and a refusal sends nothing to Today.
  • Frames: RT-A to RT-D at 1280 and 393, from this record’s fixtures.

Invented here

  • Every word.
  • Rules:
    • owner and rights badge as two separate things;
    • “looked at” as the gate before upload;
    • Approve on Today starting the render, not approving upload.
  • Frame contents:
    • the P26-BDAY figures, rounds, times and the cta-slug “tickets”;
    • the claim, built from the spec’s 25 Nov tiers (early bird, 1st and 2nd release sold out, 3rd live);
    • the ES27-ENZOSIRAGUSA picker’s state, read from #1377’s dry run (5 shareable Enzo clips).
