Rulings

#Ruling
PB1 · #1246's own words

The words #1246 added are accepted as built:

  • TikTok's tables are titled "TikTok delivery by age · TikTok says" and "TikTok delivery by UK nation · TikTok says", matching PI2's Meta title.
  • The direct-to-fan totals line is "{client} · {n} sends · {first day} – {last day}", ending "· includes {n} whole-list sends" as PI6 rules.
  • On a phone, EV4's list title "Click rate · clicks ÷ delivered", then the cost line "WhatsApp {cost} · email —". "Rate not set" is the link there too.
  • The sends table is titled "Sends", with columns Sent, Send, Show, Channel, Delivered, Read / opened, Clicked and Click rate, newest first, eight rows then "Show all {n} sends" / "Show the latest".
  • The age table's All row sums the unrounded rows and then rounds, as PI1 rules. So the whole-pound rows can add to £1 less or more than All (Locklead: rows £1,569, All £1,570). That's right; don't round rows to force it.

Three answers on #1246:

  • Age withheld, region shown: right. PI1 withholds only the age table. The region table has no results to disagree with.
  • "Whole list" on NX Loves master-tag sends: right. A send to the venue's whole list is a whole-list send (PI6), whatever the tag is called.
  • "Rate not set" links to the client's D2C tab until the rate field exists. When it lands, the link goes to the field itself.
PB2 · The Send column
  • The Send column is the send's name as the provider holds it: Mailchimp's campaign title, Bird's broadcast name. It's shown verbatim, cut with an ellipsis at the column's width, the full name on hover.
  • A send with no name reads "—" in that row.
  • A Sends table where no row has a name doesn't draw the column. A column of dashes reads as "none", the same reason PI2 dropped the result columns from the region table.
  • The frames' fixture carries the six sends' real names. A campaign title is not person data.
PB3 · Partner opt-in until CIRQLIN says the box is there

Amends PA5's last point. #631's partner_opt_in is 0 both on a page with no partner tick box and on a page where nobody ticked it, so the panel can't tell them apart yet.

  • Until CIRQLIN returns whether the page's form has a partner box, the row shows only when partner_opt_in is more than 0. A 0 is left out.
  • Once that signal exists, PA5 applies as written: the row is left out only where there's no box, and a real 0 shows.
  • The ingest keeps partner_opt_in as a number. It doesn't guess the box.
PB4 · The entered member count

Amends PA3's first source. Nothing stores a hand-entered member count yet. It belongs to HE4 (the presale log), which E2 and the phase 2 hand-back already give it to. HE2 adds no column for it.

Until HE4 lands, the community headline starts at PA3's second source, community_joined, and E3's "Community at presale" does the same.

PB5 · The self-check compares this run only

Amends HA1. A page's signups is checked against a /signups page-id total written in the same cron run, after the run's signups pass started.

  • An older total is "unchecked", never compared. That covers a signups read that failed tonight, a page skipped on the bucket or the clock, and a tag-only sync.
  • "mismatch" then means the two reads disagree minutes apart, which is worth a look. Against yesterday's total it would only mean people signed up today.
