A drop is timed where it lands

This re-rules a candidate's time in VW7 of 2026-11-17-creative-lab-video-window-2.html (#1322). With the bass-only detector, the right sections are found on P26-BDAY's real audio. But VW7 times a candidate at its rise point, the 50 ms step where the 2 s after are loudest against the 2 s before. On a ramp, that point is partway up. Clip 27's real drop is at 0:29.8 and its rise point is 0:30.1. Clip 30's approved cut lands at 0:11.48 at the end of a 2.3 s ramp, and its rise point is 0:09.9. The edit has to be built on where the drop lands.

  • Replaced: VW7's first sub-bullet, "A candidate's time is its rise point".
  • Stands: how candidates are found and merged, what "major" means, and "the main drop is the last major candidate that fits" (VW7). VW8, VW9 and the rest of VW1–VW6 also stand.
  • Accepted from the build: every level the drop rules read is the bass band, as VW12 sets out.
  • Frames: none change. VW-A and VW-B stand, because their fixtures already give the times where those drops land. VW-D is a schematic, here only.
  • Invented: the 1 dB margin, the 0.25 s average and the two 2 s spans are invented. Clips 27 and 30 settle them (acceptance).

Rulings

#Ruling
VW10 · Where a drop lands
  • A candidate's time is where it lands, not its rise point. The rise point still finds the candidate and still measures it. The landing is the time everything else uses.
  • The loud section. L is the mean bass level of the loudest 2 s that starts within 2 s either side of the rise point.
  • The landing is the first 50 ms step, counting from 2 s before the rise point, where the mean bass level of the 0.25 s from that step reaches L − 1 dB or more.
  • A landing is always found, no later than the end of L's 2 s. That 2 s splits into eight 0.25 s blocks whose means average to L, so at least one block reaches L.
  • Why a 0.25 s mean, not one step. A lone hit in a riser, such as a snare at full level, can reach L for a single 50 ms step. A quarter second is about half a beat at 120–128 BPM, so the loud section has to have arrived, not just ticked.
  • Why the search starts 2 s early. A drop that hits hard can land before its rise point, because the rise compares 2 s either side and peaks a little after the hit. Clip 27 lands 0.3 s before its rise point.
  • Stored with each candidate: the landing as its time, plus its rise point and L, as data.
VW11 · What reads the landing
  • Measured from the landing: VW2's fit (3 s or more before, 10 s or more after) and VW2's window (16 s from 4.0 s before the drop, with its shortening and earlier start).
  • Shown as the landing, to 0.1 s: the picker's times, "Use the drop at {m:ss.s}", and the candidate a refusal names.
  • As #1322 rules them: finding (a 6 dB rise over 2 s), merging within 4 s, and "major". A candidate's rise and its after-level against the clip's loudest 2 s are both measured from its rise point.
  • Two candidates whose landings are under 4 s apart are one. The one with the larger rise is kept.
  • The main drop is still the last major candidate that fits, and "last" is by landing.
VW12 · The bass band (accepted from the build)
  • Every level VW1, VW7 and VW10 read is the bass band, 150 Hz and below, per 50 ms step in dB. It is the band #1295's track drop finder uses, through one shared function.
  • VW3's "no sound" stays on the whole mix (under −50 LUFS). A clip with a voice and no bass has sound, but no drop.
  • The 6 dB, 3 dB and 4 s figures stand on the bass band. Clips 27 and 30 confirm them (acceptance).
VW13 · Existing work

  • P26-BDAY: clip 27's drafts move to the landing at 0:29.8. Clip 30's approved r3 is untouched, and its recomputed detected drop must land where r3 has it (acceptance).

A schematic shaped like clip 30, not its real levels. The bass ramps up for 2.3 s, so the steepest part, the rise point, is partway up. The drop lands where the level reaches the loud section.

Acceptance

  • Real audio. Run the detector on P26-BDAY's clip 27 and clip 30:
    • clip 27 must land at 0:29.8, within 0.15 s;
    • clip 30 must land at 0:11.5, within 0.15 s;
    • each must still be its clip's main drop under VW7.
    Both become fixtures, holding their real bass levels around the drop, not just the times.
  • If either misses, the build stops and reports for each clip:
    • the rise point and L;
    • the per-step bass level and the 0.25 s mean, from 2 s before the rise point to the end of L's 2 s;
    • where it lands for each margin (0.5, 1, 1.5 and 2 dB) with each mean (0.05, 0.25 and 0.5 s).
    Design re-rules the two figures from that table before merge. The build doesn't tune them by trial.
  • Synthetic tests:
    • a step from quiet to loud lands on the step, within one 50 ms step;
    • a 2.3 s ramp that ends in a step lands on the step, not on its rise point;
    • a lone full-level hit 1 s before the drop doesn't land the drop early;
    • a hard drop whose rise point falls after it lands before its rise point;
    • a landing is always found, by the end of L's 2 s;
    • two candidates landing under 4 s apart merge, keeping the larger rise;
    • VW2's fit and window measure from the landing.
  • Frames: none change. VW-A and VW-B baselines stand.
