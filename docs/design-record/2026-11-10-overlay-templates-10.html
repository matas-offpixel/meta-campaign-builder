Rulings

#Ruling
O68 The render copyThe answer is a stored copy, not a bigger Lambda disk. The output is never wider than 1080, so a 4K source only costs download and decode time.
  • A render copy is made at import for a clip whose crop windows are all wider than 1080 px. Its scale is s = 1080 / the narrowest of its three crop windows (9:16, 4:5, 1:1), and never above 1.
  • For example: 2160 × 3840 → 0.5, giving 1080 × 1920. 3840 × 2160 landscape → 1080 / 1215 = 0.889, giving 3413 × 1920. So every window stays at least 1080 wide.
  • Encoding: Lanczos, H.264 CRF ≤ 16, the source's frame rate and audio.
  • It is stored privately beside the clip, Lambda renders from it, and the original stays untouched as the master. A clip at or under the output size gets no copy.
O69 The scaling filterThis amends O63's "with Lanczos". Upscales of 1.6× or less use the renderer's own filter, with no stored upscaled copy. By eye, the 1.5× Enzo drafts read as normal phone footage, and the type is set at the output's size whatever the footage does. Lanczos stays for the render copy's downscale (O68).
O70 The feather curveThis amends O66 and replaces H66's 6-level limit.
  • The backing's feather is a smoothstep (3t² − 2t³) from the core's opacity to 0. The bands keep J2's fade.
  • A feather is never shorter than 12 px at the output. Where the gap can't hold the core's padding plus 12 px, the padding shrinks first, down to 0.
  • The check, on the backing layer rendered alone over flat mid-grey, from one row past the type's ink: the largest row-to-row step is at most 1.6 times the feather's mean step. A smoothstep peaks at 1.5×, and a hard cut fails at any opacity. The type's own edge and shadow are never part of the check.
O71 A no-claim refusal's wordsOn a clip with no claim, the share is over the clip's length, and the line reads "Face in the type for {n} % of the clip — choose another crop or clip". The run line (O65) is the same with or without a claim.
O72 Another name in the footageText in the footage is found on the same 5-a-second samples, by text detection.
  • A word whose letters are at least 0.02 H tall, legible in at least 10 % of the samples, is flagged on the approval card: "Another name in the footage: {text} — check before approving".
  • It is listed in the report with its share and its box.
  • The client's own name and handle, and the event's venue as written on the event, aren't flagged.
  • A flag never refuses a clip on its own (text detection misses and misreads). Whether some names should refuse is open for Matas, below.
O73 The search and the reportCrop candidates that give the same window (a source exactly the format's shape) are tried once. A refused row's arrangement column lists every arrangement tried ("A, B"), not "(A)".
O74 O63's import wiringA follow-up PR is accepted: keep the largest copy, list folder clips first, plus O68's render copy. It has to land before T1 clips can be rendered from the Lab, and it carries H63's import half and H69.
Open, for Matas
  • Competitor branding in the footage. Luke 2's clip is from a Solid Grooves night, and its neon sign sits under Puzzle's type in every format. O72 flags it. Should the Lab also refuse footage showing another promoter's brand, or is it the artist's footage and fine? My recommendation is to refuse when the name sits inside the face window or a type box, and flag otherwise.
  • The no-claim T2. With no claim, the panel's middle is empty paper. Either the no-claim T2 isn't offered, or the lineup moves to the panel's centre at a larger size. That would be a later issue.
