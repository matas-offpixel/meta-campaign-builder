A quote looks like a caption

Matas, 12 Nov, on the Luke quote finals: "we've strayed from the UGC caption approved font (TikTok Sans in white with black outline)."

His standing rule of 6 Nov is that every UGC overlay uses the refined TikTok Sans style with the black outline. Two records drifted from it:

  • quote mode's Q2 (2026-11-08-creative-lab-quote-mode.html: no stroke, weight 700 or 400, cap 0.0135 H, centred on 0.28 H);
  • C5 of 2026-11-12-creative-lab-caption-placement.html, which built a quote-only search on top of Q2.

This record re-rules both. A quote is whose words they are, not a look. Ops has already re-rendered P26-BDAY's 7 quote finals this way, at Matas's direction. The Lab's build of C1–C7 takes this record with it.

Rulings

#Ruling
Q10 One lookA quote renders in the locked caption style (rule 13, v1.json): TikTok Sans at wdth 120 / wght 400, white fill, the black outside stroke at 0.054054 em, line height 1.054, centred on 0.493 W inside O99's box. Its sizes are the caption's:
  • the standard size, 18 characters a line, 4 lines;
  • the long size where the text needs it (0.75, 24 a line, 5 lines, 73–96 characters), chosen by the validator's existing rule.
Q2's look is retired: no-stroke type, the 700 / 400 weight choice, cap 0.0135 H, pitch 0.030 H and the 0.28 H centre are no longer drawn. CLQ3's side-by-side is superseded.
Q11 Placement and sizeCaption placement C1–C3 applies to quotes as it does to captions:
  • the search starts at the caption's own spot (0.355469 H, y 682.5);
  • C2's size steps apply, from 1.0 or from the long size, down to the 0.65 floor;
  • C3 refuses only after every place × size fails. The quote keeps C3's own sentence, No place on this clip keeps the quote off the face — choose another clip or in-point.
Withdrawn from C5: the quote's 0.28 H start, "no size steps", and contrast as part of the pass. A quote's contrast is now measured and stored and never refused, as C4 rules for captions; the outline is what makes it read. C5's line on T1 and T2 still stands: a quote never sits on a T1 or T2 render, just as a caption doesn't.
Q12 LinesA caption's lines are the writer's phrases. A quote has no writer, so its lines are made once:
  • it is wrapped at word boundaries to the measure of its starting size, 18 or 24 characters a line;
  • a word is never split;
  • C2's steps then keep those lines, as they do for a caption.
The words stay verbatim (Q1). The only edit is still dropping whole sentences from the start or end.
Q13 Too longA quote over the long size's limit (more than 96 characters, or more than 5 lines of 24) isn't drawn smaller to fit. The Lab says This quote is too long — drop a sentence from the start or end. and offers Q1's sentence drop.
  • Luke's quote is 98 characters: Back in Southampton for this one… Closing out the year with Puzzle on 27th December. See you there.
  • Dropping See you there makes it 84 characters, 4 lines of 24 (CLQ5b).
  • Check the re-rendered Luke finals against this (HQ9). If one carries all 98 characters, it's outside the long size's limit.
Q14 What "quote" still meansQuote mode stays a mode for whose words they are:
  • the artist's approved words, with kind = quote and its source_kind / source_ref (Q1);
  • artist-voice and gated by the artist's clearance (Q4);
  • paired only with its artist's clips on its cleared channels (W6);
  • no quote marks and no name on the clip (Q2's content rules stay);
  • emoji inline as the caption shows them.
The writer's mode switch (CLQ1) and the quote feed-post's chips (CLQ2) are unchanged. quote_weight is no longer read by the renderer.
Q15 Renders already made
  • P26-BDAY's 7 quote finals are already re-rendered in the caption style (ops, 12 Nov).
  • Any other quote render still in Q2's look isn't offered to launch or Drive. It shows Old quote style — render again and re-renders under Q10–Q13 on request.
  • Nothing is deleted.

On a phone, the outline is the difference. Q2's white with no stroke disappears over a bright crowd, as the Enzo 1 and 4 quote finals of 12 Nov showed at 4 s. The caption style reads on any ground, which is why C4 never refuses a caption on contrast.
