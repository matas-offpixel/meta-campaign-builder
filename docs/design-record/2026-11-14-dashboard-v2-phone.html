Dashboard v2 on a phone: no sideways page, key figures on one screen

Matas opened #1230's preview share link on his iPhone: "not great on mobile — can't view all key info at once, a lot of empty space with large boxes feels unnecessary and could be compacted, the graph could have a slider to scroll left / right so the rest can fit in one screen." His screenshot also shows the page wider than the phone. The header sentence, channel line, venue row and the Spend tile's right edge are cut off.

This record rules a compact layout for phones and the overflow fix. It builds on 2026-11-13-dashboard-v2.html (D1–D13) and 2026-11-13-dashboard-v2-against-the-model.html (N1–N8). Both stand. Nothing here changes a figure, a word of the header sentence, or the layout at desktop widths. Records aren't edited after merge, so this one carries the change.

All figures are live, read from the #1230 preview share link at head 160e017 on 14 Nov: NX Newcastle, All shows, Since launch. One thing isn't live, and the frames say so: the chart's line shape, which comes from #1226's 12 Nov series and is schematic. The campaign count (29, 7 spending) is from the same preview's campaigns table.

Why the page is wider than the phone

Reproduced on the preview at 375 px. The page lays out 894 px wide, and everything is drawn at that width and cut off at the screen edge.

  • The cause: <body> is flex flex-col, and the page's <section class="mx-auto max-w-6xl …"> is a flex item. Its auto side margins stop it stretching to the body's width, so it sizes to its content. Its widest content is the show-chip row. That row scrolls (overflow-x-auto), but its chips are shrink-0 and the row has no width of its own, so its minimum width is all its chips laid side by side.
  • Checked: hiding only the show-chip row brings the section back to 375. Adding width:100%; min-width:0 to the section, and nothing else, brings the page's scroll width from 894 to 375, with every row in place.
  • Why CI missed it: the 393 fixture's show names are short, so the chip row fitted. The live NX row is 9 shows with names like "Interplanetary Criminal".
  • At 1280 the cap of max-w-6xl (1152 px) is narrower than the window, so the bug never shows on a desktop.

Rulings

#Ruling
MB1 · Nothing wider than the phone

No page-level sideways scroll at any width from 320 px up. Every row wraps or scrolls inside itself.

  • The fix: the page section gets w-full min-w-0. Every scrolling row gets min-w-0 and max-w-full, so it can never widen its parent. Do the same on any flex or grid child that holds text or a scroller. Grid tracks are minmax(0,1fr).
  • Text wraps: the header sentence wraps normally. The channel line also breaks inside long items (overflow-wrap:anywhere).
  • The chart never sets a pixel width wider than its frame (MB6).
  • -webkit-text-size-adjust:100% on the page, so iOS doesn't enlarge text when the phone turns sideways.
  • Guard in CI: a test at 320, 375, 390 and 430, on the internal page and the share link. It fails if the document's scroll width is more than the viewport width. It also fails if any element outside a scrolling row has a right edge past the viewport.
    • The fixture must be live-like: 4 options in the venue row, 9 shows with the longest names, a five-figure spend, and the longest header sentence (with the "each show's last count" clause).
MB2 · The first screen

On a phone, the first screen answers "how is it going?" without a scroll. In order, top to bottom:

  1. Title row: the scope name, as now ("NX Newcastle"), with the period on the right as one compact select chip, "Since launch ▾" (MB5).
  2. Stamps: one muted mono line, "Counts 12 Nov · Spend 13 Nov · Signups 13 Nov".
  3. The header sentence (N4), word for word, at 13 px. It wraps to about five lines at 375.
  4. The channel line (N4), at most two lines.
  5. The venue row: one scrolling row.
  6. The show chips: one scrolling row.
  7. The six key figures (MB3), then "More figures (8)" (MB4).

Budget: the first screen is 375 × 548 at the small end (iPhone SE in Safari with its bars showing) and 430 × 739 at the large end (Pro Max). At 375, everything to "More figures" fits, and the chart starts below it. At 430, the chart fits too. MBV1 and MBV2 show the fold.

The page loses its outer card on a phone: no border or rounded panel around the dashboard. Sections are split by hairlines and the side gutter is 12 px. That gives the content 351 px at 375.

MB5 · Filters on a phone
  • The venue row and show chips: one row each that scrolls sideways, never wrapping. The row's right edge fades over 28 px, so it's clear there's more. On load, the picked venue and the picked show are scrolled into view. There's no "VENUE" label on a phone; the segmented control speaks for itself.
  • All venues keeps N2's grouping inside the one scrolling row: venue labels between the groups.
  • The period becomes one select chip in the title row ("Since launch ▾"). A tap opens a sheet listing the six periods. That saves the period row, which wraps to two lines at 375.
MB6 · The chart scrolls inside its frame

On a phone, the chart shows 30 days at a time and scrolls sideways inside its own frame. The page doesn't scroll sideways.

  • Size: a fixed plot height of 176 px, plus a 22 px date row. The width is the content width minus the two axes.
  • Pinned axes: the left and right axis labels sit in fixed columns (40 px and 36 px) outside the scroller. Only the plot and its dates move. The axis titles "SPEND (£)" and "TOTAL SOLD" sit above the axes, as N1 rules.
  • One scale for the whole period: the axes are computed from the whole period, so nothing rescales as you swipe. N1's three intervals stand.
  • Opens on the latest day: the scroller starts scrolled to its right end. Dates follow N1's rule against the 30-day density: at 375 that's every 7 days, ending on the last day. A date label that the frame's edge would cut in half isn't drawn.
  • Where you are:
    • the range in view sits between the axis titles, "14 Oct – 12 Nov", and updates as you swipe;
    • a 3 px bar under the plot shows where the window sits in the period;
    • a left-edge fade appears once there are earlier dates.
  • Gestures: a swipe scrolls. A press and hold (about 300 ms) puts the dotted rule on that day and shows the readout in a strip over the titles row, above the plot: "30 Sep · Spend £164 · Total sold 4,173 · Internal Count". A finger never covers the readout. Releasing puts the titles and range back. A day between two counts says it's an estimate (D5).
  • Short periods: when the period is 30 days or less, there's no scroller. The chart fits the width, as now.
  • Captions fold to one paragraph under the chart: "Swipe for earlier dates · press and hold for a day." followed by N1's dot sentence. The "Index to own peak" toggle ends the same paragraph.
  • Desktop doesn't change. At 1280 the whole period stays in view, with hover, as N1.
MB7 · Below the chart
  • Sales trajectory: the bars only, one row per show: name and figures on one line, then the bar. The section title carries the capacity ("Sales trajectory · capacity 2,000 each"), and a one-line key replaces the legend row.
  • Campaigns: collapsed to one row, "Campaigns · 29 · 7 spending ›". A tap opens the table, which scrolls sideways inside its own frame.
  • By show: collapsed the same way, "By show · 8 ›".
  • Both collapsed rows remember their state per device, as MB4 does.
