A drop is the kick that ends the build

Matas's rule: a drop is timed at its first kick, the first strong bass onset at the front of the drop, which is where his approved cuts sit. When a clip has two drops, the later one is used if there's enough clip after it (10 s); otherwise the first. VW10's averaged landing missed both acceptance clips (27 → 30.1 against 29.8, and 30 → 12.6 against 11.5), and no margin or window passed both.

The drop-features table (#1329, comment 6975499966) measured clips 27, 29, 30 and 33 against Matas's times. No single feature picks all four. Two do between them: the kick roll stops (27, 29, 33) and the 1–4 kHz band falls (27, 29, 30). This record times a drop at the kick where either happens. Clip 33's drop is 0:30.0, confirmed by ear. The "beat returns" measures put it about 1 s late (0:31.0), so they're not used for timing.

Rulings

#Ruling
VW14 · What a drop is
  • A drop is a kick. A kick is an onset in the kick band (30–150 Hz flux), at least 2 × the clip's median flux. Measures run at a 10 ms hop. The drop's time is that onset's time, shown to 0.1 s.
  • It lands loud. The bass level (VW12) over the 2 s from the kick is within 3 dB of the clip's loudest 2 s.
  • And the build ends there, in either of two ways:
    • The roll stops. In the 2 s before the kick there are 3 or more kicks a second. In the 2 s from it there are at most 0.6 times as many, and still at least 1.5 a second. A roll at about 0.23 s gaps has given way to the beat. A breakdown, where the kicks stop altogether, doesn't count.
    • The mids fall. The 1–4 kHz level over the 1 s from the kick is at least 1.5 dB below the 2 s before it. The kick band over the 2 s from it is no quieter than over the 2 s before. That second test keeps out a breakdown where everything falls.
  • One drop, its front. Kicks that qualify within 4 s of each other are one drop, timed at the first of them. The beat settling a second or two later (clip 33's 0:31.0) is the same drop, not a new one.
  • Stored with each drop: which test it passed (roll, mids or both), and its four measures, as data.
VW15 · Which drop (Matas's rule, plainly)
  • One drop: it's the main drop.
  • Two or more: the main drop is the latest one with at least 10 s of clip after it and at least 3 s before it (VW2). If the later drop has too little after it, that means the earlier one.
  • None fits: VW3 refuses the clip, naming the latest drop. With under 10 s after it, that's "Too little after the drop". With under 3 s before it, it's "Drop too soon". No drop at all is "No clear drop". The words are VW3's, unchanged.
  • VW7's major/minor test is retired, because "lands loud" does its work. VW10's landing is retired too.
VW16 · The picker's words
  • Each drop row reads "{m:ss.s} · {why}", where {why} is "roll stops", "mids drop" or "roll stops · mids drop". The main drop's row starts "Main drop".
  • "rises {n} dB" retires, because the rise no longer finds drops.
  • VW8's order stands: the main drop first, then time order, with "Use this drop" or "· doesn't fit".
VW17 · Existing work

Acceptance

  • Real audio, all four clips, each within ±0.1 s, and each its clip's main drop under VW15:
    • 27 → 0:29.8
    • 29 → 0:16.0
    • 30 → 0:11.5
    • 33 → 0:30.0
    All four become fixtures, holding each kick's four measures from the clip's start to its end, not just the times.
  • If any clip misses, the build stops and reports, for every kick within 4 s of that clip's time:
    • the kicks a second before and after, the 1–4 kHz fall, the kick-band change, and the bass level against the clip's loudest 2 s;
    • where each clip's main drop lands for the roll ratio at 0.5, 0.6 and 0.7, with the mids fall at 1, 1.5, 2 and 3 dB.
    Design re-rules from that grid before merge. The build doesn't tune by trial.
  • Synthetic tests:
    • a roll at 0.23 s gaps giving way to 0.46 s beats is timed at the first beat kick, not the last roll hit;
    • a filter ramp ending in a mids fall with the roll running on is timed at the kick where the mids fall;
    • a breakdown, where the kicks stop or everything falls, isn't a drop;
    • a mids fall that doesn't land loud isn't a drop;
    • two qualifying kicks under 4 s apart are one drop, timed at the first;
    • VW15: the later of two drops when it has 10 s after, otherwise the earlier;
    • VW3's refusals name the latest drop.
  • Frames: VW-A at 1280 and 393 with the new row words. The VW-B baseline stands, with its fixtures restated as drops at the same times.
