Two campaigns per show: Wider and Retargeting

Matas ruled on 26 Nov: each show runs two campaigns. Wider is the prospecting campaign: Advantage+, lifetime budget. Retargeting is direct audiences only, on daily budgets, with a new Similar tier. The request is scratch/retargeting-two-campaigns-2026-11-26.md. Matas has since confirmed the split as proposed, and asked for the line that says where Similar’s share goes when it’s skipped. On 27 Nov, before merge, he set Similar’s window to 365 days and added the venue’s and promoter’s Pages to the show’s own pools (TC9). The merged record isn’t edited.

What stands from RF:

  • Cold, Warm and Hot and their windows (RF2), except that similar lists move to Warm.
  • The frequency ceiling (RF5), missing advertiser access (RF6), the lineup ad set going (RF7) and the £1.00 floor (RF8).
  • The share running 25 % to 50 % of the show’s spend, overlaps taking the higher stretch, and steps as scheduled changes.

What goes: RF4’s “takes £{x} a day from prospecting”. Wider is now the prospecting, so the money moves inside the show’s two campaigns.

Rulings

#Ruling
TC1 · Two campaigns
  • “{code} · Wider”: Advantage+ audience with suggestions on, lifetime budget to the show, or to on-sale for a presale show. Four ad sets, each “{code} · Wider · {set}”:
    • Broad, with the lineup artists as suggestions;
    • Similar pages, with the genre’s similar pages as suggestions;
    • Interests, with the genre’s interests;
    • Lookalikes: 1 % UK lookalikes of artist engagers and followers, signups and similar data lists, one per source, all in one ad set as suggestions. A source under Meta’s minimum is skipped, and the plan says so.
    Wider leaves out Warm, Hot and buyers. Cold stays open.
  • “{code} · Retargeting”: Advantage+ off (“Use as a suggestion” off) and daily budgets.
  • Location expansion is off on every ad set in both campaigns (#1401).
  • The Create line names both campaigns, Wider first. It gives Wider’s lifetime, end date, days and £ a day; Retargeting’s £ a day, share and stretch; then the show’s daily total split between them.
TC2 · Retargeting’s tiers
  • Coldest to hottest: Similar · Cold · Warm · Hot. Each tier leaves out every tier to its right, and buyers.
  • Buyers (upsell) is a fifth ad set only while the show has upsells. It sits outside the share, with a £ a day of its own, and its row says so.
TC3 · Similar
  • People who engaged with the show’s genre page group, 365 days (Matas, 27 Nov), minus Cold.
  • A group is pages we already have access to in that genre: the client’s own brand pages and pages shared with the client’s business. The group comes from events.genre_family, and Matas confirms each one. A Page with no known genre (106 of 487 on 27 Nov) stays out of every group, and so do venue and promoter Pages (TC9).
  • Meta takes 5 pages per engagement audience, so a bigger group is several audiences. The row says how many.
  • Skipped: a show with no group for its genre, or no genre, skips Similar. Its share goes to Cold (Matas, 26 Nov).
    • The Similar row says it, muted, in place of its audiences: “Similar skipped: there’s no {genre} page group yet, so its £{x} a day goes to Cold.”, or “… {code} has no genre …”.
    • Cold’s Budget adds RF5’s “Plus £{x} a day from Similar.”
TC4 · Similar lists to Warm
  • “Similar Data” customer lists move from Hot to Warm. Warm’s row ends “{n} similar lists”, and Hot’s no longer lists them.
TC5 · The split (confirmed)
  • Matas confirmed the proposed split on 26 Nov:

TC6 · Wider’s lifetime step
  • Each stretch step raises Retargeting by £{x} a day. It lowers Wider’s remaining lifetime budget by £{x} × the days left, never below £1.00 a day per Wider ad set. It doesn’t move money between ad sets.
  • The panel’s footer names the next step with both sides.
  • Wider’s row shows what’s left. After a step, a muted line says by how much, why and for which stretch.
  • When the floor stops a full step, the line says what Wider could give, in ochre, and Retargeting rises by that.
  • Each step is a scheduled change, so it shows on Today as MB1’s “Change budget” row. For Wider the change line is “{code} · Wider: £{x} → £{y} lifetime”, and its Undo is MB’s lifetime line.
TC7 · Presale shows
  • The same two campaigns, with signing up as the goal.
    • The top of the pyramid is sign-ups. They’re left out of both campaigns and get the presale reminder and WhatsApp invite, not upsells.
    • Hot is sign-up page visitors who didn’t sign up, 30 days.
    • Warm’s visitors are show page visitors, because there’s no ticket link yet.
  • Every “buyers” in Leaves out reads “signed up”. A line under the share says why.
  • The footer gives the on-sale date. From then, sign-ups move into Hot and buying becomes the goal.
  • Wider’s lifetime runs to the on-sale date.
TC9 · Venue and promoter Pages
  • A show’s own pools use its venue’s Page and the promoter’s brand Pages for that show alongside the lineup artists (Matas, 27 Nov). For an NX show that’s NX Newcastle, NX LOVES and NX In Session.
    • Cold: followers (all time), video viewers 75 % (180 days), engagers (180 days).
    • Warm: engagers (60 days), video viewers 95 % (60 days).
    • Hot: engagers (30 days).
  • Each tier row names whose Pages its account sources come from in one muted line: “From the venue’s and promoter’s Pages and the lineup artists: {n} accounts”. RF’s “· {n} accounts” after each source goes.
  • Venue and promoter Pages never join a genre group; in the genre list they’re “No single genre”.
  • plan_retargeting lists the venue and brand Pages each show matched, so Matas can correct a wrong match before the build.
  • RF6 covers them too: a missing account is named once per panel, and every tier builds without it.
TC8 · Told to Claude
  • plan_retargeting’s line gives both campaigns, then up to these follow-up lines:
    • Similar skipped, and why;
    • presale sign-ups left out;
    • each existing prospecting campaign as keep, pause or fold into Wider. Pausing and folding happen only after Matas says yes.
  • create_show_campaigns answers with both campaigns and the ad set and audience counts.
  • RF9’s skip reasons stand. “Already built” now means either campaign.

Acceptance

  • Two campaigns: names exactly as TC1 and TC2.
    • Wider: Advantage+ on with suggestions, lifetime.
    • Retargeting: Advantage+ off, daily.
    • Location expansion off on every ad set.
    A test checks each ad set’s audiences and exclusions.
  • Similar: page groups by genre, Matas-confirmed, with no unknown-genre, venue or promoter Page in any group. At most 5 pages per audience. A missing group or genre skips the tier, and its share goes to Cold, exactly.
  • Split: the plan matches TC5 on fixtures for every stretch, and Buyers sits outside it.
  • Steps: Retargeting rises by £{x} a day. Wider’s remaining lifetime falls by £{x} × days left, never under £1.00 a day per Wider ad set. Each step is a scheduled change with MB’s Undo.
  • Presale: sign-ups left out of both campaigns; Hot is sign-up page visitors who didn’t sign up; at on-sale, sign-ups move into Hot.
  • Venue and promoter Pages: in Cold, Warm and Hot with exactly TC9’s sources and windows; the plan lists each show’s matches.
  • Words: every string is a row, exactly.
