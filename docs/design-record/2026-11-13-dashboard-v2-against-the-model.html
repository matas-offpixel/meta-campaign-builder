Dashboard v2 against the model: axes, venues and totals that tie out

Matas compared v2 on prod (All shows, 14 days) with his "NX Newcastle sales vs spend" model: "no labelling on the axes makes it hard to understand, also total spends are not matching". This record brings v2 up to the model's clarity. It builds on 2026-11-13-dashboard-v2.html (D1–D13), which stands except where a ruling here says it changes a point there. Records aren't edited after merge, so this record carries those changes.

Why the totals don't match

The numbers on both screens are correct. They count different things. The model is one venue, Meta only. v2's All shows is the whole client (Electric Brixton), so it covers NX Newcastle, Electric Bristol and Electric Studios, on every channel. From the database, to 12 Nov:

Rulings

#Ruling
N1 · Axes

The chart gets real axes at both widths.

  • Left axis: money. Ticks at round steps. A step is 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4 or 5 × a power of ten: the smallest that covers the peak. That gives 6 intervals at 1280 and 3 at 393, with labels "£0 £100 … £600". At 393, £1,000 and above read "£1k". The left axis carries Spend or a cost line. When both are on, they're the same unit, so they share it.
  • Right axis: counts. The right axis has the same number of intervals as the left, so each right label sits on a left gridline. Its step is the smallest from the same set that covers the peak count, e.g. 0–9,000 in steps of 1,500 for 8,526 sold. At 393 it's 0–9k in steps of 3k.
  • Gridlines: one horizontal hairline per left tick (the border colour), a solid baseline, and no vertical gridlines.
  • Axis titles: small mono caps above each axis, naming what's on it: "SPEND (£)" or "COST PER TICKET (£)" on the left, and "TOTAL SOLD" or "CLICKS" on the right. With two count lines on, it's both names, "TOTAL SOLD · CLICKS"; with three or more, "COUNT".
  • Dates: the labels are anchored to the last day, so the end date is always labelled. The step is the smallest of 1, 2, 3, 7, 14, 21 or 28 days whose labels clear each other by at least 6 px, measured on the rendered label ("12 Nov" at 10.5 px is about 35 px). Since launch on Electric's data, that's every 7 days at 1280 and every 21 days at 393. A 14-day period at 1280 gets daily labels. A 3-day step overlaps at 1280 on this range, and a label that collides is worse than one that's missing. Format "8 Aug". Each label has a 4 px tick.
  • Caption above: "Left axis: {left}. Right axis: {right}." in plain words, e.g. "Left axis: spend (£). Right axis: total sold (tickets)." The "Index each line to its own peak" toggle sits on the same line, right-aligned.
  • Caption below: "Solid lines are volumes; the dashed line in the same colour is its cost." When Total sold is on, add "A dot is a real Internal Count; between two counts the line is a straight estimate." When Sales or Cost per ticket is on, add the model's sentence: "Sales and cost per ticket hold one value across the days between two Internal Counts."
  • Indexed (picked, or forced by three unit types, D7): the left axis is "0% … 100%" with the title "% OF OWN PEAK", and the right axis is hidden. The caption reads "Each line is drawn against its own peak (100%)." The hover still shows real values.
  • Hover: a dotted vertical rule and a card with the date, then each line's value and unit. The card sits right of the rule, and flips to the left when it would run past the plot. Estimated days say so, per D5.

Frames NV2 (1280) and NV4 (393) are drawn from the real NX data: Spend 6 Aug–12 Nov, and Total sold from the Internal Counts sums.

N2 · Scope: venues

A venue row sits above the show chips. It shows whenever the client has live shows at two or more venues.

  • Venue identity: events.venue_key, exactly as stored. It's the key the platform already uses. The display name is venue_name exactly as written on that venue's next show by date, never re-cased. Electric's live shows give three venues: "NX Newcastle", "Electric Bristol" and "Electric Studios".
  • Never inferred. A show with no venue_key isn't guessed into a venue from its code (NX/EBR/ES) or a name match. It goes under "Venue not set", on the internal view only. Today four have none: NX26-GLOBAL, NX27-EWANMCVICAR, EBR27-GLITTERBOX and ES27-LPRHYTHM. HN1 fills them through the event's existing write path, which derives the key from venue_name. That gives "nx newcastle", "nx newcastle", "electric bristol" and "electric studios".
  • Control: one segmented row: the venues by number of live shows, most first, then "All venues" last. NX Newcastle has 9, Electric Bristol 2, Electric Studios 1. The row is kept in the URL (venue={venue_key}, or all). At 393 it scrolls sideways, as the chips do, with the picked venue scrolled into view.
  • Default: the venue with the most live shows (ties go to the most since-launch spend). For Electric that's NX Newcastle, so v2 opens on the same scope as the model.
  • Show chips follow the venue. "All shows" means all live shows at that venue. The shows are in date order with that venue's umbrella last (N8), and "Include finished" comes after.
  • All venues: one chip row, grouped under small mono venue labels. Each group starts with "All {venue}", which selects that venue's All shows. The header, tiles and trajectory then cover the whole client, and the header says "3 venues".
  • Everything scoped (header, tiles, chart, trajectory, campaigns table, by-show table) follows the venue. The client link works the same way, within its token's client (D11).
  • With only one venue (most clients), there's no venue row, and the scope reads as that venue's name.
N3 · Totals that tie out

Every money total names three things: its scope (a venue, a show, or "All venues"), its channels (D12: a platform with no data isn't shown, a real £0 is), and what it leaves out.

  • Spend tile, Since launch: "{scope} · Meta £x · TikTok £y · Google £z".
  • Spend tile, in a period: the same, plus "of £{since launch} since launch" in the same scope. For NX that's "of £16,778", never the client-wide figure.
  • Hover on the Spend tile and on the header total: the breakdown, by venue under All venues and by show under a venue. Each line has its channel split. The last line reads "Not in this total: {venue} umbrella £{x}. Finished shows (Include finished adds them)."
  • Rounding: each figure is rounded from its unrounded parts, independently. Nothing is fudged to make the rounded parts add up. Electric's add up exactly today.
  • Cost per ticket says which shows it uses when that isn't all of them: "the 9 shows with counts".
N4 · The header sentence

Yes. v2 leads with one sentence, as the model does. It always runs to date, whatever period is picked (D7). Under it goes one mono line with the channel split, the data date and the exclusions.

  • Venue, All shows: "{Venue} to date: £{spend} spend since launch, {sold} tickets sold across {n} shows (Internal Counts, {date}), so £{cpt} of spend per ticket. Separate {Venue} umbrella spend: £{u}."
    Then: "META £x · TIKTOK £y · GOOGLE £z · TO {date} · FINISHED SHOWS NOT INCLUDED".
  • One show: "{Show} to date: £{spend} spend since launch, {sold} tickets sold (Internal Counts, {date}), so £{cpt} per ticket." For example, East End Dubs: £2,767, 1,731 sold, £1.60.
  • All venues: "{Client} to date, {n} venues: £{spend} spend since launch. {sold} tickets sold on the {k} shows with counts (Internal Counts, {date}), so £{cpt} of spend per ticket on those shows. Separate umbrella spend: {Venue} £{u}."
  • Umbrella: "{Venue} umbrella to date: £{spend} spend since launch, kept out of every show total. {n} signups on its page."
  • A source without data drops its clause rather than printing £0 or "0 tickets". For example, with no counts: "… £{spend} spend since launch. No ticket counts yet."
N5 · Default period

Since launch, for the internal page and the client link. Opening on 14 days was v2's own choice, not the model's. It's what made the header, tiles and trajectory describe three different spans on first open. A picked period stays in the URL (period), as now.

N6 · Trajectory

All shows draws bars, not text rows. DV2 already ruled the bars. This ruling adds the axis, the three summary tiles and the labels.

  • Three tiles above:
    • "Sold · count of {date}": {sold}, with "{pct}% of {sum of capacities} across {n} shows".
    • "Projected on show day": "{holds}–{high}", with "up to {pct}% of {sum} · each show capped at its capacity". No "~": the range already says it's a projection.
    • "Pace, last 7 days": "{n} a day", with "14-day {n} a day".
  • One row per show in date order: the name, a bar, and the label "{sold} → {holds}–{high}". The bar is layered sold → holds → best finish (D10), on a shared x-axis from 0 to the largest capacity. The ticks are 500 apart at 1280 and 1,000 at 393, and the last one reads "{n} capacity". Each show's own capacity is drawn as a dashed rule at its end of the bar.
  • Capped at the show's capacity (D6). When the holds case reaches it, the label reads "{sold} → SELLS OUT at this pace". When only the best case does, it reads "{sold} → {holds}–{cap} · SELLS OUT if best". The model drew Schak and Julian Fijma past 2,000; v2 doesn't.
  • A show with no counts reads "No ticket counts yet", and one with no capacity reads "Set capacity on the event". Both have an empty track and are left out of the sums.
  • A legend sits under the bars. Clicking a bar selects that show (D6).
  • At 393: the name and label share a line, the bar runs full width beneath them, and the three tiles stack.
N7 · Tile names, to date and in period

This re-rules D7's tile labels and D13's "Checkout actions". The order stays the same:

  1. Spend
  2. Total sold
  3. Sales
  4. Cost per ticket
  5. Checkouts + add to cart
  6. Cost per checkout / cart
  7. Landing page views
  8. Cost per LPV
  9. Clicks
  10. Cost per click
  11. Registrations (CIRQLIN)
  12. Cost per registration

The narrow labels at 393 are "Cost / ticket", "Checkouts + carts", "Cost / checkout", "LPVs", "Cost / LPV", "Cost / click", "Registrations" and "Cost / registration". "£ per …" and "CPC" go.

  • Every subline starts with its span: "since launch", "in 14 days", "yesterday" or "to date".
  • Total sold is always "to date · Internal Counts, {date}", whatever the period.
  • Sales: tickets in the period. Since launch, "since launch, to {date}". In a period, "in 14 days · {n} a day".
  • Checkouts + add to cart: the subline keeps D13's honesty: "{span} · actions, one person can do both".
  • Registrations (CIRQLIN) follows the period like every other tile. When the period isn't since launch, the subline adds the to-date figure: "in 14 days · {n} to date". Since launch, it's "to date · signup pages close at on-sale". When the venue's umbrella has a page (N8), add "plus {n} on the umbrella page". That figure is never added into the tile's value.
  • Cost per registration: "show pages, inside their signup windows" (D4).
  • Cost per checkout / cart, Cost per LPV and Cost per click divide Meta spend only, because their counts are Meta's (D3). Cost per ticket divides all channels (D12).
N8 · The umbrella
  • Chip: "Umbrella", dashed, last in its venue's row. In All venues it sits inside its venue's group, so it's never ambiguous.
  • Everywhere outside a chip row (the header sentence, hovers, the campaigns table), it's "{Venue} umbrella": "NX Newcastle umbrella". The model's "NX umbrella (GLOBAL)" depends on a short name ("NX") that isn't stored anywhere. The event name ("Global Marketing") is the umbrella view's subtitle, and the event code shows only in internal hovers. This changes D1's "Separate {client} umbrella spend" to "{Venue}" now that venues scope everything.
  • Its page: an umbrella can link a CIRQLIN page, the same field as a show (D9), so D9 stops implying it can't. NX26-GLOBAL links to nx-loves-aw26, the NX-wide signup page: 808 signups to 12 Nov, 57 of them in the last 14 days. HN1 sets the link.
  • Where it shows: on the umbrella's own view, Registrations reads "808 · to date · {page}". Cost per registration is all umbrella spend inside the page's signup window, labelled so: "all umbrella spend, 18 Sep–12 Nov" (£5.22). On the venue's All shows, it's only the "plus 808 on the umbrella page" line (N7), kept out of 6,068, as umbrella spend is kept out of show spend.
  • An umbrella still has no Total sold, Sales or Cost per ticket tile, and no trajectory (D9).
