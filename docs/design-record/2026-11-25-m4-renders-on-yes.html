A render package runs on the same yes as a Meta change

Matas ruled on 25 Nov: render packages run on his typed yes in Claude, the same as Meta changes under the full-control record (FC1–FC4). This record re-rules M4’s RT3 and RT4 to match. The merged M4 record isn’t edited.

What stands:

  • RT1’s picker and RT2’s render row. Neither pointed at Today’s Approve, so neither screen changes.
  • Starting a render in the Lab needs no approval, because Matas starts it himself.
  • Owner and rights stay separate.
  • The automatic checks block upload.
  • A file that passes can be uploaded with no further step.

Rulings

#Ruling
RY1 · The yes in Claude
  • render_package carries FC’s approval { asked, answer }. Without it, the call is refused and nothing renders.
  • The yes does what Approve on Today did: it starts the render and approves uploading every file that passes the checks.
  • Matas judges Voice and Style from the question, so asked carries what RT4’s row used to show:
    • the recipe and file count;
    • whose clips they are;
    • the claim, and any text in the artist’s voice.
  • There’s no Approve or Reject on Today for renders. Today’s Approve, Reject, waiting and expiry words now serve nothing in M3 or M4. They stay in TD’s list, unused.
  • On a yes, Claude is told “Rendering {files} for {code}. Each is filed in Drive as it finishes.”, then RT3’s done lines, unchanged.
RY2 · Today’s row
  • A package is an FC1 log row:
    • “Render package · {code}”;
    • “{recipes}: {files}”;
    • “Approved in Claude · {date, time}”, with “What Claude asked”;
    • while it runs, “Rendering · {n} of {m} files done”;
    • then “Done · {files} rendered and filed in Drive · {p} passed every check”, “{f} failed: {checks}” in brick when any failed, and “Open in the Lab”.
  • No Undo, said in one muted line: “No Undo: rendered files stay in the event’s Drive folder.”
  • Refused packages are logged muted, as FC1:
    • no approval (FC’s row);
    • needs missing (RT4’s row);
    • “Refused · over the {c}-file cap”;
    • “Refused · {client}’s footage in the picks”;
    • “Refused · artist-voice copy with no approved caption”.
RY3 · Refusals told to Claude
  • Each refusal ends “Nothing was rendered.”, in FC2’s form:
    • no approval: ask Matas, then send his exact question and answer;
    • over the package’s cap: “{n} files, over the {c}-file cap”. The builder sets {c} and sends it to design;
    • another client’s promoter footage in the picks (AL1): counted, naming the client who filmed it and the show’s client, with a one-form;
    • artist-voice copy with no approved caption: it gives the two ways out, an approved caption or the promoter’s voice;
    • needs missing: RT3’s line, which now ends “Nothing was rendered.” where it said “Nothing was sent to Today.”
RY4 · Words retired
  • today.mcp.row.render.inputs and today.mcp.row.render.approve_note are removed from M4’s list. They described Today’s Approve, and the question in “What Claude asked” now carries the inputs.
  • No word on the picker or on a render row pointed at Today, so nothing there changes.
