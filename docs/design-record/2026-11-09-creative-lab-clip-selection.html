Creative Lab — clip selection, and the words that say how a clip becomes an ad

From Matas's first live run, two things. Selection: every usable clip goes into every test today; he needs to choose which go in — all, none, or a hand-picked set — and have that choice drive the previews, the round and the renders. Words: nothing on the Lab says a clip becomes an ad only when a round pairs it with an approved caption. This record rules a selection state on the clip tile, the Clips header that controls it, the generator's Pair with reading it, the round matrix narrowed to it, the two header notes, and the three pending states Claude Code is adding. Exports (added before the PR, Matas): every finished render is copied to the event's folder on the Off/Pixel Shared Drive, the event header links to it, render rows say when a copy is there, and the MML's ② Creatives intake lists this event's finished renders as a source. Frames at 1280 and 393. Every value not read from the code or named by Matas is under Invented.

S · Rulings

#Ruling
S1 The tickEvery usable clip tile carries a selection tick: a 22 px circle on the frame's top-right corner (6 px inset), white ring on a dark wash when not selected, filled ink with a white ✓ when selected; the selected tile also takes a 2 px ink outline. Tapping the tick toggles selection; tapping the frame still plays (F3, rule 4: one primary action). The hit area is 32 px at 393. A tile is never dimmed for being unselected — dimming already means "skipped" (candidates at 55 %) and "can't run". Colour never alone (rule 10): the ✓ is the signal, the outline repeats it.
S2 What can be selectedUsable clips only: New and Keeping (and a Spark post, which keeps its own "boosted as is" row in the round, L13). A Can't run tile has no tick, is never selected and is not in the count. A clip that becomes Can't run while selected leaves the selection, and the header says so once ("Enzo #3 left the selection — too long").
S3 The Clips headerUnder the title, the S9 note. Then one line: "{n} of {m} selected" ({n} bold, {m} = usable clips) and two text actions, Select all · None; the one that would change nothing is muted (at all selected, "Select all" is muted). They are secondary to the header's primary action (Find clips on Instagram) and are not inputs (rule 3 unchanged). At 393 the count line sits under the note, the actions at its right.
S4 Default, and new clipsDefault: all usable clips selected. The event's selection has two modes: all (never narrowed, or Select all pressed) — every usable clip is selected, including clips imported later; chosen (any tick or None pressed) — exactly the ticked clips. In chosen, a newly imported clip arrives unselected and the count line adds "· 1 new, not selected" until it is ticked or the line is dismissed. Select all returns to all.
S5 PersistencePer event, server-side, for everyone who opens the event — not per browser. Saved on each tap with who and when (rule 5: never on screen). A round that ran keeps the clips it ran with; changing the selection never edits a past round.
S6 Pair withThe generator's Pair with becomes Selected clips · Best of selected · Each selected, all reading the selection, never the whole grid. Selected clips: one preview per caption, the captions spread across the selected clips in display order (caption 1 on the first, caption 2 on the second, wrapping). Best of selected: one preview per caption, all on the selected clip with the lowest cost per result in the client's band; before any round has run, the first selected clip. Each selected: every caption on every selected clip, grouped per caption, the clips scrolling sideways; the line above says "{captions} × {clips} = {n} previews". Default: Best of selected (parity with today's Best clip). These are previews, not ads (S9).
S6b None selectedThe writer still writes (captions don't need a clip). Previews draw the caption on a plain plate with one sentence: "No clip selected — select clips to see the captions on them." Pair with stays visible.
S7 The roundThe matrix's rows are the selected usable clips of the round's artist (the round stays per artist, as proposeRound builds it); columns are the approved captions; every cell starts on and can still be tapped off (F5's knock-out, kept). Unselected clips do not appear, not even as off rows. The round bar reads "{n} ads · {clips} × {captions}, {off} left out" as before, then "· {k} of {artist}'s {m} clips not selected", and under the matrix one muted line names them, up to five then "+n" ("Not selected: Enzo #3, Enzo #4, Enzo #6 · change on Clips"). A round-2 plan that keeps a winner the selection has dropped says so in one ochre line ("Enzo #2, round 1's winner, is not selected") — the selection wins.
S7b Nothing to pairNo selected clip for the round's artist: the button is off with "Select at least 1 clip to propose round {n}." No approved caption: F5's sentence stands. Both missing: the clip sentence first.
S8 RendersA render runs only for an on cell of a proposed round. Unselecting a clip never deletes a render that exists; it only keeps the clip out of the next round. Generate's previews are drawn in the browser and are never renders.
S9 The wordsTwo one-line notes, verbatim, muted ink-2 under the section titles. Clips: "Selected clips become ads when a round pairs them with an approved caption". Captions: "✓ a caption, then Propose round 1 renders it on the selected clips", with "round 1" following the next round's number ("Propose round 2" once round 1 has run). No full stops added. The notes never change with state and are not dismissible.
S10 The stripThe Next line stays exactly as F1 ruled. The strip's Clips cell keeps its count ({m} usable); its state line reads "{n} selected" when the event is in chosen mode, and as today in all.
S11 Pending statesOne busy state for an action button: the label becomes the action's present participle with an ellipsis — Finding… (Find clips, on the Clips header and the artist row), Importing… (Add on a candidate tile, with the tile's progress bar under it, then ✓ Added), Writing… (Write {n}). The button keeps its idle width, is disabled at 60 %, and nothing else in the section locks: ticks, Select all · None and other tiles stay usable. No spinner glyph without the word. On failure the label returns and one sentence under the control says what to do (rule 7).
S12 393Ticks at 22 px with a 32 px hit area; the count line under the note; the grid 2 across with all usable tiles in it (no sideways group hides a tick); Pair with stays visible (Mode · Angle · Pair with are the three inputs; How many and Stage behind More); the matrix is the list of pairings, first three then "+n more · {off} off", the round bar's not-selected count on its own line. Exports: "Drive folder ↗" drops under the event name beside View client; render rows keep the mark on their second line; From the Lab rows stack frame, name and Add, with round, length and the mark on one line under the name.
S13 Exports — whereEvery finished render (status ready) is copied to the event's folder on the Shared Drive "Off/Pixel Exports": Off/Pixel Exports / {Client} / {Event code} {Event name} / Creative Lab / Round {n} — for Puzzle 4th Birthday, Off/Pixel Exports / Puzzle / P26-BDAY Puzzle 4th Birthday / Creative Lab / Round 1. Folders are created on the first copy that needs them. Client and event names as written on their records; the event code verbatim (never normalised). An event with no code uses its name alone. The Shared Drive is Off/Pixel's; nothing is shared with the client from here.
S14 Exports — the file nameMatas's pattern {event code}_{artist}-{clip n}_{angle}-{stage}_r{n}.mp4, amended: three Lineup captions on Enzo #1 in round 1 would all be P26-BDAY_enzo-siragusa-1_lineup-on-sale_r1.mp4, so the angle carries the caption's number — {event code}_{artist}-{clip n}_{angle}{caption n}-{stage}_r{n}.mp4 → P26-BDAY_enzo-siragusa-1_lineup2-on-sale_r1.mp4. Artist lower-case with hyphens (accents dropped, nothing else changed); angle the style key with hyphens (lineup, artist-first-person, pov, scarcity, quote); stage signup or on-sale; the event code verbatim. A re-render of the same pairing in the same round adds _v2, never overwrites.
S15 Render rowsA finished row gains a small "in Drive" mark (olive dot + the words, ink outline) once the copy has landed; while copying, nothing extra is drawn. If the copy fails after its retries, the row says "Drive copy failed — retry" in brick, "retry" the link that re-queues it (rule 7). A failed or missing copy never blocks pairing, proposing or launch — the render in the app is the ad; the Drive file is a copy.
S16 The event header"Drive folder ↗" sits next to View client on the event header, opening the event's folder ({Event code} {Event name}) in a new tab. It appears once the folder exists (the first copy); the header has no empty state for it. Operator pages only — never on a client page (as R3).
S17 MML ② Creatives — From the LabThe intake gains a source, From the Lab, listing this event's finished renders, newest round first, one row each: frame · pairing ("Enzo #1 × Lineup 'at Circuit'") · round · 9:16 · length · the "in Drive" mark when copied · Add (→ ✓ Added). An added render becomes an intake asset like an upload, keeps its pairing as its name, and enters the match groups as the 9:16 of its creative. Renders of other events never appear. Empty: "No finished renders for this event yet — propose a round in the Creative Lab."

CLS1 · The clip tiles with a selection, and the Clips header (1280 and 393)

Puzzle 4th Birthday before round 1: nine usable clips and one that can't run. Matas has ticked the first, second, fifth and seventh — his "clips 1, 2, 5, 7".

The tick sits where nothing else does: the source badge owns the top-left, the length the bottom-right, the play glyph the middle. An unselected tile looks exactly like a tile today plus an empty ring — it isn't greyed, because grey already means skipped or can't run. The count is the hierarchy; Select all and None are words, not buttons, because Find clips is the header's one action.

CLS2 · Generate reads the selection — Pair with (1280 and 393)

The generator never looks past the selection. With Selected clips the three captions land on three different selected clips, so one Write shows the selection's range; Best of selected puts all three on one clip; Each selected shows the full cross, grouped per caption, and says how many previews that is before drawing them. Unselected clips (Enzo #3, #4, #6, Luke Dean, Reeshy) appear nowhere here.

CLS3 · The round, narrowed to the selection (1280 and 393)

The round is still one artist's, as proposeRound builds it; the selection decides its rows. Twelve pairings, one knocked out, eleven ads — the knock-out is per pairing exactly as F5 drew it, so "Enzo #2 with these two captions but not that one" is still one tap. What's missing is named, not hidden: the three unselected Enzo clips are listed under the matrix and counted on the bar.

CLS5 · Exports — the event header and the render rows (1280 and 393)

The file name carries the caption's number after the angle (lineup1, lineup2) — without it, the three Lineup captions on Enzo #1 would share one name (S14).
