Top tracks: a ranked-list reel

Matas, 18 Nov. This is a new Lab render, posted from the promoter's or venue's page. It plays 3 to 5 of one artist's tracks back to back over a still or clips of the artist. A list on screen names each track as it plays.

The reference is a creator's reel, "What's your CamelPhat level?": a percent ladder down the left, with each rung's track name appearing as that track plays. It builds on:

  • the snippet pipeline: CT1's sound, CT2 and AU1's files, CT3's Spotify picker, CT4's drop marker and AU3's rights flag;
  • the 9:16 rules, O95 and O96;
  • #1326's framing, O117–O121, with O19, O39 and O63;
  • the kit and band rules: O24, O25, O40, O46, O49–O51 and O57;
  • T1's end pieces, O23 and O54, and the approved claim ladder;
  • owners and filing: W1–W4, D1–D2 and S14.

All of those stand. Values not taken from them are listed under "Invented here". The fixture's tracks, date and claim are invented.

Rulings

#Ruling
TK1 · The format
  • One 9:16 video, 1080 × 1920, and nothing else. There's no 4:5. O95 stands: video is one 9:16, and Feed shows its centre 4:5 (y 285–1635). The whole layout sits inside O96's band (x 120–960, y 353–1238), so Feed's crop shows every line. A separate 4:5 would add nothing, and it would re-rule O95.
  • One artist per reel, one lineup act, named exactly as the Lab lineup. Titles come from Spotify's top tracks (CT3). Audio comes only from files in 02 Source / Audio / {artist} that pass AU1 (CT2).
  • The file name follows S14. It files under Videos for the background's owner (TK7, D1–D2):
    • {event code}_{artist}_top-tracks-{framing}-{n}x{s}s-{cta|nocta}-{stage}_r{n}_9x16.mp4
    • {framing} is ranked, level, forever or cant-live.
TK2 · The headline
  • Four headlines, and the operator picks one. The words are Matas's and exact. {artist} is the lineup name. {n} is the number of tracks.
    • "Top {artist} tracks ranked 1–{n}"
    • "{artist} tracks that changed music forever"
    • "What's your {artist} level?"
    • "{artist} tracks we can't live without"
    No writer, no other words. The apostrophe is the kit's typographic ’. The kit's case applies; Puzzle's is uppercase.
  • Ranked and Level are Spotify's order. The picked tracks keep Spotify's top-tracks order, which is never re-sorted (CT3). The numbers 1–{n} and the rungs are places in that order. A Drive-only track, one that isn't in Spotify's top tracks, can't go into a Ranked or Level reel: it has no place to stand on. The other two framings are the promoter's own taste, so their order is the operator's.
  • Size. Cap 0.024 H (46 px) in the kit's display face, at most 2 lines in 840 px, with size steps 0.85 and 0.75. A line break never splits the artist's name. If it still doesn't fit: "The headline doesn't fit two lines for {artist} — pick another framing."
  • It shows from frame 0 until the end card.
TK3 · The audio: track length and crossfades
  • Two lengths. One length applies to every slot in a reel.
    • 4 or 5 tracks: 6, 7 or 8 s each, 7 s by default.
    • 3 tracks: 10 to 15 s each, 12 s by default.
    The default reel is 5 × 7 s.
  • Every slot is cut around its track's drop, from CT4's drop marker, which the operator can move as CT4 allows. The slot starts 2.0 s before the drop for 6–8 s slots, and 4.0 s before for 10–15 s slots, which is CT1's lead.
  • Crossfades are equal-power and centred on each slot boundary: 0.5 s for 6–8 s slots, 1.0 s for 10–15 s. The drops of two tracks never line up, so a short fade avoids two beats fighting.
    • The first slot fades in over 0.25 s (CT1).
    • The last slot runs on under the end card and fades out over its last 1.5 s (CT1).
  • Loudness. Each snippet is normalised to −14 LUFS before joining. The joined reel is checked at −14 LUFS integrated (± 1), with peaks no higher than −1 dBTP, encoded as AAC at 256 kbps (CT1).
  • Room in the track. A slot needs its lead before the drop and the rest after it, plus 3 s more for the last slot. Without that room: "{title}: the drop is too close to the {start | end} for a {s} s slot — move the marker or pick another track."
  • Rights. AU3 stands: a flagged file can't be picked. When Meta mutes a reel, every file in it is flagged, because Meta doesn't say which track it heard. The render row reads "Meta muted the audio (rights) · {n} files flagged".
  • A cap of 45 s of tracks. 5 × 8 s and 3 × 15 s are the longest reels. The trade-off table below shows why 10–20 s per track stops at 15.
TK4 · Order, reveal and the playing row
  • Ranked counts down. Ranks 1–{n} show top-down from frame 0 at 40 %. #{n} plays first and #1 plays last, so the list fills from the bottom. The best track is the reason to stay to the end, so it comes last.
  • Level climbs. The rungs show from frame 0:
    • 5 tracks: 10%, 50%, 100%, 500%, 1000%;
    • 4 tracks: 10%, 50%, 100%, 1000%;
    • 3 tracks: 10%, 100%, 1000%.
    Spotify's most played track takes the top rung, 10%, and the deepest cut takes 1000%, the joke the reference makes. It fills top-down.
  • Forever and can't-live have no numbers. Empty rows are 2 px rules at 40 %. The rows fill top-down in the operator's order, which defaults to Spotify's.
  • A row's title appears as its slot starts, at the crossfade's midpoint: a 0.25 s fade with a 24 px slide in from the list's outer side.
  • The playing row. The title is at 100 %, the number or rung turns the kit accent, and a 6 px accent bar runs the row's height on the list's outer edge (x 120, or x 954 for a list on the right). Played rows drop to 70 %. Unplayed numbers and rungs sit at 40 %.
TK5 · A row
  • The title is Spotify's, verbatim: "Track four (Club Mix)" stays whole. Only the kit's case is applied. A title is never shortened or edited.
  • Other artists go on a second line: "with {names}", from Spotify's artist list minus this artist. Names are joined with ", " and " & " before the last, at 0.65 of the title's cap. The line is left out when the title already names them ("feat." or "with").
  • Sizes.
    • Titles: cap 0.021 H (40 px) for 4–5 rows, 0.027 H (52 px) for 3, in the kit's info face (the face T2's details use). At most 2 lines in the column after the marker, with size steps 0.85 and 0.75.
    • Numbers: the display face, cap 56 / 72 px.
    • Rungs: cap 44 / 56 px.
  • If a title doesn't fit at the last step: "'{title}' is too long for the list — pick another track."
TK6 · Where it sits
  • Headline: x 120–960, cap top at 353.
  • List: a 480 px column, at x 120–600 for a list on the left or x 480–960 on the right, left-aligned either way. It starts 0.036 H (70 px) under the headline's last baseline. Its last line's baseline sits on or above 1238. The rows share the height equally.
  • The end card takes the list's column.
  • Nothing goes above 353, below 1238, or outside x 120–960 (O96).
TK7 · The background, and no text on a face
  • Sources. The artist or the event, never someone dancing to camera. The operator picks one:
    • Press shot: a still the Lab can already use for an artist card (O19, O22). It moves in a slow push from 1.00 to 1.04 across the reel, centred on the head, but only when the photo has the pixels: no upscaling.
    • DJ moment: one artist clip at least as long as the reel.
    • Clip montage: one artist clip per slot, each at least as long as its slot, cut hard on the slot boundary.
    • Event b-roll: the promoter's own footage from 02 Source / Venue & crowd, as one clip or one per slot.
    Clips are muted: the tracks are the only sound. Every clip passes O93: a 9:16 window of at least 675 px.
  • One owner per reel (W4). Artist footage (press shot, DJ moment, montage) or promoter b-roll, never both. The reel files under that owner (D2).
  • The head goes on the side away from the list. This amends O120's centring for this template only.
    • With the list on the left, the head's centre sits at 0.74 W. With the list on the right, at 0.26 W. Either way within 0.03 W.
    • Clips use O120's head finding (the face box, or the top 0.18 of the person mask) and crop focus x, within O63.
    • Stills put the eyes at 0.40 H (band 0.36–0.46), with a face box 0.18–0.30 W wide. O19 and O119's side extension (up to 0.12 W) apply.
    The list starts on the side the source leaves free. "List on the right" / "List on the left" switches it. The picture is never flipped.
  • The test runs on every text piece, in every state: headline, rows and end card. For stills it is O118's (no text pixel inside the head box grown by 0.02 H). For clips it is O39's limits on the head box (≤ 10 % of the window, ≤ 0.5 s in a row).
  • Refusals.
    • "The artist's head is where the list goes — choose another {photo | clip}."
    • "The photo can't put the artist clear of the list: it ends at the {left | right}."
    • In a montage: "In this clip the artist is on the list's side — choose another clip." Every clip's head must sit on the same side, so the list never jumps.
TK8 · Bands and contrast
  • Two bands. O25's top band (20 % black, to 0.44 H). A side band behind the list: 55 % black at the list's outer edge, falling to 0 at 0.60 W (mirrored for a right-hand list), dithered (O46).
  • Contrast is measured on the p95 sample (O40), on the playing state (≥ 4.5:1) and the played state (≥ 3:1). Before refusing, the side band steps 55 → 70 → 85 % (O57). Then: "Too busy behind the list — choose another background."
  • The kit accent is decided per reel, as O40 decides red. If it misses 3:1 at the top step, the marker and bar go white.
TK9 · The last frame
  • With the date (default), 3.0 s. The headline and list fade out over 0.4 s. In the list's column, top-down:
    • the kit's event lockup, top at 353, 0.22 W;
    • the artist's name, cap 0.05 H, O3's steps to fit the column, at most 2 lines, never split inside a word;
    • T1's date line (O54);
    • the event's approved live claim, with its last line on 1238 (O23, with its steps fitting the column).
    With no approved live claim there's no claim: just lockup, name and date. Rule 11 and the approved ladder decide, never this template.
  • No CTA, 2.5 s. The full list holds at 100 %, with no highlight, under the headline. Nothing is added: no date, no claim, no lockup.
  • Both ends run over the last track's tail, which fades over the final 1.5 s.
  • If the claim doesn't fit the column at its last step: "The claim doesn't fit beside the artist — make the no-CTA version." Claims aren't edited to fit.
TK10 · In the Lab
  • A "Top tracks" tab beside Snippet (CT10). One artist chip row (CT3). The card leads with the 9:16 preview (rule 2), playable with sound as CT5's is. Three visible inputs (rule 3):
    • Framing: four chips with the headline as it will read;
    • Tracks: CT3's picker, in a sheet;
    • Background: four chips.
    Length, list side and end sit behind Edit. The primary action is "Make the reel". Renders land as render rows.
  • Strings.
    • "{n} of 5 picked · at least 3" (CT3's count, with a floor of 3).
    • "Order follows Spotify's top tracks" under Ranked and Level; "Drag to reorder" under the other two.
    • "{n} × {s} s · {total} s with the end card"; "Not on Spotify's top tracks, so it can't be ranked" on a Drive-only row under Ranked or Level.
    • The refusals in TK2, TK3, TK5, TK7, TK8 and TK9.
  • At 393, the preview sits full width above the inputs. The track picker and Edit open as bottom sheets, and the chips wrap.

The trade-off: how long each track plays

"#1 lands" is when the countdown's last slot starts, (n − 1) × s. "Bars" is time after the drop at 124 BPM, where a bar is 1.94 s. The lengths include the 3 s end card.

7 s × 538 s28.0 s1 bar, the drop, 2.6 barsDefault: every pick lands on its drop, and #1 is in before 30 s.
8 s × 543 s32.0 s1 bar, the drop, 3.1 barsAllowed

12 s × 339 s24.0 s2 bars, the drop, 4.1 bars3-track default: fewer tracks, and each one heard long enough to recognise a deeper cut.
15 s × 348 s30.0 s2 bars, the drop, 5.7 barsAllowed, and the longest: 45 s of tracks.
20 s × 363 s40.0 s2 bars, the drop, 8.2 bars, a full phraseNot offered. 60 s of tracks, and #1 is 40 s in.
20 s × 5103 s80.0 sa full phrase eachNot offered. #1 lands after 80 s, so most viewers never reach it.

Matas's 10–20 s range is kept up to 15 s on the 3-track reel. Past that, each extra second per track pushes #1, the payoff, later, and the list stops being the point. The cap applies to tracks only (45 s); the end card adds to it.
