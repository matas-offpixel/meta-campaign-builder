This issue covers two things. First, Matas has answered four open questions (O103–O107), and #1182's fixed centre crop needs a ruling. Second, it adds a new video layout. The triple-fit

Three landscape parts stacked in one 9:16, each a third of its height, with one part's sound. T1's type sits in issue 15's band. TF1 is the frame. TF2 is what Feed's centre 4:5 shows of it. TF3 is how each part is cut from its source.

Rulings

#Ruling
O108 When it's offered
  • A triple-fit clip is one too small for its own 9:16 (its 9:16 window is under 675 px, O93) whose part window (O109) is at least 675 px wide. It's imported, not refused. Its tile has one status line:
    • "Landscape — triple-fit only".
  • These tiles sit in their owner's group (W2), in a "Triple-fit only · {n}" group after the clips that can run and before "Can't run". They have no selection tick, so they never go into a single-clip round.
  • A clip under both floors keeps "Too small for 9:16". O93's import refusal applies only when both floors fail, with its words unchanged.
  • The offer: "Make a triple-fit" shows on the "Triple-fit only" group only when both are true:
    • the owner has fewer than 3 clips that can run in 9:16 (O93, O102: 9:16-capable and not refused);
    • the owner has at least 3 triple-fit clips.
  • Never automatic: the Lab never proposes a triple-fit, and a triple-fit never takes a 9:16 clip's place in a round. A landscape clip that does make its own 9:16 can be a part only when picked by hand. A portrait clip is never a part.
  • Examples:
    • Luke 1 (956 × 718): its 9:16 window is 404 px, under the floor; its part window is 956 px, so it's a triple-fit clip.
    • Luke 15, 16, 18 and 21 follow the same two widths. I don't have their sizes. The import reads them, and the tile shows the result.
O109 The layout
  • Three different clips from one owner, never one clip twice. A clip in two parts reads as a glitch, and its sound would sit on two parts.
  • Each part is 1080 × 640: part 1 is y 0–640, part 2 is y 640–1280, part 3 is y 1280–1920. They are exact thirds, with no gaps and no dividers. The seam is where the two footages meet.
  • Crop: each part's window in its source has the part's shape (1.6875 : 1). It is as wide as the source allows: width = min(source width, source height × 1.6875).
    • A 16:9 source keeps its full height, and the window is centred, with crop_focus_x moving it across the source's slack (1280 × 720 → a 1215 × 720 window).
    • A 4:3 source keeps its full width, and crop_focus_y places it (O41) (956 × 718 → 956 × 566).
    • Each part's focus is set on the part and stays for the whole length.
  • Scale: O63's limit, at most 1.6× from the window, which is the 675 px floor. O69's filter applies.
  • The render copy for a triple-fit clip wider than 1080 is its part window at 1080 × 640 (Lanczos, CRF ≤ 16, as O68 and O101).
  • Order: the sound clip is always part 2. Only the middle third is whole in both crops: Feed shows part 1's lower 355 px and part 3's upper 355 px (TF2), and part 3 sits under the Reels controls. Parts 1 and 3 are the next two by likes on their posts, the higher on top. The operator can swap 1 and 3, or pick another sound clip, which then moves to part 2.
  • Length: the shortest of the three clips' stored windows, at most 30 s. Each part plays from its own in-point, with no loops and no freezes. Under 6 s it isn't made, because the claim needs 1.5 s plus at least 3 s on screen (O37).
O110 Sound
  • Exactly one part's sound plays: part 2's, from its own in-point and in sync with its picture. Parts 1 and 3 are muted for the whole length, at volume 0, with no ducking and no mix.
  • The pick: by default, the clip with the most likes on its post (the viral one) among the three whose sound can run. A sound can run when it isn't flagged "Music match — mute or replace before launch" and the clip has an audio track.
  • The operator can choose another, and the tile reads "Sound from #{n}".
  • If none of the three has sound that can run, it isn't made: "No clip with sound to run — choose another".
O111 Type
  • A triple-fit takes T1 on a clip, arrangement B only (O54, moved by O97):
    • the lockup at 353;
    • the name's cap top at 469;
    • the date above the claim;
    • the claim's last baseline on 1238.
    Everything stays inside issue 15's band.
  • A's date (632–689) would cross the seam at 640, so A is never used. No type ink sits within 24 px of a seam (y 616–664 and 1256–1304). B's top block ends at 603 and its date starts at 963.
  • The top block sits on part 1, and the date and claim on part 2. Part 3 carries no type.
  • O55's bands stay anchored to the blocks and run across the seams. O40's contrast is read on whichever part's pixels sit under each type box. Red is decided per triple-fit.
  • Not on a triple-fit: UGC captions and quote mode, because their blocks centre on 0.355 H and 0.28 H and cross the 640 seam. T2 isn't used either (O116).
O112 Faces
  • Tested per part, on that part's own window:
    • the floor is 0.08 of the part's width (O64);
    • time in the type (O39, O65, O71) is measured against the type boxes over that part.
    Part 1 is tested against the lockup and name, and part 2 against the date and claim. Part 3 has no type boxes, so it has no face-in-type test.
  • Crop candidates (O56) are tried per part. The first set that passes is kept for the whole length.
  • A refusal names the part's clip: "Face in the type for {n} % of #{clip} — choose another crop or clip".
O113 Rights
  • Each part keeps its own clip's rights (rights_ref, and the "✓ Cleared — artist channels too" gate).
  • A triple-fit runs only on the channels all three are cleared for. The approval card lists the three source posts.
  • If no channel is common to all three, it isn't made: "No channel all three clips are cleared for".
  • W4 holds: the three clips have one owner.
O114 In the Lab
  • A triple-fit is one 9:16 clip from then on. It has its own tile in its owner's group:
    • "Triple-fit · #{a} · #{b} · #{c}", in part order;
    • "Sound from #{b}" under it.
  • It is selectable, and it pairs only with T1, arrangement B.
  • It renders, launches and lists in "From the Lab" as one 9:16 asset (O95). Its three clips stay as their own tiles.
  • Deleting it is never needed. Unticking it takes it out of rounds.
O115 Invented hereThese values are invented, not measured, and are judged on drafts:
  • the 24 px seam clearance;
  • the 6 s minimum;
  • the part order by likes;
  • "fewer than 3" for the offer;
  • every new line of words in O108, O110, O112, O113 and O114.
O116 The promoter's triple-fit
  • Promoter clips become triple-fit clips at import (O108), and their tiles show it.
  • "Make a triple-fit" isn't shown for the promoter yet. Promoter footage takes T2 on a clip, and T2's lineup runs from 397 to 698 (or from 353 when it moves up), which crosses the 640 seam.
  • T2 on a triple-fit needs its own layout in a later issue, with drafts.
