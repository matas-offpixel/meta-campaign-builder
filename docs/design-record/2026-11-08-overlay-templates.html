Three named templates for the assets the asset bank makes from a brand kit (pass 2 B5/B7, design-rules rule 14, asset bank issue 2 A5'). Each is drawn from Matas's real Puzzle assets, measured, and ruled as slots with sizes as a fraction of frame height. The 4:5 information rectangle is drawn on every frame. Every value not read from an asset or the PSD is listed under Invented.

O · Rulings

#Ruling
O1 One master, one rectangleEvery asset is composed at 9:16, 1080×1920. The 4:5 rectangle (1080×1350, y 285–1635, 14.84 % in from top and bottom) is the information rectangle: every slot sits inside it, so the 4:5 is a pure centre crop of the master. The 1:1 (1080×1080, 21.875 % in) is not a crop: it is the 4:5 arrangement scaled by 0.8 (1080 ÷ 1350) — every slot at the same fraction of the 1:1 rectangle, type at 0.8× its pixel size — with the background extended at the sides, rendered from the same props. Three files per asset: 9:16, 4:5, 1:1.
O2 Positions and sizesA slot's vertical position is a fraction of the target rectangle (4:5 or 1:1), so it lands the same way in each format. A slot's size is a cap height as a fraction of the 9:16 frame height; the pixel size is the same in 9:16 and 4:5, and 0.8× in 1:1. Widths are a maximum fraction of frame width. Text is centred unless the slot says otherwise. Nothing sits closer than 7 % W to a side edge.
O3 T1 artist clipFor artist content, still or clip. Slots: artist (one line; cap 10 % H is the ceiling and the size drops until the name fits 80 % W, floor 6 % H; under the floor the name breaks first / last name on two lines at 6 % H; centre at 12 % of the rect = 23 % H in 9:16), details (four lines venue · city · date · time, cap 3.1 % H, pitch 3.5 % H, centre at 31 % of the rect = 37 % H), claim (optional; cap 4 % H, centre at 88 % of the rect = 76.7 % H, max width 80 % W). The middle 30–70 % H carries no text: the face or the clip. In the 1:1 file the slots sit at 12 / 31 / 88 % of its height (the master's 28.6 / 39.3 / 71.4 % H), type at 0.8×.
O4 T2 standalone messageFor a message with no artist image. Slots: claim (centred on the rect's centre = 50 % H, cap 6 % H, line pitch 8.75 % H, max width 86 % W; a number token — a count, %, or price — may draw at 1.5× the line; headline at most two lines plus one qualifier line at 0.6×, e.g. THIS WEDNESDAY), lineup or partners (top-right, right-aligned, cap 2.6 % H, pitch 3 % H, block top at 10 % of the rect = 21.9 % H, right edge 14.3 % W in; partners draw as logos at the same height when the event has them, else the typed lineup, max 6 lines), details (bottom-left, two lines date / venue, cap 2.66 % H, centre at 88 % of the rect = 76.7 % H, left edge 14.3 % W in), logo (bottom-right, the brand kit's wordmark, 15 % W wide, same centre line as details).
O5 T3 lineup artwork editThe PSD, unchanged except its live text layers, each a slot named by its layer name. Puzzle 4th Birthday has one: PART 1 ENZO SIRAGUSA LUKE DEAN… — one line "PART n" in the coral run, then one name per line, "&" before the last name in the coral run. Style runs are kept by role (first line, the "&" token), not by character index. The logo, the script and the venue · date · time lines are pixels inside 4th bday poster NEW: the kit lists them as locked · inside "4th bday poster NEW", and the date cannot change until the designer makes it a live layer — ask once, per kit. Hidden layers (uzzle-december-lineup-1080.jpg) are ignored. Rule 14 holds whole: shorten, never shrink; flagged if it can't be shortened.
O6 Type and edgeBootzy Condensed TM Regular, all caps, white, on T1 and T2. The edge is a black stroke outside the letters, 0.28 % of frame width (3 px at 1080) on both — Matas's rule ("a black outline throughout") and what the clip overlay actually carries; the statics' soft shadow is not reproduced. For Matas: the two statics measured carry a shadow, not a stroke; say if T2 should keep the shadow instead. T3 draws whatever effects the PSD's text layer carries (the audit reads them); no stroke is added.
O7 Claim wordsA claim comes from the ladder (asset bank issue 2, A5'), lands in the claim slot in caps, names the release verbatim, and never carries a number the trigger didn't supply. Table below. "Live" and "selling fast" claims say now available / selling fast; a % claim says n% sold out; final X says FINAL X {release} TICKETS; sold out says {release} sold out. Price is rarely the message: the number token is a price only on a price rung (a release ends on a date), otherwise it's the count or %.
O8 Too longT1 and T2 slots step, never a third line. T1 claim: step 0 one line at 4 % H (≤ 36 characters); step 1 two lines at 4 % H; step 2 two lines at 3.4 % H (≤ 2 × 42). T2 claim: step 0 two lines at 6 % H (≤ 2 × 30); step 1 two lines at 5.2 % H (≤ 2 × 34). Past the last step the words are shortened in a fixed order (drop TICKETS → drop NOW → the release's number form, "3RD" for "3RD RELEASE" only when the release word is already on the frame) and the proposal says what was dropped; if it still doesn't fit, it's flagged and can't be approved (rule 14). The fit meter reads the step reached ("step 1 of 2 · 2 lines"). T3 never steps: rule 14 as written.
O9 Lineup and partnersT2's top-right slot draws the event's typed lineup (the Lab's artist rows, in their order; "& MORE" when the event says more TBA) or the event's partner logos when the kit has them. Never typed on the asset. T1's details read the event's venue, city, date and times. T3 reads the lineup from the same rows.
O10 One component per templateT1, T2 and T3 are three components in design-rules.md with the slots above. A new asset type (artist card, key-moment push) is a T1 or T2 with its claim set, not a fourth template, until a record says otherwise.

OT1 · T1 artist clip — 9:16 master, the 4:5 crop, the 1:1 re-sit

The artist name is the biggest thing on the frame and sits where the Locklead assets put it; the four detail lines keep the designer's pitch; the claim sits at the bottom of the rect so a face or a clip's action in the middle 40 % stays clear. On a clip the overlay is static for the clip's length. The 4:5 is the master cropped; the 1:1 is a second render of the same props at 0.8×, which is why it is listed as a format and not a crop.

OT2 · T2 standalone message — claim central, lineup, details, logo in the corners

The claim is the message and the biggest thing; the number token (a count, a % or a price) draws at 1.5× the line so the figure reads first, as £16.50 does on the Brighton asset. The corners carry what the asset is about — who, when, where, whose — at the designer's 14.3 % W inset. Matas's "tickets to move up in price…" is this template with a two-line headline and the day as the qualifier.

OT3 · T3 lineup artwork edit — the PSD with its one live layer

T3 is the only template where the file decides the layout. Its slots are the live text layers by name, and this PSD has one. The 1:1 is a crop here because the PSD carries no 1:1 arrangement to re-sit into: the kit says so ("No 1:1 artboard in the file — ask the designer", as B7b). The date on this artwork is not editable: the kit shows the date line as a locked chip with the smart object's name, and the first proposal for a date change on this kit asks the designer once.

OT4 · Size steps — the claim slot when the words are long

Steps are tried in order and the first that fits wins; the fit meter names the step. Shortening follows the fixed drop order in O8 and the proposal says what went. Over the last step with nothing left to drop, the asset is flagged in brick and has no Approve. The claim slot never takes a third line; T2's qualifier line is not a third line of the claim, it's its own slot.

Invented in this record

  • Rounding of the measured sizes to the rules: artist 10 % H (measured 9.5–11.5), details 3.1 / 3.5, claim 4 % H, T2 claim 6 % H and 8.75 % pitch, corner inset 14.3 % W, number token 1.5×, qualifier 0.6×. The 7 % W side margin.
  • Positions as fractions of the target rectangle (12 / 31 / 88 % for T1; 10 / 50 / 88 % for T2) and the 1:1 as the 4:5 arrangement at 0.8× rather than a crop: the designer's sizes do not fit a 1:1 crop (the artist name's top sits above the 1:1 line), so the choice was scale or re-lay; scale keeps the look.
  • The stroke width 0.28 % W (3 px at 1080) on T1/T2: read from the UGC overlay's ≈ 2 px at 830 wide; the UGC caption's own stroke measured 2 px at 720, so this is the heavier of the two, and not the statics' shadow. O6 asks Matas to confirm.
  • The artist slot's shrink-to-width (ceiling 10 % H, floor 6 % H, then two lines): the Locklead name is 8 letters; ENZO SIRAGUSA is 13 and would not fit one line at 10 % H even in Bootzy.
  • The step ladder (two steps, the 15 % size drop, the character limits per line) and the drop order (TICKETS → NOW → number form).
  • The claim words in the ladder table; the "release ends" rung.
  • The T3 style-run rule (by role, not index) and the "ask the designer once" behaviour for locked details.
  • The stand-in font in these frames (Impact / Arial Narrow with cap ≈ 0.72 em) and the gradient backgrounds; nothing about Bootzy's metrics is known until the audit (H8 e).
  • The PSD canvas size ≈ 3375 × 6000 (from the 25 % ruler); the details' cap 1.6 % H.
