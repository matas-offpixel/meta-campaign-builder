Off/Pixel · design record · creative testing · variant pairs · 18 November 2026

Variant pairs: two versions of one asset, tested against each other

Matas, 18 Nov: "Make it general, not snippet-only." A variant pair compares two versions of the same asset, each with an operator label:

  • two windows of one track;
  • two cuts of one clip;
  • two framings of one card.

It runs as its own ad set in the show's {code} · Tests campaign for four days, and it's called by TF2's read.

The first use is P26-BDAY's Luke Dean snippets. Can't Decide and Get Hard each have an "earlier drop" window and a "payoff drop" window. The two pairs run at the same time, as two ad sets, and go in live at Approve once Matas has signed them off (VP7).

The test maker can't make a pair today:

  • it takes each track once;
  • it takes one window per track, from the drop CT4 uses;
  • it labels a variant with the track's title, so both windows would read "Can't Decide";
  • none of CT6's tests compares two versions of one asset.

This record adds that test. Everything else stands:

  • Snippets and audio: CT1's format, CT2 with AU1–AU4, CT4 as amended by #1338's TK3 (every drop found, the last qualifying one used), and CT5.
  • The plan: CT6's default plan, and the money and calling rules TF1–TF4.
  • Campaigns: TT8–TT11.
  • Lab edits: the Lab's video-window rules (VW).
  • Approve: the cap sentence ruled on #1297.

Matas's rulings, 18 Nov: sign-off is Matas's, and Approve stays off until it's recorded (VP7); P26-BDAY's two pairs run at the same time, as two ad sets (VP4); the winner is TF2's read, unchanged (VP5).

The P26-BDAY windows and Drive files are real, from the Luke Dean session's table. Can't Decide's length (2:55) and its payoff drop (2:07.5, where the bass returns after the 2:04 break) are from the Luke Dean session too. The other drop positions, Get Hard's length, the static's name, the clip and card examples and every figure in the frames are invented.

Rulings

#Ruling
VP1 · A variant pair
  • A test of exactly two variants: two versions of one asset. One thing differs between them, and everything else is held.
  • What a pair always holds: the copy, the audience, the placements, the optimisation, the page it runs from and the budget. Both variants are ads in the pair's one ad set (VP4), with the same primary text, headline and link, so only the asset differs.
  • Three kinds. "Same asset" means:
    KindSame assetWhat changesAlso held
    Track pairOne track file in 02 Source / Audio / {artist}, as an audio snippet static (CT1)The window: where in the track the snippet startsThe static, the length (15–20 s, 16 by default), the fades and the loudness
    Clip pairOne Lab clip (creative_lab_clips), as a Lab video editThe cut: where the edit starts and ends in the clip, under VW's rules (15–20 s, the drop 3–5 s in)The caption or overlay, its style and its placement, and the 9:16 framing
    Card pairOne card: the same photo or cutout with the same wordsThe framing: the crop, or where the photo sits on the cardThe words, the type, the colours and the format
  • Each variant has an operator label, shown on the event page, the share link and in the result (VP6). The defaults:
    • Track pair: "{Track} · earlier drop" and "{Track} · payoff drop".
    • Clip pair: "{clip} · cut 1" and "{clip} · cut 2".
    • Card pair: "{name} card · framing 1" and "{name} card · framing 2".
    {Track} is the variant name CT11 already uses: the Spotify title, else the file's name. {clip} is the clip's name in the Lab. {name} is the card's artist or title. The variants are ordered by start, earlier first (for a track or a clip), or in the order the operator added them (for a card).
  • Changing a label: an operator can change either label until Approve. After Approve the labels are fixed, because the reads, the result and the share link carry them. A label can't be empty, the two can't be the same, and each is at most 60 characters.
  • Limits: never two different assets, never more than one thing changed, never more than two variants.
VP2 · Making a track pair
  • In the Lab's Snippet tab, on the track that's open (CT10): a "Pair" switch above the waveform turns the one window into two boxes, one per variant. Each box is titled with its label.
  • Markers: each box has its own drop marker, the brick line with "DROP · {m:ss}" from CT4.
    • The later box takes the drop CT4 uses: with #1338's TK3, the last qualifying drop, the last third searched first.
    • The earlier box starts with no marker. The waveform already shows every drop CT4 found (TK3), and the operator picks one of them or places the marker by hand. The Lab never fills the second box by itself.
  • Two ways to set a window:
    • A start time. Type it in "Starts at" (m:ss, up to two decimals: 2:03.5, 1:46.85), or drag the box. Dragging the marker moves the box with it, as in CT4.
    • A snippet file. "Window from" lists the snippets the app has already made from this track's file. Picking one copies its start and length.
      • Files cut outside the app aren't listed, because the app doesn't know their window or their loudness. P26-BDAY's four in Luke Dean / Statics are files like that.
      • Their starts are typed instead.
  • The rule for each window:
    • 15–20 s long, with its drop 3 to 5 s from the start. That's VW2's shape, and CT1's default of 4 s sits inside it.
    • CT4's "past the first 20 seconds" is where the Lab looks, not a limit on the operator: an earlier drop placed by hand can sit in the first 20 s.
    • The two windows can't overlap.
  • Each box's line: "{start}–{end} · {length} s · drop {d} s in", for example "2:03.5–2:19.5 · 16 s · drop 4.0 s in".
    • Times keep up to two decimals when they aren't whole.
    • {d} has one decimal.
  • Preview: CT5's preview plays the box touched last.
  • The button: "Make pair · {Track}". It renders both snippets on the chosen static ("Static: {name}", CT10).
    • Names follow CT1's S14 pattern, with the first variant as _r1 and the second as _r2, as P26's files were filed by hand.
    • They file under Videos (CT10), never overwriting (_v2).
VP3 · Making a clip pair or a card pair
  • A clip pair: in the clip's edit window picker (VW5), a "Pair" switch gives two windows on the one clip, each held to VW's rules.
    • "Window from" lists the clip's own approved video renders. Picking one copies its window.
    • The caption, its style and its placement are taken once and applied to both cuts.
  • A card pair: on a card, "Pair" makes a second framing of the same card.
    • The operator moves the photo or the crop on the second. Changing the words or the type is refused, because that would be a different card.
    • "Framing from" lists the card's own approved renders.
  • Each kind has its own button: "Make pair · {clip}" or "Make pair · {name} card". Both renders go through the Lab's usual render and approval (CT10: "A snippet is approved like any render", and so is a cut or a card).
  • This record rules the words and the rules for clip pairs and card pairs; their frames come with their build. The first build is the track pair (P26-BDAY). The clip and card makers follow in their own PRs, each shooting its own frame against this record.
VP4 · The ad set
  • A pair runs as its own ad set in the show's one {code} · Tests campaign (TT8), found by its exact name and never made twice. It has no campaign budget.
  • Ad set name: "{code} · Tests · {kind} pair · {d Mon}", where {kind} is Track, Clip or Card (TT10, extended).
    • For example "P26-BDAY · Tests · Track pair · 18 Nov".
    • A same-day repeat of the same kind adds " · 2", as TT10 says: "P26-BDAY · Tests · Track pair · 18 Nov · 2". {d Mon} is the London day the ad set is made.
  • Ads: the two variants are its two ads, named by the render's file name.
  • Four days: the ad set ends at 23:59 London on the fourth day after the London day it starts (TT9).
  • Lane: pairs run in a lane of their own, as A/B copy and artist-card tests do (TT9).
    • A pair doesn't wait on CT6's tests (Tracks, Statics, Together), and they don't wait on it.
    • Pairs run at the same time (Matas, 18 Nov): each pair is its own ad set, and one pair never waits on another. Each holds against the cap with every other test ad set (VP8).
    • The app follows each ad set by its stored id (TT9).
VP5 · Called by TF2's read
  • The result is the campaign's own (R3): checkouts, else landing-page views, or signups on a signup campaign.
  • TF2's read, unchanged (Matas, 18 Nov): daily from 48 h, with the last read at day 4. For a pair, that means:
    • A variant wins with at least 60 % of the spend, at least 3 results, and no cheaper judged variant.
    • A variant under 10 % of the spend isn't judged. It reads "Not enough spend to judge" and never wins.
    • With no winner at day 4, the pair reads "No clear winner by day 4".
  • Two pairs at once are read separately. Each ad set's read is its own, and one pair's winner says nothing about the other's.
  • "Called" means Matas calls the winner once TF2's read finds one, as for every creative test ("Ready to call", then "Winner: …").
  • Calling changes nothing else. The track's stored window, the clip's stored window and the card's stored framing stay as they were. A later edit takes the winning version only when an operator moves it there.
VP6 · The event page, the share link and the result
  • The card's name: "Test {n} · {kind} pair · {asset}", on the Test plan panel and on the share link. {asset} is the track, the clip or "{name} card". For example "Test 1 · Track pair · Can't Decide" and "Test 2 · Track pair · Get Hard".
  • At 1280, internal only: a line under the name saying what changes and what's held.
    • A track pair: "{span 1} vs {span 2} · Static: {name}".
    • A clip pair: "{span 1} vs {span 2}".
    • A card pair: "Framing 1 vs framing 2".
    It's not shown at 393.
  • Rows: the two labels. TF1's line names the ad set in full (TT10). TF2's states and TF3's share-first rows don't change.
  • The result reads with the label: "Winner: {label} · {share} % of spend · £{cost} per {result}" on the panel, and "Winner: {label} · £{cost} per {result}" on the share link, as TF-A and TF-C word them.
  • The share link: the card's name, its state, the labels, results and cost per result.
    • CT11's list of what the share link never shows stands.
    • The span line and the sign-off lines (VP7) aren't shown either.
VP7 · Sign-off, then live at Approve
  • Sign-off is Matas's (Matas, 18 Nov). His yes approves an asset to run from a promoter's or venue's page. This record applies that rule to pairs.
    • Until he has signed a pair off, Approve is off: "Waiting for sign-off from Matas."
    • Only Matas can record it, with "Sign off" on the pair. It then reads "Signed off by Matas · {d Mon}", the London day he recorded it.
  • A pair that runs from the artist's page needs a separate artist-page approval as well. Matas records it when he gives it, with "Record artist-page approval".
    • Until then Approve stays off: "Waiting for artist-page approval."
    • Once recorded: "Artist-page approval recorded by Matas · {d Mon}".
    • The page a pair runs from is held between its two ads (VP1), so one approval covers both.
  • Then live at Approve (TT9). "Approve variants" creates the ad set and its ads ACTIVE (createPaused: false), through #1297's route, behind CREATIVE_TESTS_CREATE_ENABLED and "Only Matas can approve a test's variants.", with TF1's budget and VP4's ad set. There's no paused step and no separate "Go live".
  • Where it shows: the sign-off lines are kept on the test, next to the labels. They show on the pair in the Lab and on the event page card at 1280 (internal only), never on the share link. They can't be changed after Approve.
  • Approve checks VP2's and VP3's rules again and refuses with the same words (VP9). Nothing is made.
  • No Approve control on the event page: it has none yet. "Sign off", "Record artist-page approval" and "Approve variants" sit on the pair in the Lab, beside "Make pair" (frame VP-A).
VP8 · Money
  • One budget per pair: TF1 sizes each pair's ad set like any creative test's: 10 % of the event's current daily Meta spend, rounded down, at most £10 a day and the median live main ad set's budget, never under Meta's minimum. Both ads share it, and TF2 reads the spend share.
  • The budget always comes from TF1, never a fixed £10. £10 a day is TF1's cap, not its amount: a show with a small daily spend gets a small test budget.
  • The hold: the daily budget × 4 days, for the one ad set. It's not per variant. A pair at £1.00 a day holds £4.00.
  • R5: before anything is made, the hold is checked against the 20 % cap, together with what every other test ad set of the event holds now (approved or running). That includes another pair running at the same time, and a CT6 test.
  • The cap sentence: #1297's sentence stands word for word, shown only when the pair doesn't fit, with Approve off: "This test needs £{hold} for its 4 days, and the plan's 20 % cap has £{left} left."
VP9 · The refusals

At "Make pair" and again at Approve. Nothing is made when one shows.

  • "Place the second drop." (a track pair)
  • "{label}: the drop is {d} s in. Move the window so the drop is 3 to 5 s in." (a track or a clip)
  • "The two windows overlap. Move one so they don't." (a track or a clip)
  • "The two framings are the same. Move one." (a card)
  • "A card pair keeps the card's words and type. Change only the framing." (a card)
  • "Give each version a label."
  • "The two labels are the same. Change one."
  • "Waiting for sign-off from Matas." (Approve only, VP7)
  • "Waiting for artist-page approval." (Approve only, an artist-page pair, VP7)
VP10 · Never
  • A pair never compares two different assets, never changes more than one thing, and never has more than two variants.
  • It never changes the copy, the audience, the placements, the page or the budget between its two ads.
  • It never takes a file cut outside the app as a variant, and never moves or deletes those files. P26-BDAY's four stay in Luke Dean / Statics.
  • It never fills the second window, cut or framing by itself, and never picks either version without an operator.
  • It never changes a track's, clip's or card's stored window or framing because a pair won.
  • It never goes live without Approve, never before Matas's sign-off, and never from an artist's page before the artist-page approval.
  • Only Matas records a sign-off or an artist-page approval.
  • It never shows the span line, the sign-off lines, budgets or spend share on the share link.
  • It never changes a label or a sign-off after Approve.
  • It never makes a campaign beyond TT8, nor an ad set beyond VP4's.

Acceptance

  • What a pair is: one asset, one thing changed, exactly two variants.
    • A track pair holds the file, the static and the length.
    • Copy, audience, placements, page and budget are the same for both ads.
  • Labels: they default to VP1's strings, can be changed until Approve, and are fixed after it. They show on the event page, the share link and in the result.
  • Track windows: set by a typed start or by one of the app's own snippets of that file. Files cut outside the app are never offered. The later box takes CT4's drop and the earlier one is the operator's. Each drop is 3 to 5 s in, the windows never overlap, and the renders are _r1 and _r2.
  • The ad set: its own, in {code} · Tests, named "{code} · Tests · {kind} pair · {d Mon}" with TT10's " · 2". It ends at 23:59 London on day 4. Two pairs run at the same time, each read on its own.
  • The call: TF2's read and words, unchanged.
  • The hold: TF1's daily budget × 4 per pair, never a fixed £10, checked against the 20 % cap with every other test ad set. P26-BDAY's two pairs hold £8.00. #1297's cap sentence is word for word.
  • Sign-off and Approve: Approve is off with "Waiting for sign-off from Matas." until Matas records "Signed off by Matas · {d Mon}", and for an artist-page pair until he records the artist-page approval. Only Matas records either. Approve then creates everything ACTIVE through #1297's route, gate and Matas check.
  • The refusals: VP9's are exact.
  • Frames: VP-A, VP-B and VP-C are shot at 1280 and 393, with the expected figures above.

Build notes

  • The kind: migration 255's creative_tests.kind check allows tracks, statics, together, copy and clip only. A pair kind, with the pair's kind (track, clip or card), its sign-off and its artist-page approval kept in held, needs one migration that widens that check. It's unapplied; Matas applies it.
    • Storing a pair as kind tracks instead would avoid the migration. But then a later Statics test would take the pair's winner as its winning track, so the build doesn't do that.
  • Everything else fits the existing columns:
    • creative_lab_snippet_renders.start_s and length_s are per render already;
    • creative_test_variants.label is per variant already.
  • Code:
    • makeTest1 stays as it is. A pair has its own maker in lib/creative-tests/make-test.ts.
    • canApprove leaves pairs out of the plan's order, lets pairs run beside one another, and checks the sign-off (and the artist-page approval for an artist-page pair).
    • TEST_AD_SET_KINDS gains "Track pair", "Clip pair" and "Card pair".
  • First build: the track pair only. Clip and card pairs come in their own PRs.
