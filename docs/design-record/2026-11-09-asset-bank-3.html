#Ruling
A12 A11 — yesAn approved warning is a promoter claim with its trigger recorded. The caption validator accepts a caption that repeats an approved, live claim's words for that release ("90% sold out", "final 50") while the claim is live; everything else keeps the strict rule. Built in #1114 as H4' / H11; the test is the acceptance. The deck's X2 closes with this row.
A13 The window is the code'sThe payday window is isInPaydayWindow: 18 days inclusive from the Thursday before the last Friday — for November, Thu 26 Nov – Sun 13 Dec. Issue 1's AB3 figure shaded 14 Dec and its H6 acceptance named it; both are superseded by this row and by the AB3 baseline at 22088967. The calendar draws the code's rule and never redefines it (issue 1's data note stands).
A14 The roll-call rungA rung on every client's ladder, seeded. Trigger: two or more releases in state sold_out and exactly one on sale or selling fast. Claim: two lines — line 1 the sold_out releases in position order, joined with commas and "&", the word RELEASE once at the end unless a release's name carries its own ("EARLY BIRD, 1ST, 2ND & 3RD RELEASE SOLD OUT"); line 2 the live release's current claim from its own rung ("FINAL RELEASE RUNNING LOW", "FINAL 100 FINAL RELEASE TICKETS"). The words are generated from event_releases; the approval card shows both lines. Preferred: when its trigger is met the roll-call is proposed ahead of a plain % rung for the same release. Line 1 draws in the kit's second colour, line 2 white (O12); on the card the two lines read as two lines. Puzzle's four releases at sold out · sold out · on sale · selling fast meet the trigger only if one of the last two is not on sale — the fixture's state in AB2 is sold out · sold out · sold out · selling fast.
A15 Four derived rungsAdded below the sold-out rung, seeded, each with its trigger column. Sales velocity: "1,000+ TICKETS SOLD IN THE LAST 2 WEEKS" — trigger: sold(now) − sold(now − window) ≥ threshold, window and threshold per client (defaults 14 days, 500 — Invented); needs two counts, locked without them, the card names the two reads. Price increase on a date: "PRICES RISE {DAY}" with the qualifier "LAST CHANCE AT £{n}" — trigger: the next release's list time within n days (default 4, as A7's price-rise window); the price only when the current release carries one, else the qualifier is dropped. Countdown + %: "LESS THAN 2 WEEKS TO GO" / "OVER 90% SOLD OUT" — trigger: days to show ≤ n and the % rung's trigger met (or the state rung that stands in for it); the two lines are the claim. Lineup addition: "{ARTIST} (JUST ANNOUNCED)" — trigger: a new row in the event's typed lineup since the last proposal; it refreshes the lineup slot (floating kit) and every headliner card's ALONGSIDE: list (frame kit) rather than making a new asset. A derived rung never carries a number its trigger didn't supply (rule 11, O7).
A16 Offers — entered, not derivedThree offer claims exist and no trigger makes them: a payment plan ("£{n} TODAY · SPLIT THE REST IN {n}"), a group ticket ("4 FOR 3 · GROUP TICKETS"), an early-entry price ("£{n} EARLY ENTRY" with the qualifier "ARRIVE BEFORE {time}"). Matas enters an offer on the event (the Offers sheet: kind, the figures, the condition, from / until), and it becomes a proposal like any rung. Its approval card carries the marker entered, not derived beside the claim and names who entered it and when; the card has no trigger line because there is none. An offer is not scarcity: it never says sold, final, last or % and the validator treats its words as a price (£ must equal a tier or offer price). Offers are not on the ladder (they are not claims about truth) — they live on the event's Offers sheet and on the board as their own row.
A17 Cancelled eventsAn event whose status is cancelled shows no releases panel, no ladder, no proposal card and no Today row; the board's ticket-warning rows read "cancelled · nothing to make". Its event_releases rows are kept (history), never deleted. Uncancelling restores the panel as it was. The page header already carries the status word (pass 2 A4); nothing else says it twice.
A18 Backfilled rows read plainA release row whose state came from the backfill (source entry, no state_set_by) draws its state control with the derived segment outlined, not filled, no "set {date} · {source}" line, and the sub-line "from the event's dates · tap to confirm". It makes no proposal and counts in no panel number until Matas taps a segment — the first tap saves his entry and the row reads like any other. The Puzzle rows are his already. The 174 backfilled events read this way until touched; Today does not list them.
