Rulings

#Ruling
E1 · Who we reached

Two kinds of figure, kept apart. Proposal:

  • Paid audience (the platforms' own):
    • Meta: age, gender and region per campaign-day, with results by age: spend, impressions and share, CTR, signups (complete registrations), cost per signup, purchases, cost per purchase and revenue. This is the report's "Meta results by age" table, per show and per client.
    • TikTok: age, gender and UK nation with spend, impressions, clicks and CTR only. TikTok doesn't report results by age reliably, so the table says "TikTok reports clicks by age, not signups or sales".
  • First-party (CIRQLIN, from the signups themselves):
    • Signup geography from geo_country, geo_region and geo_city on each signup: the top cities with counts, "Everywhere else", and "Couldn't tell" always shown, never spread across the others.
    • Per show, the share from the venue's own region against the rest. For example, across Electric's pages London (1,157) out-signs Newcastle (927).
    • Age only when the page's form asks for it. Today none do, and the panel says so.
  • Never blended. Meta's age is Meta's modelled audience, CIRQLIN's city is where the signup arrived from. They sit side by side, each labelled with its source: "Meta says" and "from your signups".
  • Privacy floor. A city shows by name only with 10 or more signups. Smaller cities fold into "Everywhere else". No person-level row ever appears on this surface.

Invented: the home-region-vs-travelling split, the 10-signup floor, and the TikTok sentence.

E2 · Signups & communities

The three-step funnel, each step naming its source. Proposal:

  1. Email signups. CIRQLIN signups on the show's linked page (D9) when it has one. Otherwise the Mailchimp event tag's member count, labelled "Mailchimp tag, today's subscribers". "Still subscribed" comes from Mailchimp.
  2. WhatsApp opt-ins. CIRQLIN signups whose consent snapshot includes WhatsApp and who left a phone number. This is cross-checked against the show's CQ-{slug} Bird list, and when the two differ by more than 2 %, both are shown.
  3. Community members. WhatsApp communities have no API, so the count is entered by hand per show, with its date. That's how the report got it.
    • CIRQLIN's own "Community" figure is taps on the confirmation page's community link, so it shows as "community link taps", never as members.
    • Electric's taps are 623 against 12,387 signups.
  • Join rate: members ÷ WhatsApp opt-ins for that show (the report's "Opt-in → community").
  • Cost per signup, two figures that are never merged:
    • All paid: signup-phase spend, all channels (D12) ÷ CIRQLIN signups.
    • Ad-tracked: registration-objective spend ÷ the platforms' complete registrations.
    Ironworks shows why both matter: £0.85 for all paid vs £1.34 ad-tracked.
  • Consent and partner opt-ins: CIRQLIN's consent snapshot counts per channel (email, SMS, WhatsApp), plus a partner opt-in count where the form carries one.

Invented: the 2 % cross-check line and the "community link taps" label.

E4 · Creative

One score source: creative_scores, the same scores the Lab and the on-sale refresh read. Proposal:

  • Format scorecard, judged by objective. Signups count only inside signup campaigns (cost per signup), and purchases only inside purchase campaigns (cost per purchase). A cost shows only where £500 or more ran, as the report does. A format is the creative's S14 angle (UGC edit, static artwork, promo/motion, ticket warning, presale reminder).
  • AI-tag filters: asset type, visual format, hook and messaging angle, with the counts per tag, and "match every selected tag".
  • Top 10 by signups and top 10 by sales, each tile with spend, the result, its cost, impressions, clicks, CTR, tags and "View ad".
  • Least impact: £150 or more spent, and the highest cost per result for its objective.
    • When the on-sale refresh is on, the list is exactly the refresh's pause candidates (R2: 2× the campaign's cost per result, 7 days live), so the two never disagree.
    • Each row links to its refresh line.
  • TikTok creatives: spend, impressions, clicks, CTR, 2-second views and completion (full views ÷ 2-second views), ranked by spend.
  • Links: a creative row opens its Lab render (by drive_file_id) and its Drive file. No score is recomputed on this surface.

Invented: tying "least impact" to the refresh's R2 when the refresh is on.

E5 · Direct-to-fan

Proposal:

  • WhatsApp (Bird): per broadcast, sent, delivered, read, clicked and click rate. Opens are named "read", because they're read receipts.
  • Email (Mailchimp): per campaign, delivered, opens, clicks and click rate.
    • Opens use Mailchimp's bot-filtered rate where its report gives one. Otherwise they're estimated with this client's own ratio, from sends that have both, and marked "est.".
    • A raw open rate (Apple Mail Privacy and scanners included) is never shown as the open rate.
    • Marketing and ticket-holder sends are split.
  • Clicks are the like-for-like comparison. The headline compares click rates ("WhatsApp 13.4 % vs email 3.8 %"), never open rates across channels.
  • Channel cost per click:
    • WhatsApp: delivered × the client's Bird per-message rate (config), or the invoice when entered.
    • SMS: entered.
    • Email: "—", because the plan cost isn't per send.
  • Per show: the broadcasts carrying the show's tag or list. A send to the whole list shows under the client, not under every show.

Invented: "read" as Bird's open word, the per-message rate in config, and putting whole-list sends at client level.

E6 · Objective split and budget

Proposal:

  • Spend and results by objective × channel. The objectives are Registration, Purchase, Traffic, and Awareness & engagement. Each row's result is its objective's own metric (registrations, purchases, landing page views, reach). Cost per result and ROAS are shown only where the objective is purchase.
  • Budget: the plan (events.budget_marketing, or a client season budget), paid spend (D12), credits (entered, such as a Google promo credit), D2C distribution (E5's costs) and sub-budgets (afterparties). Variance is plan − paid + credits. The total marketing cost is paid + sub-budgets + D2C.

Invented: the variance formula as written, and the season budget as a client-level entry.

E7 · Client-visible vs internal, and the season report

Client-visible: everything in D1–D13 and E1–E6 about the client's own shows, including D2C costs and budget.

Internal only:

  • any cross-client benchmark or comparison;
  • Off/Pixel's fees;
  • D's audit and review lists;
  • the "Not linked" pill;
  • raw error text;
  • person-level rows.

A season report becomes a saved, dated view of the dashboard, not a separate build.

  • A saved view stores the client, shows and period, the computed figures frozen at a date, and written learnings (entered text, Off/Pixel's words, client-visible).
  • It's served behind its own share token, and the existing /share/season/[token] route renders it.
  • The frozen figures never change after saving. "Data as of {date}" heads it.

Invented: frozen figures plus learnings as the saved view's shape.

E8 · Build pieces, after D's HD1–HD5
  1. HE1 · Audience breakdowns (Claude Code): Meta age, gender and region results per campaign-day, and TikTok's audience report, stored per show. Read-only from the platforms.
  2. HE2 · CIRQLIN first-party read (Claude Code): per page, signups by geography and consent channel, phone present, and community link taps. Read server-side from CIRQLIN, aggregated with the 10-signup floor.
  3. HE3 · Direct-to-fan ingest (Claude Code): Bird broadcast stats and Mailchimp campaign reports per show and client, with the bot-filtered rate where given. Read-only. Bird deletes nothing (standing rule).
  4. HE4 · Presale log (Claude Code + Cursor): the entered fields in E3, with who and when; the derived figures; and the community member entries (E2).
  5. HE5 · Creative section (Cursor, reading HD3): the E4 surfaces on creative_scores, linked to the Lab and the refresh.
  6. HE6 · Objective and budget (Claude Code + Cursor): E6.
  7. HE7 · Saved views (Claude Code + Cursor): E7, then Ironworks Season 1 re-made as the first saved view. Its numbers should match the hand-built report, except where a figure was entered.

Order: HE1–HE3 together, then HE4–HE6, then HE7. Frames EV1–EV4 at 1280 and 393, internal and share.
