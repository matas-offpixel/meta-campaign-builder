Overlay templates — issue 5: T2 round 2

Extends issues 1–4. Claude Code's round-2 T2 drafts (cc/t2-claim-statics at 09a5554, H27–H30 built) showed that O26's "ink box" includes Puzzle's tape strips, so a line can still leave the paper — the thing O26 was written to stop. This issue moves the slot to the paper, adds a third size step so O12's own roll-call fits, rules the build's new invented values, and sets the drop order on T1 and the artist card. The video questions (O31) move to issue 6. The round-2 sheets were seen: round-2/SHEET-9x16.png (ink-box slot) and round-2/paper-box/SHEET-9x16.png (paper slot).

Rulings

#Ruling
O32 The slot is the paperAmends O26. The claim sits on the panel's paper, not its ink box: tape, torn strips and shadow are not ground, and a letter on the tape reads as a mistake. Ops' lean is right.
  • The paper box is measured once per kit at the kit audit (H8) and stored on the kit; the build's method (each edge the median over the rows or columns with ink) is accepted as the measurement. Puzzle: x 94–970, y 373–1548.
  • Inset 0.03 W (32 px) each side (was 0.04 W) and 0.02 H (38 px) top and bottom. Puzzle's slot: x 126–938 (812 px), y 411–1510. The 1:1 at 0.8× (650 px).
  • Claim lines centre on the slot's middle (Puzzle x 532), not the frame's.
  • Why 0.03 W: it keeps a clear 32 px of paper beside the widest line — the record's own sample (b) at 795 px fits with 17 px to spare — while 0.04 W made (b) unfittable by 4 px. Tracking is never tightened to fit (rule 14: never condensed; the kit's +25 stands).
O33 T2 step 2Amends O8 for T2. O12's own example roll-call ("EARLY BIRD, 1ST, 2ND & 3RD RELEASE SOLD OUT") can't fit the paper at step 1, and a template that can't draw its own record's claim is short a step.
  • T2 gains step 2: two lines at 4.5 % H (86 px; a roll-call's line 1 at 0.625 of it, 54 px), the equal-cap pitch scaled with the cap (6.56 % H). Never a third line.
  • The ladder on T2 is step 0 → 1 → 2, then each drop in O8's order, retrying steps 0–2 after each. The meter reads "step {n} of 2".
  • Predicted on the 812 px slot (the build's measured widths decide): (a) step 0; (b) step 1, 795 px; (c) longest step 2 with TICKETS dropped (about 766 / 629 px); the six-release fixture still can't shorten (about 990 px at step 2).
  • Hierarchy holds: at step 2 the claim's white line (86 px) is still the largest text on the card and the red line (54 px) still larger than the lineup's 50 px.
O34 The build's new invented values
  • Accepted: claim lines centre on the slot's middle; a slot wider than 86 % W narrows about the panel's centre.
  • Accepted: a claim whose lines fit but whose block is taller than the slot meters "{block} of {slot} px tall · can't shorten".
  • Accepted for drafts and the flagged preview only: a state that doesn't fit draws its lines cut at the slot's edges, with the fit meter in brick and no Approve. It is never filed or exported.
  • Accepted, with one more name: no break inside a release name — any word followed by RELEASE, EARLY BIRD, GENERAL SALE, the event's own release names — and never between FINAL and its number ("FINAL 100").
  • Accepted: two O25 stops less than a pixel apart share one 3 × 3 reading, which passes when it lies between their two values ±1.
O35 Drops on T1 and the artist cardO8's shortening order — drop TICKETS → drop NOW → the number form, ordinals only ("3RD RELEASE" → "3RD"; "FINAL RELEASE" is never shortened to "FINAL") — applies on T1 and on the artist card after their last size step (T1: O8's step 2; the artist card: O23's floors), before "can't shorten" or "Claim too long for the artist card". A roll-call's line 1 is never shortened (O27); line 2 takes the drops. The fit meter and the approval card name what was dropped. #1156's H28 build is this rule.
O36 The draft stampA draft carries one pill, "DRAFT · claim not approved" or "DRAFT · sample claim", in the 4:5 rectangle's top-left corner inside the 76 px inset, clear of every slot, the lockup and the bands' text. Drafts only: an approved, filed or exported asset never carries it. The three diagonal bands are withdrawn.
