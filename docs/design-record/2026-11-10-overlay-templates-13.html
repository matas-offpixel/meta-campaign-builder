Overlay templates — issue 13: tracking on the photo ground, the feather check

#1177 builds issues 11 and 12 in real Bootzy. Matas's example fits Bootzy at tracking 0 in the experience line and the lineup. The claim doesn't fit at any honest tracking: "STUDENT TICKETS SELLING FAST" is 582 px wide at a 54 px cap in the example, while Bootzy Condensed sets it at 453 px at tracking 0 and 505 px at the kit's +25. Matching it would take about +63, or another face. #1174 also measured O70's feather check with the bands' dither, and asked whether that is right. This issue rules both. Issues 1–12 stand except where a row says otherwise.

Rulings

#Ruling
O90 Tracking on the photo groundThe experience line, the lineup and the details are all set at tracking 0 on the photo ground. That is Matas's example's own setting, read against the face. It replaces O79's +5 for the lineup and details on this ground. The panel ground keeps its live layer's +5 (issue 4).
O91 The claim's face and widthThe claim on the photo ground stays in the kit's face, Bootzy Condensed, at the kit's claim tracking of +25 (O23), with its cap at 0.50 of the experience line's (O85). Its width is whatever those words set at that size: 505 px for Matas's line, against the example's 582.
  • No horizontal scaling of a kit face, ever. It distorts the strokes, and it is an effect (O5).
  • No tracking past the kit's +25 to chase a width.
  • H86's 0.005 W check applies to the claim's centre and cap, not its width.
  • Open, for Matas: if the claim in his file is a wider Bootzy cut, or Bootzy at a horizontal scale, say which. A cut the client provides joins the kit (O51), and a later issue can give the claim that face. A horizontal scale stays out.
O92 The feather check, as shippedThis amends O70's check; the curve, the 12 px minimum and the 1.6× limit stand. The check is made on the backing layer as it ships: computed at 16 bits and dithered per O46, read as each row's mean level across the core's width, over the feather's rows only, from the last core row to the first row at 0, with the padding not read. #1174's 1.50–1.55× passes. The build's measuring with the dither is accepted as T1V_INVENTED.
