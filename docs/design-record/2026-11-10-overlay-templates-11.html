Overlay templates — issue 11: T2 on the promoter's own photos and footage

Matas, 10 Nov, gave two answers. Another promoter's or venue's branding in the footage is fine: most artist footage has it. And T2 is fine with or without a claim. It is also the template for experience-led marketing: an event-led message set straight onto the promoter's own photos and clips, with no taped panel. This issue rules that second ground for T2: its experience line, positions, contrast, faces, resolution and timing. It also withdraws O72. Issues 1–10 stand except where a row says otherwise.

Matas's example is a Puzzle 9:16 for Sat 17 Oct: a crowd photo, full-bleed, all Bootzy and white. The lineup is top right, the experience line centred, the claim under it, date and venue bottom left, and the wordmark bottom right. The example image didn't come through to the design thread. The frames below are drawn from its description at the T2 kit's own measures; H85 compares them with the example when it arrives.

Rulings

#Ruling
O75 O72 withdrawnMatas, 10 Nov: another promoter's or venue's branding in the footage (SOLID GROOVES, SPACE, amnesia) is fine to use. O72 and H73 are withdrawn: nothing is detected, flagged or refused for it, and there is no "Another name in the footage" line.
O76 T2's two grounds
  • The panel ground (issues 4–5): the kit's taped poster panel over its paint ground. It is unchanged.
  • The photo ground (this issue): the promoter's own photo or clip, full-bleed, with no panel. It is only for footage and photos whose owner is the promoter (W1), including promoter clips that feature an artist (W8). Artist-owned footage takes T1, never T2.
  • This supersedes O30's "a T2 on a panel kit always draws the panel", for promoter photos and footage only. A bare colour ground is still never a T2.
  • The crop moves the photo, never the type (O41).
O77 The experience line's wordsThe headline slot carries the experience line: what the night is, not what's on sale. One line per event and ground, kept on the asset board beside the claims.
  • Typed: Matas, or whoever runs the event, types it, and it is kept verbatim. The display is upper case, the kit's case (O23).
  • Suggested: "Suggest" has the Lab writer propose three in the promoter's voice (V-rulings: the promoter's corpus) from the event's facts only: city, venue, the night's name, the sound as the facts record has it, and the lineup.
  • The writer never writes a number, a ranking or superlative ("biggest", "best", "No. 1", "only", "first"), or a sale or scarcity word. Those are the promoter's to type, or the ladder's to claim.
  • Matas's "SOUTHAMPTON'S BIGGEST HOUSE PARTY" is a typed line.
  • Approval: it is ✓'d like a claim. Until then, a draft carries the stamp "DRAFT · experience line not approved" (O36).
  • Limits: two lines at most and 40 characters at most, with no emoji. A break typed into the line is kept. Otherwise the line breaks at the most even break among O28's allowed breaks. A line that doesn't fit two lines at the last size step can't be saved: "Too long for two lines — shorten the experience line". It is never shortened or re-worded by the Lab.
O78 Size, and the claim under it
  • Experience line: T2's steps, cap 0.06 / 0.052 / 0.045 H. The two-line pitch is O4's 0.0875 H at step 0, scaled with the cap. The width limit is 0.86 W (0.07–0.93 W) on a still and 840 px on a 9:16 clip (O43). It is white and centred.
  • The claim is optional. When the event has one (approved, or held and stamped as T2's are), it sits under the experience line:
    • cap 0.55 of the experience line's cap at its step;
    • 0.025 H from the experience line's last baseline to the claim's first cap top, scaled with the step;
    • the same width limit.
  • A roll-call takes O23's sizes at that cap: line 1 kit red at 0.625, the gap 0.29. The tiers come from O59. O8's drops apply to the claim and never to the experience line.
  • The block is the experience line plus the claim. It is centred as a whole: on 0.50 H on a still, and between the lineup and the details on a clip (O80). With no claim, the block is the experience line alone.
  • A photo-ground T2 needs an experience line or a claim. With neither: "Add an experience line or a claim".
O79 The other slots
  • Lineup: top right, right-aligned. These are the T2 kit's measures: cap 0.026 H, pitch 0.03 H, at most 6 lines, at most 0.40 W, tracking +5. The names come from the event's facts in their order, verbatim, upper case.
  • "& MORE" is the last line only when the event's facts say more acts are to be announced. It is never added otherwise.
  • Details: bottom left, two lines, date then venue: cap 0.0266 H, pitch 0.0307 H, at most 0.40 W, coral on ">" only. They are the kit's live layer verbatim where the event has one ("SUNDAY 27TH DECEMBER / CIRCUIT > SOUTHAMPTON"); otherwise "{EEE} {d} {MMM}" and the venue's name ("SAT 17 OCT / CIRCUIT").
  • Logo: bottom right, 0.15 W wide by its ink box, centred on the details' centre line. It is the kit's lockup for that event where there is one, otherwise the wordmark piece.
  • Every slot is white Bootzy (the kit's face, O24, O49), except the roll-call's red line and the coral ">". There is no stroke and no shadow (O5).
O80 PositionsRatios of the 9:16 master (1080 × 1920).
  • 9:16 still (Stories; H44's preview check stands):
    • the lineup's right edge at 0.857 W and its first cap top at 0.219 H;
    • the block centred on 0.50 H; with Matas's words (two lines at step 0 and a one-line claim) it runs 0.397–0.603 H;
    • the details at the left inset 0.143 W, centred on 0.767 H;
    • the logo's right edge at 0.857 W.
  • 9:16 clip (Reels / TikTok): everything inside O43's safe area, x 120–960 and y 269–1248:
    • the lineup's right edge at 0.889 W and its first cap top at 0.15 H;
    • the details' last baseline on 0.65 H at the left edge 0.111 W, and the logo's right edge at 0.889 W on the details' centre line;
    • the block centred halfway between the lineup's last baseline and the details' first cap top (0.342–0.547 H with Matas's words).
  • 4:5: the 9:16 still's 4:5 rectangle (O1), with the type at its 9:16 size. 1:1: O1's 0.8× re-sit, at the same fractions. Clips in 4:5 and 1:1 take the same positions (feed: no controls over the media).
  • The middle of the frame belongs to the block, so there is no face window on this template (O82).
O81 ContrastThe photo is busy behind every slot, so the photo ground carries three bands, all O25's 20 % black and stepped per band, once per photo or clip, as O57.
  • Top band behind the lineup, anchored as O55's top band: full to the lineup's last baseline + 0.02 H, then fading to 0 over 0.09 H.
  • Centre band behind the block: full from the block's first cap top − 0.02 H to its last baseline + 0.02 H, fading to 0 over 0.06 H on each side.
  • Bottom band behind the details and logo, anchored as O55's bottom band on the details' first cap top.
  • All three use J2's fade, steps 20 → 30 → 40 % (the bottom band 20 / 30 → 30 / 40 → 40 / 50 %), and O46's dithering on clips.
  • Limits (O19, O40, O61): the lineup and the details ≤ 10 % below 3:1; the block box ≤ 15 % below 3:1; the logo ≤ 15 %; the roll-call's red line ≤ 25 % below 2:1, with O58's backing (O66's shape, O70's feather) before white. These are measured on the photo, or on the p95 sample on a clip.
  • Past the last step: "Too busy behind the {lineup / experience line / details} — choose another photo or clip".
O82 Faces and resolution
  • Faces: faces at least 0.08 W wide count (O64), tested by the upper 60 % of the box (O56) against every type box, each padded 12 px. Crowd faces below the floor are ignored, as on T1.
  • On a still, a counted face may not touch type at all: the crop is tried first, else "Face in the type — choose another crop or photo". On a clip, O56's limits apply (10 % and 0.5 s), with O65's and O71's lines.
  • Resolution: a still is never upscaled (O19). The crop window must be at least the output's width, else "Photo too small for {format} — {w} px wide in the crop, needs 1080". A clip follows O63 (up to 1.6×), O68 (the render copy) and O69 (the filter).
O83 On a clip
  • Timing (O37): the lineup, details, logo, all three bands and the experience line are on from frame 0. The experience line is this template's hook, as the name is T1's.
  • The claim enters at 1.5 s with the 0.25 s fade and stays at least 3 s. A clip under 4.75 s runs without its claim.
  • Safe area on the 9:16: O43's, as O80 places it.
  • A UGC caption and this template never share a clip (O44, as for T1).
O84 Issue 10's open itemsBoth are closed by Matas's answers. Competitor branding: fine (O75). The no-claim T2 on the panel ground: fine as drawn in #1167's round 3, with the panel's middle left as paper. The panel ground may also carry an experience line in its claim slot (the paper, O32) at O78's sizes, with or without a claim.
