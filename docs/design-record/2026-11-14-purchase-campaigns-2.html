Purchase campaigns, issue 2: Meta's budget floor, reused ads, the custom conversion

This record amends 2026-11-14-purchase-campaigns.html (PC1–PC6), which stands except where it's named here. It answers the HP3 check of Meta's limits in #1239's body. That check found three things to rule: Meta refuses to lower a budget below 1.1 × what it has spent; reusing a creative across campaigns isn't documented outright; and the custom conversion's domain format hasn't been tried. It also corrects three things the first record drew wrong. No new frames: the only on-screen change is the "keeps at least" figure and its bracket.

Rulings

#Ruling
PC7 · The floor is the larger of two

Amends PC3's floor and every place that uses it: PC1 step 5, PC2's card, PC3's funding and step 5, PC4's cap and step down.

  • A campaign's floor is the larger of:
    • its spend to date plus 3 days at its last-7-day daily spend (PC3's floor), and
    • 1.1 × its spend to date (Meta's minimum for lowering a lifetime budget).
    It's rounded up to the penny.
  • It's checked before the line is offered. PC1 step 5, the £10 / £5 choice and PC4's step all use it, so the panel never offers a move Meta would refuse. It's checked again at apply, on live spend, as now.
  • It applies to the Purchase campaign too. On a step down, its own lifetime never goes below its floor's 1.1 × term (PC4 said "its own spend to date").
  • The words say which term won:
    • "{main campaign} keeps at least £{f} (spent £{s} + 3 days at £{d})" when the 3-day term is larger. That's PC3's existing form.
    • "{main campaign} keeps at least £{f} (Meta's minimum: 1.1 × £{s} spent)" when the 1.1 × term is larger.

PC8 · The floor-blocked words

PC1 and PC2 worded only the case where the 20 % cap blocks. The build added the case where the floor blocks. Those words are ruled as written:

  • PC1's "No money to start it", floor form: "No budget to start it: £5 a day to the show needs £{c}; {main campaign} keeps at least £{f} and can give £{m}."
  • PC2's £5 note, floor form: "£5 picked: £10 a day to {day month} needs £{x} from {main campaign}, which keeps at least £{f}."

The 393 forms are PC1's and PC2's, unchanged.

PC9 · Reusing the ads (step 4)

Amends PC3's "reuse the same post (its object_story_id)":

  1. First, the source ad's creative, by its creative_id. A creative belongs to the ad account, not to a campaign, and it carries the same post. Nothing new is made.
  2. If Meta refuses that, one fallback: a new creative made from the source creative's effective_object_story_id. That's the same post, so likes and comments still carry over, and copy and creative are still never edited (R5).
  3. If both fail, step 4 fails with Meta's reason and PC5's "Failed before anything went live" words. Nothing is rebuilt from the creative's parts, and no other fallback is tried.

The applied line doesn't say which path each ad took. The ledger stores it per ad.

PC10 · The custom conversion (step 1)

No show has own-domain tickets today, so the own-domain path stays behind ONSALE_REFRESH_CUSTOM_CONVERSION_CREATE_ENABLED, default off. The rule as built is accepted:

  • Event equals Purchase, and URL contains the domain.
  • The domain is exactly the string E1's read matched on, never retyped or reshaped.

Before that gate is first turned on, step 1 gains a read-back. After the conversion is made or found by name, it's read back and its rule must name that domain exactly. If it doesn't, step 1 fails with "The custom conversion's rule doesn't match {domain}" and nothing else is built. That's HC3 below. It isn't needed for the current PRs to pass, because the gate is off.
