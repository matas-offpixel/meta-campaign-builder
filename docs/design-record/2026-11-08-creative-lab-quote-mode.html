Creative Lab — quote mode: the artist's own words as the native text

A second native-text style beside the locked caption (rule 13): the artist's own words, first person, on the clip. The style is measured from the four native references (Max Cooper / Ahmed Spins) by Claude Code on 8 Nov (overlay templates record, H12). A quote is never written by the Lab: its source is a press kit, a real post, or Matas typing what the artist said. It is artist-voice, so it runs only once the artist is cleared for their own channels (pass 2, B1). Captions keep rule 13 unchanged. Frames at 1280 and 393; every value not measured is listed under Invented.

Q · Rulings

#Ruling
Q1 What a quote isThe artist's own words, first person, as the native text on a clip. Three sources and no fourth: a press kit line (from the kit uploaded to the artist's library row), a real post (a URL; the post's text is read and the words picked from it), or typed — Matas typing what the artist said, with where. The words are stored verbatim with their source (kind, URL or kit reference, date). The Lab never writes, rewrites or paraphrases a quote: there is no Write n in quote mode, no angle, no proven line. Picking a shorter passage is allowed; a cut lands only at a sentence boundary and nothing is added. Rule 5 holds: the source shows as a chip on the card (● @enzosiragusa · post, 12 Oct), never as a URL on the tile.
Q2 The quote styleMeasured on the four references and locked like rule 13. TikTok Sans, weight 700 (default) or 400 (Matas's choice per quote, the only option); white; no stroke, no shadow, no box; centred (0.499–0.506 W); the block's centre at 0.28 H of the 9:16 frame (0.275–0.285 measured), which is 0.187 down a 4:5 crop; cap height 0.0135 H (font size ≈ 0.0193 H); line pitch 0.030 H (0.029–0.031); static for the clip's length; emoji inline as the platform renders them (Q7); no quote marks and no attribution on the clip. Compared with rule 13 the quote is smaller (cap 0.0135 H against the caption's ≈ 0.020 H), higher (0.28 H against 0.355 H), looser (pitch 0.030 H against 0.030 H at a larger size, so more air per cap) and clean-edged. The block stays inside the 4:5 rect; a 1:1 crop is not a quote format (rule 13's safe-area logic holds: the block sits at 0.187 of the 4:5, inside the 1:1's 0.09–0.91 band, so a 1:1 crop keeps it — drawn, not ruled, until a reference shows one).
Q3 LinesA quote wraps at 70 % W (Invented) and runs at most four lines; the fit meter reads lines, not characters ("3 of 4 lines"). Over four lines the writer says so and asks for a shorter passage; it never shrinks (rule 14's spirit) and never edits the words. Line breaks the artist wrote (a post's own breaks) are kept.
Q4 Rights — artist-voiceA quote is the artist's voice, so it runs only when the artist's rights chip reads ✓ Cleared — artist channels too (B1). On an artist who is Promoter page only the quote card is Held with the one sentence that unlocks it — "These are Luke Dean's own words, so they run only once he's cleared for his own channels." — and the chip's action, Clear for artist channels, beside it. A held quote never enters a round and the round's count leaves it out. A Spark code does not clear a quote (B6). The Cleared tick is recorded on the quote (who, when), as on any artist-voice ad.
Q5 The writer in quote modeThe captions generator gains a Mode control, Caption · Quote, as its first control. In quote mode the row is: Mode · Artist (the lineup, each with its rights state; a held artist can be picked and the card says why it's held) · Source (press kit · a post · typed, and the words that come with it: the kit's lines to pick from, the post URL that fills the words, or the typed box). Pair with keeps its default (Best clip, the artist's own) and sits behind More. Three inputs visible (rule 3). The button is Use these words, never Write. Stage is read from the event as before; the CTA follows it.
Q6 One component, two modesThe caption overlay component (design-rules.md) gains a mode: caption renders rule 13; quote renders Q2. Everything else is shared: the clip's frame, play bottom-left, scrubber along the bottom, time bottom-right, nothing over the middle 70 % beyond the text block. Everywhere a caption sits on a clip a quote can sit: the feed post, render rows, round cells and the timeline. At cell size the words go on the label, as rule 13 does.
Q7 EmojiAllowed inline, as written in the source, rendered by the platform (never an image, never recoloured). A quote that is only emoji is refused with one sentence. Emoji count as characters for nothing; they never change the line rule.
Q8 The validatorcaption-facts.ts runs on a quote as on a caption: scarcity words the artist said ("last tickets") still need the ticket data that makes them true (rule 11), or the quote is held with the validator's sentence. Open for Matas: whether an artist's own scarcity words, quoted verbatim with their source, may run on the artist's channels without the data — proposed no, the rule is channel-blind.
Q9 393The writer's controls: Mode and Artist on one row, Source on its own line, the words box full width, More holds Pair with. The quote feed-post is one at a time, as the caption's. The held card: chip and sentence stacked, the action full width. CLQ3's side-by-side becomes a swipe pair.

Invented in this record

  • The quote words in every frame ("Southampton, this one's going to be special…", "Been waiting all year…") are placeholders written for the drawing. They are not anything Enzo Siragusa or Luke Dean said. The real words come from a kit, a post or Matas, and the fixture uses these placeholders only.
  • The wrap width (70 % W) and the four-line maximum (Q3). The references were not measured for width; four lines is the longest any of them runs.
  • The block centre is drawn at 0.28 H, the middle of the measured 0.275–0.285; the pitch at 0.030 H, the middle of 0.029–0.031. The font-size conversion (cap 0.0135 H → 0.0193 H) assumes TikTok Sans's cap ratio ≈ 0.70; H8 (e)-style measurement of the font file sets the exact figure.
  • The three source kinds and their words (press kit · a post · typed) and the "where" note on a typed quote; the sentence-boundary cut rule.
  • The held sentence's wording; the chip text quote; the button Use these words.
  • The frames use the system fallback for TikTok Sans, as pass 3's caption frames did; the render path has the real font.
  • The post copy above the media ("Enzo Siragusa on the 4th Birthday, 27 Dec at Circuit.") is a placeholder for the ad's own text.
