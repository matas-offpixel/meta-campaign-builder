The Lab ranks the drops and picks the top one

Matas's ruling, 18 November: drop detection ships as ranked candidates. The Lab auto-picks the top-ranked drop, so no operator step is needed. The picker lists the next candidates with the reason for each, and one click switches. VW14's single definition of a drop missed all four acceptance clips. The labelled study that followed (Matas marked 20 P26-BDAY clips) found no single rule that times every drop. But a ranked list has the right drop near its top on 18 of the 20.

Matas's notes on the study become rules here (VW22): wheel-ups and rewinds aren't drops, a vocal build's start isn't a drop, a drop is timed where the bass lands, and a strong crowd sing-along can beat "latest that fits". Today's measures can't yet tell the first two or the last one apart reliably. So each is ruled now and switched on once it's measured, and the study's numbers below say why.

  • Replaced: VW14's definition of a drop and VW15's choice of main drop. These become candidates, a ranking, and the top-ranked candidate as the default. VW16's picker rows also gain the rank order and a third reason, "filter opens".
  • Stands: VW2's window (the drop 3.0–5.0 s in, 15–20 s long), VW3's refusals, their order and words, VW4's nudges and "never a drop by hand", VW6, VW9, VW13 and VW17's handling of existing work, and VW12's bass band (150 Hz and below). VW3's "no sound" test stays on the whole mix.
  • Frames: VW-A is re-shot with ranked rows. VW-B stands.
  • Invented: the three gates' thresholds, the ranking's measures and weights (fitted on Matas's 20 clips), and showing three rows are all invented. The numbers below settle them on P26; a second event's labels should re-check them.

Rulings

#Ruling
VW18 · Candidates
  • Beats. Drops are found on the clip's tracked beats. A beat tracker on the kick-onset envelope follows the clip's own drift, with its tempo (100–150 BPM) as the guide. A single grid sat 0.1–0.5 of a beat off Matas's marks.
  • Kicks. Kick onsets are the 30–150 Hz level's sharp rises, against the local level over ±1 s, at least 0.1 s apart. That reads 2–4.5 kicks a second where VW14's measure read 5–7. It's the measure from the drop-features table (#1329).
  • A candidate is a tracked beat that fits VW2 (3 s or more before it, 10 s or more after it) where at least one of these holds:
    • roll stops — there are at most 0.6 × as many kicks a second over the 2 s after as over the 2 s before, and fewer;
    • mids drop — 1–4 kHz is at least 1.5 dB lower over the 1 s after than over the 2 s before;
    • filter opens — the low end against the top end (20–250 Hz against 2–16 kHz) rises at least 3 dB, 2 s after against 2 s before.
  • Stored with each candidate: its time, its reasons, its score and its rank, with the measures below as data.
VW19 · The ranking and the default
  • Each candidate is scored on seven measures of the clip's own sound. The weights, fitted on Matas's 20 marked clips, are steady across every fold:
    • the bass over the 2 s after against the clip's loudest 2 s (+2.2);
    • how much the section changes, read one beat later (+1.4);
    • kicks a second lost (+1.2);
    • the 1–4 kHz fall (−0.9: among candidates, the false ones fall more);
    • the roll restarting 1–4 s later (−0.6);
    • a dip in the kick just before (−0.5);
    • the filter opening (+0.1).
  • The top-ranked candidate is the main drop, picked at import with no operator step. The window follows VW2.
  • VW15's "latest that fits" is retired as the default. Its sense survives in the ranking: false later candidates (fills, breakdown returns) sit lower in the bass, change the section less, and their roll restarts.
VW20 · The picker
  • The picker lists three candidates, best first: the main drop, then the next two by rank. Only candidates that fit VW2 are listed. That's where recall stops rising (below): three rows have the right drop on 18 of the 20 clips, and a fourth or fifth adds none.
  • Each row reads "{m:ss.s} · {why}". {why} is one or more of "roll stops", "mids drop" and "filter opens", joined by " · ". "crowd" is reserved for when crowd response is measured (VW22). The first row starts "Main drop" and says "in use". The others offer "Use this drop".
  • Against VW16:
    • "filter opens" is new;
    • the order is by rank, not time (it replaces VW8's "main first, then time order");
    • "· doesn't fit" rows go, because only fitting candidates are listed.
    "rises {n} dB" stays retired.
  • One click switches. "Use this drop" moves the window to that candidate (VW2) and re-renders the clip's drafts. Approved renders are kept and never replaced (VW6, VW9). The switch is stored as set by hand.
  • Stands: VW4's "Drop in at", "Length" and "Back to the detected window", and VW5's summary line.
VW21 · Refusals
  • The order is VW3's: no sound, too short, no clear drop (no candidate), then drop too soon or too little after the drop. The words are VW3's.
  • A clip is refused only when no candidate fits VW2. When candidates exist but none fits, the refusal names the top-ranked one. Under 10 s after it reads "Too little after the drop", otherwise "Drop too soon".
  • On P26-BDAY's 51 clips, 50 make an edit and one is refused ("Too short for an edit", Luke Dean #10 at 9.4 s). The earlier rules refused 11–12 (VW1–VW13) and 5 (VW14). The three gates are broad, so a clip with no real drop still gets a ranked top candidate.
VW22 · Matas's four notes
  • A wheel-up or rewind isn't a drop. A candidate followed within about 8 s by a sharp collapse of the whole mix, or a rewind, is rejected. When measured: a 10 dB collapse within 0.5 s missed clip 24's false candidates (8.0 and 8.1 dB). At 7 dB it caught them, but it also rejected Promoter #6's true drop.
  • A vocal build's start isn't a drop. When the mids rise and the bass doesn't land, the beat is rejected. When measured: the 300 Hz–3 kHz rise rejected Promoter #3's true drop and missed clip 24's 0:19.9.
  • Time is where the bass lands. On the beat, never a free time. Video never sets the time (Promoter #8 drops at 0:41, not at the hands going up at 0:39). When measured: snapping to the bass level's landing put picks a beat late, so the tracked beat stands for now.
  • A strong crowd sing-along can beat "latest that fits". Promoter #9's 0:09 wins over its later drops. "crowd" is reserved as a reason. When measured: a mid-band crowd residual didn't separate it (+1.6 against +1.75 for the later drop). It needs a vocal/track split, which a separate study is running.
  • A note switches on in a later record, once its measure passes leave-one-out on the labelled clips.
VW23 · Existing work
  • As VW9, VW13 and VW17: approved renders and hand-set windows are kept. Detected windows are recomputed by the backfill's --recheck, and drafts are re-cut the next time they render.
  • Storage: the ranked candidates live in the drops column. Migration 260 renames drops_paused back to drops, and ops applies it at merge. Rows from earlier detectors read as unchecked.

Acceptance

  • Real audio: on Matas's 20 marked clips, a dry run of the backfill's detection, with the weights fitted on all 20, gives the default right on at least 13 and the right drop in the picker's three rows on at least 18. Clips 27, 29, 30 and 33 are among the fixtures, holding each candidate's measures. Leave-one-out stays the study's figure (13 and 18).
  • Refusals: across P26-BDAY's 51 clips, refusals only where no candidate fits VW2. The dry run reports the count against this record's one.
  • Synthetic tests:
    • each gate on its own makes a candidate;
    • a beat with no gate is never listed;
    • only candidates that fit VW2 are listed, at most three, best first;
    • the top one is the default;
    • "Use this drop" re-renders drafts only;
    • with no fitting candidate, the refusal names the top-ranked one in VW3's words.
  • Frames: VW-A at 1280 and 393 with ranked rows. The VW-B baseline stands.
  • Nothing is written to prod until migration 260 is applied by ops, and no --apply runs until the build is merged and design has passed it.
