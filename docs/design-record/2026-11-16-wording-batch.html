WB1 · The budget panel (HE6)

Line order: Plan, Paid, Brand campaigns (client view only), Credits, the sub-budgets, D2C, Variance, Total. Each line's note:

  • Plan: "Season budget" when a season budget is entered, else "Show budgets".
  • Paid: "Meta £{x} · TikTok £{y} · Google £{z}", naming only channels with spend. It's left out when only one channel has spend.
  • Brand campaigns: "Included in paid." when there's a figure. "No brand campaign." with "—" when there's none. The figure is inside Paid and is never added again.
  • Credits: "{credit name}. Changes the variance, not the total." When none is entered: "—", with "None entered. Changes the variance, not the total."
  • A sub-budget is its own line, by name. The figure is what the total counts: the spend when it's entered, with "Spent, of a £{allocation} allocation.", else the allocation, with "Allocation. Spend not entered yet." With no sub-budgets at all, one line, "Sub-budgets", reads "—" with "None entered."
  • D2C: "WhatsApp delivered × rate, plus SMS". When there's no WhatsApp rate, the total's footnote reads "The total excludes WhatsApp."
  • Variance: "Plan − paid + credits."
  • Total: "Paid + sub-budgets + D2C."

The column adds up to its total. Ironworks (OB3): £83,421.09 + £1,181.76 + £115.51 + £8,681.15 = £93,399.51. The variance is £83,000 − £83,421.09 + £460 = £38.91.

WB5 · The creative section (HE5)
  • Rows with nothing delivered. A TikTok row with no impressions and no spend in the period isn't listed or counted. A row with spend and no impressions, such as a billing correction, stays. No line says how many were left out (#1265).
  • Channels in the source line. This amends PH3's "channels with data": a channel counts only when it has a row that's listed. So when every TikTok row is left out, the source line names Meta alone.

    • "No TikTok creatives for this venue yet." on a venue view;
    • "No TikTok creatives yet." on a client view.
WB6 · A scope with no ticket counts (#1271)

When no show in scope has a count, nothing borrows a date from another scope:

  • Total sold, Sales and Cost per ticket read "—". Their sublines are "to date · no ticket counts yet", "since launch · no ticket counts yet" and "since launch · no ticket counts yet", or "in 7 days · no ticket counts yet" in a period.
  • On a phone, the Sales span is "no ticket counts yet" and the Cost per ticket span is "since launch".
  • The freshness line drops "Counts to …" and keeps "Spend to {day} · Signups to {day}".
  • The chart draws the spend axis only. The caption is "Left axis: spend (£).", the legend "Spend", and the Internal Count sentence is dropped.
  • The trajectory: Sold reads "—" with "No ticket counts yet", Projected reads "—", and Pace reads "—". No unit and no 14-day line.
  • The case rule: "No ticket counts yet" takes a capital when it starts a line, and is lower case after a "·".

WB8 · On-sale refresh: moving budget (#1266, #1268)

A move line, when it's capped by the floor:

  • Wide: "… · £{amount} a week (keeps {giver} at £1.00 a day) · cap £{cap} (20 % of £{remaining} remaining)".
  • Narrow: "£{amount} a week · £1.00 a day floor · cap £{cap}".
  • A move with room to spare carries no floor words.

No move, on Watch (wide · narrow):

  • "{giver} → {receiver} · no move: the giving ad set is already at the £1.00 a day floor" · "{giver} → {receiver} · no move, £1.00 floor";
  • "… · no move: the giving ad set is at £{x} a day, under the £1.00 a day floor" · the same narrow line;
  • "… · no move: the budget is set on the campaign, so ad-set budgets can't be moved" · "… · no move, campaign budget";
  • "… · no move: the giving ad set runs on a lifetime budget, so there's no daily budget to move" · "… · no move, lifetime budget";
  • "… · no move: the receiving ad set runs on a lifetime budget, so there's no daily budget to add to" · "… · no move, lifetime budget".
WB9 · On-sale refresh: new ad sets (#1266, #1268, #1269)

The start amount is the amount Apply writes.

CaseWideNarrow
From headroom"starts at £{daily} a day, {ad set}'s daily budget, from plan headroom (recent spend £{spend} a day)""starts at £{daily} a day from plan headroom"
Moved"starts at £{daily} a day, {ad set}'s daily budget, moved from {giver}""starts at £{daily} a day, moved from {giver}"
Budget not read, headroom"starts at {ad set}'s daily budget from plan headroom (recent spend £{spend} a day)""starts at {ad set}'s daily budget from plan headroom"
Budget not read, moved"starts at {ad set}'s daily budget, moved from {giver}"the same
  • Recent spend shows on the wide line only, in brackets, and only when it differs from the start. It never shows under a campaign budget.
  • The comma rule: "moved from …" describes the amount, so a comma comes before it. "from plan headroom" is part of the same phrase, so none does.
  • Under a campaign budget the line says "shares the campaign budget". When no daily-budget giver above £1.00 is left: "No budget to start it: …".
  • Only copy source on a lifetime budget: "{title} · not proposed: the ad set it would copy, {ad set}, runs on a lifetime budget, so there's no daily budget to give the new one" · "{title} · not proposed, lifetime budget".
  • Retargeting, no pixel on the copied ad set: "Builds 1 audience: video viewers, last {n} days. The ad set it copies has no pixel, so landing-page and ticket-link visitors can't be built." Here n is the video audience's own retention constant (60).
  • Blocked:
    • "The ad set it copies has no pixel and this campaign's ads have no video views, so there's no audience to build."
    • "The ad set it copies has no pixel, so there are no registrations to build from."
