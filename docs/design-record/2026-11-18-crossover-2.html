Crossover: one seed from several neighbours

This amends XO2 and XO3 of 2026-11-18-crossover.html (#1333). Checked against CIRQLIN's live read for Ewan McVicar, the two best neighbours both fail the seed rule's list floor: 19.2 % of Locklead's list of 645 signed up for Ewan, as did 16.2 % of Robbie Doherty's 685. Neither reaches 1,000, so no lookalike would ever be proposed for him. Small promoters' lists will hit this often. Ops' proposal is accepted: the strong neighbours combine into one seed.

  • Amended: XO2's seed row and XO3's line, both for the case where no single neighbour reaches 1,000.
  • Stands: the rest of XO1–XO8, including the switch, the people path (CIRQLIN to Meta, never through the app), the floors and the share words.
  • Frames: XO-E is new, at 1280 and 393.
  • Invented: the merge rule and the new words are invented, and so are XO-E's after-duplicates count and registration counts. The shares and list sizes are the live read.

Ruling

#Ruling
XO9 · The combined seed
  • When it applies. Every neighbour at 10 % or more qualifies on share, but none has a list of 1,000. Then they all become one seed together: one LOOKALIKE line, which counts as one of XO3's two a proposal. When one or more neighbours reach 1,000 alone, XO3 stands: each gets its own line, with no combining.
  • The floor moves to the combined list. CIRQLIN counts the neighbours' signups together after duplicates. The line is proposed only when that count is 1,000 or more; under 1,000, there's no seed. The app receives only the count, and CIRQLIN builds and uploads the combined list, as XO3 does for one list.
  • The words. Neighbours are named closest first, joined with "+".
    • Title: "Lookalike from {A} + {B} signups"
    • Why: "{share A} % of {A}'s list and {share B} % of {B}'s also signed up for {show}." With three: "{share A} % of {A}'s list, {share B} % of {B}'s and {share C} % of {C}'s …".
    • Writes: "Uploads {A} + {B} signups to Meta as one list · {n} people after duplicates · a 1 % lookalike in {country} · into {ad set}"
  • Switch off. XO3's page alternative combines the same way.
    • Title: "Lookalike from people who signed up on {A} + {B} pages"
    • Writes: "Registrations on {A} + {B} pages · 180 days · {n} people · a 1 % lookalike in {country} · into {ad set}"
    It's proposed only when those registrations come to 1,000 or more together. The brick line is XO3's, unchanged.
  • Not re-proposed. XO3's "already has a lookalike from that neighbour" counts a combined seed as one from each of its neighbours. If one of them later reaches 1,000 alone, it isn't offered again while the combined seed runs.
  • XO2's seed row now reads: "share_of_that ≥ 10 % and a list of ≥ 1,000: one neighbour's, or every such neighbour's combined after duplicates (XO9)".

Acceptance

  • With no neighbour at 1,000 alone, the 10 % neighbours combine into one line, but only when CIRQLIN's count after duplicates is 1,000 or more.
  • With one neighbour at 1,000, XO3's single lines appear and nothing combines.
  • The words are exact, with names closest first and joined by "+".
  • The app stores only the count: no table gains a person-level column.
  • Switch off: the pages combine, with the same 1,000 floor.
  • A combined seed blocks a later single seed from any of its neighbours.
  • Frames: XO-E at 1280 and 393.
