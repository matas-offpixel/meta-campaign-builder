Rulings

#Ruling
PH1 · Signups at presale

Amends E3's first row.

  • The count stops at the day before the presale day. A daily count can't be split at the open.
  • The figure names its last day: "Signups at presale · to Tue 4 Aug". The weekday is worked out from the date.
  • The source is CIRQLIN. When there's no CIRQLIN count on or before that day, it's the show's Mailchimp tag count for that same day, sourced "Mailchimp · {tag} · {day}". That covers a show with no page and a page that launched after the presale.
  • A CIRQLIN count of 0 stays 0. It never falls back.
  • The Mailchimp count is the latest row stored that day, and an end-of-day read wins. When the only row is a read during the day, the label names its time in the show's time zone: "to Tue 4 Aug, 13:00". A ramp-estimated row is marked "est.".
  • It's never today's count, and never a nearer day. With no row for that day, the figure is missing.

Charlotte de Witte (Ironworks) reads 12,868 from Mailchimp, "to Tue 4 Aug". The season report's 14,500 is a later figure, picked by hand.

PH2 · The rest of the presale log
  • Signup spend per presale ticket is Meta only until TikTok's campaign objective is stored. Its source reads "Meta signup spend".
  • "77.6 % of all sold" (EV2) isn't shown, because E3 doesn't list it.
  • Entered figures read "entered by {first name}, {date}", or "entered {date}" when the name isn't known.
  • Community at presale is the entered member count nearest before presale (PB4). Without one, it's missing. It's never today's all-time community_joined, until CIRQLIN gives a dated read.
PH3 · Creatives (E4)
  • Source line: "{client} · {show} · {channels} · to {day}". It names only the channels that have data in scope: "Meta", "Meta and TikTok" or "TikTok". No table names or internal tools.
  • Empty states: "Formats show once creatives are named with their format." and "No TikTok creatives for this show yet."
  • Share link: figures only. No Lab, refresh-line or Ads Manager links, until a public ad preview link is stored.
  • At 393, a top-10 row stacks as the name, then "{n} signups · £{cost} each · £{spend}" (or "purchases"), then the tags. The formats and TikTok tables scroll inside their frames.
  • Words: the lines say "purchases", and the title stays "Top 10 by sales".
PH4 · Objective by optimisation goal

Amends E6's objective split.

  • An ad set's objective is what it optimises for, whatever its campaign's objective:
    • purchases, purchase value or checkouts → Purchase;
    • registrations or leads → Registration;
    • landing page views or link clicks → Traffic;
    • everything else → Awareness & engagement.
    Ironworks' £10,740 Laylo push is Traffic, as the season report has it.
  • Cost per result shows on every row, labelled for its result: "£ / signup", "£ / purchase", "£ / landing page view". This amends E6's "only where the objective is purchase".
  • ROAS shows on Purchase rows only. It reads "—" until Meta purchase value is stored.
  • Reach is never summed across ad-days, because that counts people again. Until campaign-level reach is stored, Awareness reads "—" with no cost.
PH5 · Credits (Matas)

Confirms E6 as written, and reverses #1256's built default.

  • Total marketing cost = paid + sub-budgets + D2C. A credit isn't subtracted.
  • The credit is its own line and adjusts only the variance: plan − paid + credits.
  • Ironworks: the total is £93,399.51 and the variance £38.91, on the season report's inputs.
PH6 · Brand campaigns (Matas)

A brand campaign is "brand awareness with a sub goal of purchases or registrations" (Matas, 15 Nov). So it does sell tickets, and no single show owns it.

  • HE6: its ad sets are classified by PH4 like any other. It keeps its own "Brand campaigns" line in the client's paid total.
  • Sales dashboard, client level: brand spend counts in total spend and in cost per ticket. The total's scope line (N3) adds "includes brand campaigns (£{n})", in whole pounds. For Ironworks that's "includes brand campaigns (£22,651)", from IRWOHD's £22,651.10. This replaces the "excludes" note proposed in the #1256 verdict.
  • Sales dashboard, show level: brand spend and results never count, and nothing is said.
  • Tickets and registrations stay counted figures. Cost per ticket is client spend ÷ tickets sold, from Internal Counts or ticketing. Cost per registration uses CIRQLIN or Mailchimp signups. A brand campaign's pixel purchases or registrations are never added to those counts, because the counts already include the people they bought. Adding them would count them twice.
  • Meta's own figures (HE6's objective table) can show brand Purchase and Registration results beside the shows' results. Meta credits each conversion to one ad, so those rows don't overlap.
  • The sales calc's loader must read undated brand campaigns at client level. Today it drops events with no date.
PH7 · Years

For every date range and dated row on the dashboard, in Europe/London:

  • within the current year, no year;
  • a range across years names both: "1 Dec 2022 – 13 Nov 2026";
  • a range wholly inside an earlier year names it once, at the end: "3 Jul – 13 Nov 2025";
  • a row from an earlier year carries its year ("1 Dec 2022"), on one line.

Frames and tests use a fixed "today", so baselines don't change on 1 January.
