O85 SizesThis amends O78; its other rows stand.
  • Experience line: step 0 at the example's cap, 0.056 H, then T2's step ratios: 0.056 / 0.0485 / 0.042 H. The two-line pitch is 1.40 of the cap (0.0786 H at step 0). The width limit is 840 px (x 120–960) on every 9:16, still or clip, replacing 0.86 W on the still. White, centred on 0.50 W.
  • Claim: cap 0.50 of the experience line's cap at its step, replacing 0.55. The gap from the experience line's last baseline to the claim's first cap top is 0.34 of the experience line's cap (0.019 H at step 0), replacing 0.025 H.
  • The block is centred on 0.535 H on a still, replacing 0.50 H. With no claim, the experience line alone is centred on the same line.
