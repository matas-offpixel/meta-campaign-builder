A pin where Meta has no city

Meta has no "Leeds" city in West Yorkshire. Searching "Leeds" returns one city, Leeds in Kent, and that's how six live campaigns came to target Kent. The live ad sets now use a pin on Leeds city centre (53.7997, -1.5492, 80 km). The app's picker can't hold a pin, though, so seven drafts lost Leeds.

This record gives the picker (Step 5, Location targeting) a pin, a Leeds preset, and a warning for a search hit in the wrong place. Nothing else in the picker changes.

Rulings

#Ruling
LP1 · A pin is a location
  • A pin is a name, a point (latitude, longitude) and a radius in km, included or excluded like any other location. At launch it goes to Meta as a custom location.
  • Its row in Locations reads "Leeds (pin) · 80 km".
  • Under the row, its chip reads "+ Leeds (pin) · 80 km · 53.7997, -1.5492", or "−" when it's excluded. The coordinates show as entered, in place of the key a city shows.
  • Its badge reads "pin" when it was added by hand, or "preset" when it came from the Leeds preset.
  • Tiers (Primary, Secondary), removing it, the exclusion pool, and one ad set per location all work as they do for a city.
  • Every Locations row, at any width: the label wraps and is never cut short with "…". At 393 the row stacks: the label and badge, the chip, any amber or kept line at full width, then Primary and Secondary on their own line. ✕ stays top right. Amended 24 Nov from #1370's 393 shots, where "Leeds (pin) · 80 km" was cut to "Leeds (…" and the Kent warning was squeezed into a narrow column.
LP2 · Add a pin
  • A text button, "Add a pin", sits under the search field. It opens a small form in place:
    • Name;
    • Latitude;
    • Longitude;
    • Radius (km), which starts at 40, like a city's;
    • Include or Exclude, using the existing words.
    The form's own line reads: "A pin targets a circle around a point. Use it where Meta has no city."
  • Pasting a pair like "53.7997, -1.5492" into Latitude fills both fields. The line under Latitude says so, because coordinates come from a map as a pair. The quoted pair in that line never breaks across two lines.
  • The Name field's placeholder is "e.g. Leeds". A bare "Leeds" in grey reads as a value already typed, which made "Name the pin." look wrong (amended 24 Nov from #1370's LP-B-name).
  • Checks, shown when Add pin is pressed, each under its own field:
    • a name is required;
    • latitude is -90 to 90;
    • longitude is -180 to 180;
    • radius is 1 to 80 km, Meta's range for a pin.
    "Add pin" adds it to Locations and closes the form. "Cancel" closes it, and nothing is kept.
LP3 · The Leeds preset
  • One new chip in Quick presets, after "UK excl London +40km": "Leeds, West Yorkshire (pin)". Hovering it reads "Leeds city centre, 53.7997, -1.5492, 80 km. Meta has no Leeds city in West Yorkshire."
  • It adds the pin row "Leeds (pin) · 80 km", with the preset badge. It needs no Meta search, because the point is fixed. Pressing it again removes the row, as the other chips do.
LP4 · A hit in the wrong place
  • A search hit is in the wrong place when it's on the known list: Meta locations whose name most people mean for somewhere else. The list is in code and keyed by Meta's own location key, so it matches exactly and never reads Meta's region text. It starts with one entry: key 811209, Leeds in Kent, expected West Yorkshire, with the Leeds preset as its pin. It's added to as cases turn up.
  • When the text typed already names the hit's own region ("Leeds, Kent"), there's no line, because that's what was asked for.
  • Amended 24 Nov, as #1370 built it: the first draft also compared a region typed after a comma with Meta's region text. That's dropped. The hit's label already prints its region, so a region typed and not matched shows on the row. The trap that cost six campaigns was the bare name, which only the list catches.
  • The hit's row in the results gets one amber line: "Leeds, Kent: this is in Kent, not West Yorkshire."
    • When a pin preset exists for that place, its fix is "Use the Leeds pin", which adds the preset instead.
    • Otherwise the fix is "Add a pin", which opens the form with the name filled.
    • The hit can still be added: Leeds in Kent is a real place.
  • Once added, the row in Locations keeps the line and the fix, with a second fix, "Keep Leeds, Kent". Keep clears the line for that draft, and stores who kept it and when, like the launch checks' Keep (RC3). The row then reads, muted, "Leeds, Kent kept by Matas, Tue 24 Nov, 16:40".
  • It's a warning, never a block. Names inside the fix buttons keep their case, as every fix's do (RC8).
LP5 · Drafts that hold Leeds, Kent
  • A draft saved with Leeds, Kent shows LP4's line on its row as soon as it's opened. One click on "Use the Leeds pin" swaps the row for the pin, and keeps its tier and mode.
  • The seven drafts that lost Leeds get the pin back from Ops' list. That's a data job for the builder with Ops, not drawn here.

Acceptance

  • Preset: "Leeds, West Yorkshire (pin)" adds "Leeds (pin) · 80 km". The launched ad set's targeting holds one custom location at 53.7997, -1.5492, 80 km, and no city (the builder's test, with Meta mocked).
  • Search: "Leeds" shows Leeds, Kent (key 811209) with the amber line and "Use the Leeds pin". "Leeds, West Yorkshire" gives the same line, because the hit is the same key. "Leeds, Kent" gives no line, because that's what was asked for.
  • 393: no Locations row label is cut short; each row stacks as LP1 says.
  • Form: pasting "53.7997, -1.5492" into Latitude fills both fields. 81 km shows the radius line, and nothing is added.
  • Drafts: one of the seven, opened before the data job, shows the line on its Leeds, Kent row, and one click swaps it for the pin.
  • Words: every new string on screen is a row, exactly.

Invented here

  • The known list as a rule, keyed by Meta’s location key, and its first entry.
  • Pasting a pair into Latitude.
  • The pin's default radius of 40.
  • The kept line and its time in LP-C.
