    • recipe not built, which names the recipes that are;
    • not a recipe;
    • approval over 1,000 characters;

    • the title is “Let Claude render videos in Off/Pixel”;
    • above the buttons: “If you cancel, it keeps the access it has now.”
  • The page’s apostrophes stay straight.
MA4 · The builder’s choices
  • cta-slugs: signup, presale and tickets (on sale), with no-cta for No text. Accepted.
  • Teaser and announcement: the teaser keeps the lineup back (hook, then date and venue on the drop, then the close), and the announcement puts one name on each cut. Accepted.
  • Default hook: the event’s name as stored, never its code. picks.copy.hook overrides it. Artist-voice copy needs an approved caption, word for word. Accepted.
  • Track: the headliner’s track that has a drop on the Snippet tab, until “best-performing” is measured. Accepted.
  • Hands-up moments: the machine part is accepted. Matas judges the rest when he says yes.
  • A file whose Drive copy failed: its row reads “Rendered, but not in Drive yet: the copy didn’t finish.” in ochre, with “Copy to Drive” and no Drive ↗. It can’t be uploaded from Drive until it’s there.
  • File cap: 24 per package, as proposed. One yes can carry the montage, roll-call and top tracks (21), refuses all four (33), and can never start more than 24 Lambda renders.
