Top tracks, issue 2: drops and bars on studio tracks

From the build on #1338 (comment 6982968275). VW14 is the wrong tool for a master. It was ruled for live clips. On Can't Decide it counted 6–8 "kicks" a second (the hats), found 9 drops, and never settled a beat grid.

This record re-rules how TK3 finds the bars and the drops on studio tracks. Its parts:

  • Beat This! for the beat grid;
  • Demucs's bass stem for the drops;
  • Matas's choice of drop, unchanged;
  • the hook from the new grid.

Live clips keep VW14–VW17, untouched. Both models are the ones the Demucs study ran.

What stands: everything in #1338 except the pieces TK12 names, including:

  • TK3's window shape, its limits and refusals, the fades, the 0.75 s join and the end card;
  • the qualifying test and Matas's choice;
  • the phrase finder's values;
  • TK1–TK2 and TK4–TK11.

Also standing: CT1's window for snippet statics, VW14–VW17 for clips, and the variant-pair record's later box taking CT4's drop.

Can't Decide's length (2:55), its break (2:04) and its bass return (2:07.25–2:07.5) are real, from the Luke Dean session. Every other drop and level in TK-H is invented.

Rulings

#Ruling
TK12 · Studio tracks, and what this replaces
  • A studio track is a file in 02 Source / Audio / {artist} that passes AU1. That covers every track in Top tracks, and every audio snippet static's track (CT1–CT4, #1295).
  • A live clip is a Lab clip (creative_lab_clips). It keeps VW14–VW17 as ruled. This record changes nothing about clips, and clip work goes on separately.
  • For studio tracks, this replaces three pieces of TK3:
    • "The bar grid comes from the track's kicks" (VW14's kick detection, the median gap, 4 beats a bar) → TK13.
    • "The Lab finds every drop in the file as VW14 finds them in a clip" → TK14.
    • "When VW14 misses", the bass-stem fallback → withdrawn. The bass stem is now the main method, not a fallback, and the row's " · found on the bass stem" goes.
  • TK3's repeated-phrase finder keeps its values and now runs on TK13's grid (TK16).
TK13 · The beat grid: Beat This! on the master
  • Beat This! runs on the full mixed master, the same model as the Demucs study. It gives the beats and the downbeats.
  • A bar runs from one downbeat to the next. The grid's own bars are used, with no 4-beats-a-bar assumption.
  • TK3's windows are counted in these bars: the bars nearest 6 s before the drop within 4–8 s, and nearest 8 s after it within 6–10 s.
  • Steadiness. When the bar lengths in the 16 bars around the chosen drop vary by more than 3 %, TK3's seconds cut applies, with its line: "No steady beat found — cut on seconds, not bars."
  • No grid at all: when Beat This! returns no downbeats, the same seconds cut and line apply.
TK14 · Drops: the bass returning, on the bass stem
  • The stem. Demucs separates the bass stem from the master, the same model as the study. Its level is read per beat on TK13's grid, as the RMS from one beat to the next.
  • The reference level R is the median per-beat level over the loudest half of the track's beats.
  • Bass out or well down: a beat at least 10 dB under R. A break is at least 4 beats in a row like that, and at least one bar on the grid.
  • A drop is where the bass returns after a break:
    • the first beat after the break back within 3 dB of R;
    • timed at the downbeat at or nearest that beat, within half a bar;
    • the 8 beats from that downbeat averaging within 3 dB of R. A blip that falls away again isn't a drop.
  • Merging. Returns less than 4 bars apart are one drop, timed at the first of them that passes the 8-beat test.
  • Track start. A track that starts with the bass out counts as a break from the first downbeat, so an intro's bass entry can be a drop.
  • The picker. Each drop is listed as "{m:ss.s} · bass returns after {n} bars", where {n} is the break's whole bars, at least 1, with "1 bar" for one. The operator can drag the marker as before, and it snaps to the nearest downbeat.
TK15 · Which drop (Matas's rule, unchanged)
  • TK3's qualifying test and choice stand, run on TK14's drops:
    • the last drop in the last third that fits the snippet window (the payoff);
    • else the latest earlier one, with "Drop {m:ss} · an earlier drop (no later drop fits)";
    • "Drop {m:ss} · the last drop" for the payoff.
  • With no drop found: "{title}: no drop found — drag the drop marker to it."
TK16 · The hook, from the new grid
  • As ruled in TK3: the last place the track's repeated phrase plays before the chosen drop.
  • The bars are TK13's: phrases of 1, 2 or 4 grid bars, matched at 0.90 or more, at least 3 places. Places starting within 8 bars after any TK14 drop don't count. The 14 s limit and the lines stand.
  • The phrase is still read from the master's mids (200 Hz–5 kHz), not from a stem.
TK17 · Snippet statics (#1295) and variant pairs
  • CT4's drop on an audio snippet static is now TK14's drops with TK15's choice. That replaces TK3's VW14-based amendment.
  • CT1's window stays as ruled: from 4 s before the drop. The hook stays Top tracks only.
  • A variant pair's later box takes this drop. Its earlier box lists TK14's other drops.
  • Existing work. Snippet static drafts re-pick their drop. Approved renders, and drops an operator set or dragged, are kept.
TK18 · When the analysis runs, and the stem
  • When it runs. Beat This! and Demucs run once per file version, when a file is first offered in the Snippet or Top tracks picker. The results are stored with the file, keyed by drive_file_id and Drive's modified time, and run again when either changes. Stored: the beats, downbeats, per-beat bass levels, R and the drops.
  • While it runs, the row reads "Finding the drops…" and the file can't be ticked yet.
  • If it fails, the row reads "Couldn't read this track's beat — drag the drop marker to it.", and the seconds cut applies.
  • The stem is data only. Renders always use the master. The bass stem is never rendered, never filed to Drive and never offered as audio.

Invented here

  • Bass levels: per-beat RMS; R as the median of the loudest half of the beats; "out or well down" at 10 dB under R; back within 3 dB.
  • Breaks and drops: a break of at least 4 beats and one bar; the 8-beat test; the 4-bar merge kept at its first passing return; the half-bar snap; the track-start break.
  • The grid: the 3 % test over 16 bars, kept from TK3, and the no-grid case.
  • Analysis: when it runs and how it's keyed.
  • Words: "Finding the drops…", "Couldn't read this track's beat — drag the drop marker to it." and "{m:ss.s} · bass returns after {n} bars".
  • TK-H: every drop and width other than the real ones named.
