Creative Lab — the caption follows the footage's owner

Matas, 10 Nov: "if using promoter copies we should be using promoter footage (puzzle footage) not artist footage — can't see what Puzzle 4th Birthday looks like when it's not their footage, because it's a different party. Artist footage should be with artist focused copy, i.e. 'Luke Dean set to bring this energy to Circuit to close out the year'. First person UGC captions from artists are approved and provided." This record makes the footage's owner a property of every clip and lets it decide which captions a clip may carry. It extends the Creative Lab records (F1–F5, pass 2 and 3, quote mode Q1–Q9, voice V1–V13, clip selection S1–S27); they stand except where a row says otherwise. Frames at 1280 and 393.

Rulings

#Ruling
W1 Footage ownerEvery clip has an owner: promoter (the client's own footage, from any of its events — what a Puzzle night looks like) or artist (one named artist's footage from anywhere else). Stored on the clip; an artist-owned clip names its artist.
  • Derived at import, from where the clip came from: Instagram from an artist row's handle → that artist. Instagram from the client's own handle (the page the voice corpus reads, e.g. @puzzle) → promoter. The artist's clip folder, press kit or 02 Source / Artist clips / {artist} → that artist. 02 Source / Venue & crowd → promoter. An upload → the folder its S23 question chose.
  • Corrected by hand, once: the tile's ⋯ gains "Whose footage is this?" with two choices, "{Client}'s" and "{artist}'s" (the artists on the event). It saves with who and when (never on screen, rule 5). Use it when, say, an artist's own reel was filmed at one of the client's nights.
  • Changing the owner never changes the rights: a clip from an artist's account keeps that artist's rights chip whoever it's credited to.
  • The 26 existing clips are derived from their source: all artist-owned. No backfill guess is needed.
W2 How the tile shows itBy grouping, not a new mark. The Clips section groups tiles by owner: "{Client} footage" first, then "{artist} footage" for each artist in the lineup's order. Each group header carries one muted line saying what it takes: "the promoter's own clips · Lineup, POV and Scarcity" and "{artist}'s clips · Artist and {artist}'s own words". A promoter tile is named "{Client} #{n}" ("Puzzle #1"), an artist tile as today ("Enzo #1"). The clip tile itself is unchanged (source badge, length, name, one status, tick). Selection (S1–S5) works across groups; "{n} of {m} selected" counts all of them. At 393 the groups stack, each a two-across grid.
W3 Angles by owner
  • Promoter footage takes Lineup, POV and Scarcity (the rail's "Event announcement" maps to Lineup). These are about the night, so they need the night's footage.
  • Artist footage takes Artist — artist-focused, third person, in the promoter's voice ("Luke Dean set to bring this energy to Circuit to close out the year") — and that artist's approved quote, verbatim (quote mode, Q1–Q9).
  • "Artist, first person" is no longer generated. First-person words are the artist's own: they come approved and provided, through quote mode. The generator's Angle row becomes Lineup · Artist · POV · Scarcity, and Artist writes third person.
  • Voice (V-rulings): every generated angle now reads the promoter's page — the promoter is the one speaking, including about the artist. The artist's own corpus is no longer a writer's source; it is kept, and the voice chip's sheet still shows it.
  • An Artist caption names its artist; it is written for, and only pairs with, that artist (W4).
W4 Pairing never crossesA caption pairs only with clips its angle allows: Lineup, POV and Scarcity with promoter clips; an Artist caption or a quote with its own artist's clips. Pair with's three modes (S6) read only those: Selected clips spreads each caption across its allowed selected clips; Best of selected picks the best of its allowed selected clips; Each selected makes one preview per allowed pair, never a crossed one. A caption with no allowed clip selected gets no preview and one line in its place (W5). Rounds (S7) are per owner: the promoter's round has its selected promoter clips down and its Lineup / POV / Scarcity captions across; an artist's round has that artist's selected clips down and that artist's Artist captions and quote across. A crossed cell never exists, so it is never "off" and never counted.
W5 No promoter footage
  • The "{Client} footage" group opens on its empty state (rule 8), one sentence and one action: "No Puzzle footage yet. Lineup, POV and Scarcity need it." · Find clips on @puzzle (the existing Find clips on the client's handle; candidates arrive as candidate tiles and Add brings them in as promoter clips) · and the muted line "or drop them in 02 Source / Venue & crowd" (the folder import, S22; a clip there imports as promoter footage).
  • In the generator, Lineup, POV and Scarcity stay selectable — a caption can be written before its footage arrives — and the why line says: "Lineup needs Puzzle footage — none selected yet."
  • A Lineup, POV or Scarcity caption with nothing to pair with shows its feed post with a dashed frame and "Needs Puzzle footage" in it, and can still be ✓'d; it joins the promoter's round once promoter clips are selected.
  • The strip's Next line (F1) points at the gap when no caption can be paired: "Add Puzzle footage — 20 Lineup captions are waiting for it."
  • A client with no Instagram handle on file gets "Add {Client}'s Instagram" instead of Find clips, as the artist row does.
W6 The approved quoteAn artist's quote pairs only with that artist's own clips — never with promoter footage and never with another artist's (W4). It runs only on the channels it was cleared for:
  • The quote's clearance (who on the artist's side approved it, and when, kept on hover per rule 5) may name channels. When it does, those channels are the limit. When it doesn't, the artist's rights chip decides, as Q4: "Promoter page only" holds it; "✓ Cleared — artist channels too" lets it run from the promoter's page and the artist's own.
  • Luke Dean's quote (typed verbatim, approved by his team on 9 Nov, no channels named) pairs with his 21 clips and, with his chip at "✓ Cleared — artist channels too", may run from Puzzle's page and his own. Its feed post adds one line: "pairs with Luke Dean's clips only".
  • A quote is never edited, shortened or re-cased by the Lab (Q-rulings unchanged).
W7 Captions already proposedThe caption is kept; the crossed pairing goes.
  • A proposed caption paired with a clip its angle doesn't allow is unpaired: the pairing is cleared, the caption stays proposed, and its card says once "Unpaired — Lineup needs Puzzle footage." On Puzzle 4th Birthday that is the 11 Lineup captions on Enzo's clips; they join the 9 already unpaired.
  • Existing generated "Artist, first person" captions (none on this event) are retired: kept, not ✓-able, with "First-person words come from the artist — use Quote." A retired caption already ✓'d stays ✓'d but enters no new round.
  • Nothing is hidden and nothing is deleted. A round that ran keeps every pairing it ran with (S5); a render that exists stays. Only new previews and rounds follow W4.
