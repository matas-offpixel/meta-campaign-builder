The caption finds a place to work

Matas, on the 12 Nov caption runs: "something's not right here, need to be less rigid — the caption overlay should find a place to work, reducing the font size of it if needed."

Today a UGC caption sits in one place:

  • its block centred on 0.355469 H (y 682.5 at 1920), as rule 13 measured it from Luke Dean - UGC 1.mp4 (v1.json verticalCenterPctOfSourceHeight);
  • centred on 0.493 W inside O99's box, x 120–944.

It never moves. On 12 Nov the ops run applied T1's face rule (O39, as amended by O56 and O64) to that one box. Luke 4, 6 and 7 and Enzo 1, 4 and 5 were refused at every in-point from 14 % to 82 %, and Luke has no new caption ad.

This record keeps the face rule and lets the caption move, then shrink, before it refuses. The caption style itself, rule 13 and v1.json, is unchanged.

Rulings

#Ruling
C1 The placement searchThe caption block may sit anywhere in O96's band whose ink stays inside it: x 120–944 (O99), first ink at y ≥ 353, last baseline at y ≤ 1238.
  • Candidates: today's spot (centre y 682.5), then steps of 0.02 H (38.4 px) up and down from it, as far as the band allows. At full size a four-line block reaches 5 steps up and 11 down.
  • The test: O39 in the block's own box, using T1's judge as it is (judgeFaceOverTime):
    • the caption's line boxes are the always boxes, with no claim;
    • 5 samples a second;
    • a face may be in the box for ≤ 10 % of samples and ≤ 0.5 s in a row;
    • only faces ≥ 0.08 W on the source (O64) and ≥ ½ the widest face count;
    • only the upper 60 % of a face is tested (O56);
    • the type box is padded as T1's is.
  • Which passing place wins: the one nearest today's spot. When up and down are equally near, up wins. This changes the proposal, which took the place with the least face time. The pass already keeps the face clear, and the measured spot is where native captions sit. "Least face time" would send a caption to the top or bottom edge whenever that edge reads 0 %, even with a passing place one step from home.
  • One place for the whole clip, with no jumping. The block stays centred on 0.493 W, as now.
  • The crop isn't searched: the clip's stored crop stands. Only the type moves.
C2 Size stepsIf no place passes at a size, the caption steps down one size and the search runs again (C1).
  • Steps, as fractions of the standard size (fontSizePctOfWidth 0.051389 W, 55.5 px at 1080):
    • 1.0: 55.5 px, ≈ 20.2 pt on a 393-pt-wide phone.
    • 0.85: 47.2 px, ≈ 17.2 pt.
    • 0.75: 41.6 px, ≈ 15.1 pt. This is the existing long size, 0.038542 W.
    • 0.65: 36.1 px, ≈ 13.1 pt. This is the floor.
  • The floor: 0.65 of the standard size (0.033403 W).
    • At 13 pt on a phone the cap is about 9 pt, the size of iOS's footnote text.
    • Below that, white type with a 2 px outline over moving footage reads as small print, not as something a person typed on their own clip.
    • Feed shows the same 9:16 at the same width in its 4:5 crop, so the floor holds there too.
  • Long captions (73–96 characters) start at 0.75 and have one step, to 0.65.
  • Lines are never rewrapped. This changes the proposal. The writer's lines are phrases (one phrase per line, ≤ 18 characters, or ≤ 24 in a long caption), and they are what Matas approved. Smaller type keeps the same lines, so the block gets shorter and narrower, which is what clears a face. Rewrapping to longer lines would make the block wider and change the copy.
  • Everything else scales with the em, as the long size does: stroke 0.054054 em, line height 1.054 em, the 0.493 W centre, and wdth 120 / wght 400.
  • Order across in-points: where the run tries several in-points, every in-point is searched at a size before the next size down. The caption stays as large as any in-point allows. Within a size, the run's own in-point order stands, then C1's nearest place.
C3 The refusalOnly after every place × size, at every in-point the run tries, fails:
  • a caption reads No place on this clip keeps the caption off the face — choose another clip or in-point.;
  • a quote reads No place on this clip keeps the quote off the face — choose another clip or in-point.
These replace T1's Face in the type for {n} s at a time — choose another crop or clip on caption and quote renders. T1 keeps its own line.
C4 The 12 Nov ops choices
  • O39's thresholds on the caption block: confirmed, as amended by O56 and O64. This is the test C1 and C2 search against.
  • Contrast on a caption: measured as O40 measures it, stored on the render, and never refused. Rule 13's white fill with a black outside stroke reads on any ground; that is what the stroke is for.
  • O44 per ad, not per clip: confirmed. One ad never carries both a caption and T1's type. The same footage may make a caption ad and a T1 ad.
C5 Quotes and long captions
  • Long captions: C1–C3 as written, from 0.75 (C2).
  • Quotes (Q2): the same search from the quote's own measured spot (block centre 0.28 H, y 537.6), in the same steps and band.
    • No size steps: Q2's font (≈ 0.0193 H, 37.1 px) is already at the floor of 36.1 px.
    • Contrast is part of the pass: a quote has no stroke (Q2), so C4's reason doesn't hold for it. A place passes only if the quote's line boxes also measure ≥ 3:1 against the footage on O40's p95 sample.
    • No fallback to the caption style: Q2 stays locked, and a quote with no place is refused with C3's quote line.
  • T1 and T2 renders never carry a caption (O44, O83). The search exists only on caption and quote renders.
C6 What the Lab saysOn the render row and the round cell, one 11 px mono ink-3 line, only when the caption isn't at today's spot at full size:
  • Moved up to keep off the face
  • Moved down to keep off the face
  • Smaller to keep off the face — 85 % (or 75 %, 65 %)
  • Moved up and smaller to keep off the face — 85 % (and the same with down)
A long caption at 0.75 is at its own size and says nothing about size. The refusal (C3) sits where refusal lines sit today, in brick. The words never name steps, pixels or the judge.
C7 Stored, and the re-run
  • Stored on each caption render: the chosen centre y (px at 1920) and the size fraction, beside the stored face share, longest run and contrast.
  • The preview and Lambda read the same two numbers (O99's same-box rule), so the Lab's preview shows the caption where it will render.
  • Renders already made stay as they are.
  • The re-run makes the refused Luke 4, 6, 7 and Enzo 1, 4, 5 under C1–C3.
