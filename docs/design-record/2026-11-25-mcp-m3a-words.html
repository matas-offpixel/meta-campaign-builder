  • the answers and refusals of launch_draft, upload_draft_assets, set_draft_name, attach_drive_creatives (pairs and any folder) and update_event.

Rulings

#Ruling
MW1 · The consent page, with Meta
  • Line 2 (consent.line2.meta), when the scope includes mcp:meta. It describes the whole scope, not just M3a’s tools, so M3b’s live changes don’t need new consent words:
    • what it can read and draft;
    • sending files, launching paused or live, changing live campaigns, and changing event dates and links;
    • each one only after a yes in Claude;
    • the ceiling, and the log on Today.
  • When a drafts grant adds Meta:
    • the title is “Let Claude make Meta changes in Off/Pixel” (an app on this computer has its own form);
    • above the buttons: “If you cancel, it keeps the drafts access it has now.”
    A read-only grant adding Meta keeps M2’s note (“…the read-only access it has now.”).
  • The page’s apostrophes stay straight, as on its other lines.
MW2 · Told to Claude
  • Plain text, in FC2’s form:
    • refusals start “Refused:”;
    • each ends with what didn’t happen: “Nothing was sent to Meta.”, “Nothing changed.” or “Nothing was saved.”
  • launch_draft answers with:
    • Done;
    • whether it’s paused (“nothing spends until it’s switched on”) or live (“up to £{x} a day”);
    • the Ads Manager link;
    • for a paused launch, the Undo note.
  • A launch that stops part way says how far it got: the campaign id, and “{a} of {n} ad sets”. Paused, Matas can delete it on Today; live, Claude is told to pause it.
  • An upload that fails:
    • counts the files, “{f} of {n} files”, with one-forms;
    • names the existing slot words, “Upload failed — retry or remove”;
    • gives the two ways on;
    • lists each failed file by name, with “Meta didn’t take it.” or “Meta couldn’t process the video.”
  • update_event answers with the code and each field as “{field}: {before} → {after}”, using “not set” and “cleared”, then “Only the app changed; nothing went to Meta.”
  • Tool descriptions are Claude’s documentation, not rows. They can stay as written, but must not contradict a row.
