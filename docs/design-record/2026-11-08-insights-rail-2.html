R · Rulings added in issue 2

#Ruling
R12 Build-1 corrections(a) The event page's sections are caption angle, hook, visual format, asset type, creatives, Lab; H4 in issue 1 left asset type off and #1102 built it in correctly. (b) Colour reads the stored shrunk_index, never cpr ÷ baseline: Billboard (0.964) is ink. (c) "+n more" counts the non-thin rows not drawn: Visual format reads "+3 more", Hook "+5 more", Asset type "+1 more", Caption angle none. (d) The confidence line's funded count is the minimum of the drawn rows: angle "225+", hook "54+", visual "15+", asset "10+".
R13 Row orderNon-thin rows sort by stored shrunk_index ascending (cheapest first); the first four draw; the rest are "+n more". Thin rows never take a slot. Ties break by funded ads descending. Visual format therefore draws Nostalgia, Headline, Billboard, POV.
R14 What TikTok may borrowMechanism learnings carry across platforms; costs do not. On a TikTok surface the caption angle, hook and visual format sections may show the client's Meta learnings, with the section kicker reading from Meta ads and one ochre line at the top of the group: "TikTok ads aren't tagged yet. These angles, hooks and formats come from {client}'s Meta ads; the costs are Meta costs." Their bars keep Meta's £ / result and %, so nothing is converted or implied. Asset type does not carry (TikTok is video only). The TikTok drawer's own section, Who clicks on TikTok, uses TikTok's own units: £ / click and 2-s view rate (hook), by age and gender, this client only, with a baseline tick at the client's blended TikTok figure. For a rate (views ÷ impressions) higher is better, so olive is ≥ 1.05 of the blended figure and brick ≤ 0.95; for a cost the R6 direction holds. Until TikTok conversions are stored, no TikTok section says "signup" or "ticket".
R15 Lookalikes and retargetingThe empty state names its unlock: "Not measured yet. Appears once results are stored per audience (source, lookalike range, cost per result), from the next nightly run after that job ships." When filled: one bar per audience source (page lookalike 1 %, 1–3 %, pixel retargeting, customer list…), cost per result and % vs the client's usual, same rules as R6–R8, action Use audience which pre-selects it in the builder.
R16 Not shownTikTok interest categories (stored as ids, split pro rata) and region are not drawn until they carry a per-row result. A section that would only show a pro-rata split is omitted, not faked (as H10).
