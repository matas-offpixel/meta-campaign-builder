Copy a campaign to another event without carrying the old one with it

Copying a campaign caused three live mistakes this week:

  • Glitterbox Bristol's campaign, copied to Newcastle, kept Bristol's account and pixel.
  • Paul Van Dyk's link was left on Dave Pearce's ads.
  • A Lewis Carroll copy was left under the source campaign's name.

The cause is that a copy re-derives only the event and its code. This record gives words and frames to the six surfaces in the brief (scratch/campaign-replicate/brief.md, R1–R8). Each copy now re-derives everything that belongs to the event, says what it changed, and can't launch while anything from another show is left in it.

Scope. Matas overrides the C1–C4 freeze for this record (24 Nov). The words in lib/launch-guard/words.ts (CC-2) and every surface in CC-1, CC-3 and CC-4 take their words from this record only. R5's nightly alert words aren't ruled here; they come with that cron. Frames use this week's Electric cases. Where a figure isn't from the brief it's marked invented.

Rulings

#Ruling
RC1 · Replicate to event, the entry and the picker
  • In a published campaign's ⋯ menu, the first item is "Replicate to event…", sub-line "Another event: account, pixel, link and copy follow it". "Duplicate" stays directly under it, with the sub-line "Same event, same settings". Nothing else in the menu moves.
  • The dialog is titled "Replicate to event" and asks "Which event is this for?". It lists the client's events, upcoming first, each with its name, code, venue and show date. The source's own event stays in the list, marked "this campaign's event". Picking it makes a plain duplicate.
RC2 · What changed, what was kept
  • The title: "Replicate {source name} to {target code}".
  • "Changes to match {code}" lists one line per re-derived field, in this order. Each line is the new value, then "(was {old})":
    • Event;
    • Name;
    • "Account: {account name}";
    • "Pixel …{last 4}";
    • "Link: {url} · {n} ads";
    • Dates;
    • Copy: "Rewritten from the {voice} templates for {artist} at {venue}";
    • Audiences: "{n} found in {account} · {m} will be created".
    A field that doesn't change isn't listed.
  • "Kept from the source" is one line: objective, ad sets, budgets, placements, interests.
  • The page question is boxed, and must be answered before "Create draft" turns on: "Ads run from {page}: keep or change", with "Keep {page}" or "Change".
  • A venue with no stored account or pixel reads amber: "{Field}: not set for {venue}, check". The draft can still be made, and RC3 blocks its launch.
  • "Create draft" never launches. The footnote reads "Nothing launches. Launch runs the checks." The new draft opens with the line "Replicated from {source name}, {date}. Launch runs the checks first."
RC3 · Launch checks
  • Order: on Review & launch, blockers come first, in the order the checks run, then warnings.
  • Count line: "● {n} to fix before launch" in brick and "● {n} to check" in ochre. When nothing is found, the line reads "● Nothing to fix" in olive, under it "Account, pixel, link and names match {code}.", and Launch is on.
  • Launch button: it reads "Launch · {n} to fix" and stays off while any blocker is left.
  • Each check shows a short key word, its sentence, an evidence line (where it was found, with ad names listed up to four, then "and {n} more"), and the fix it offers. Every sentence is in the word table below.
  • A warning never blocks. "Keep this link" clears it for this draft, and the check stays clear as long as the link is unchanged.
  • No false hits: an artist who plays both shows, a name under 4 characters, and an event-level pixel override never fire a check (CC-2's tests).
RC4 · Venue card, event override, link
  • Venue card: each venue card in Clients › {client} › Venues holds an Ad account and a Pixel, each a picker. Values show as name · …{last 4}.
  • Page and Instagram aren't on the venue. The card ends with the line "Facebook page and Instagram aren't set per venue: ads often run from artist pages. Set them in creative setup."
  • Event override: an event shows its account and pixel with "from the venue". Overriding one marks it with an ochre dashed edge and the line "Set for this event. The venue's is …{last 4}.", followed by the link "Use the venue's". This is how Sheffield's Skiddle pixel is set.
  • Signup page and ticket link: the event's Signup page is its CIRQLIN page ("from the CIRQLIN page"), and its Ticket link sits under it.
  • Creative setup's Link: a three-way switch, "Event page · Ticket link · Other", defaulting to Event page. "Other" is the override RC3's warning respects. "Ads run from" is in creative setup only.
RC5 · Audience templates tab
  • The tab: its own tab, "Audience templates" with a BETA chip, beside "Audiences". The Audiences tab and its flow don't change.
  • Picking a template: the four templates are chips: House & disco · House & garage · Techno · Commercial house. A template shows its families and shows, who confirmed it and when, and the ad account it's being read in.
  • An unconfirmed template reads "Not confirmed yet" and "Matas confirms a template before it's used." It can't be applied.
  • Items come in four groups, in this order: Customer lists · Retargeting pools · Artist page engagers · Interests. Each item shows "Reused" (olive: it exists in this ad account) or "Will be created" (dashed). A reused item never calls Meta.
  • The footer: "{n} reused · {m} will be created in {account}". When audience writes are off, it adds "Creating audiences is switched off, so these {m} wait."
  • Adding pages: "Add page to this group" sits at the foot of Artist page engagers. A change shows "{n} change since this template was saved" and "Save to template".
RC6 · Campaign tree on the event
  • The tree: the event's Campaigns section lists its stages, Signup then On sale (and Presale and Show week when they have campaigns). Each stage lists its campaigns, each with its status and ad set count. The Signup master carries a "master" chip.
  • Additions: under each campaign, its additions run oldest first. Each reads "+ {n} ads added" or "+ audience added", then the date, then "to {n} ad sets" (or "{audience} → {n} ad sets"), then who. One the on-sale refresh proposed adds "proposed by the on-sale refresh".
  • Stage actions: each stage header carries "Add creatives to…" and "Add audience to…". The sheet lists that stage's campaigns, with their ad sets under each:
    • live ones start ticked;
    • paused ones start unticked;
    • each campaign shows "{n} of {m} ad sets".
    The button reads "Add to {n} ad sets in {m} campaigns", and the footnote reads "Logged under each campaign."
  • The menu: these replace "Add to campaign" and "Add audience to ad sets" in the menu.
RC7 · Caption approval
  • A new artist-voice caption reads "Draft for artist team" in ochre. Its actions are "Copy to send" and "Record the OK".
  • "Record the OK" asks for:
    • Who;
    • When (defaults to now, in form 2);
    • How (WhatsApp · Email · Instagram · In person).
    "Save the OK" turns the caption to "Approved by {name}, {date}, {channel}" in olive.
  • Editing an approved caption turns it back to ochre: "Edited after the OK · needs a new one".
  • Venue-voice captions read "Venue voice · no OK needed".
  • RC3's artist-caption check blocks any ad whose primary text is an artist-voice caption that isn't approved.

Acceptance

  • Glitterbox: Replicate [EBR27-GLITTERBOX] SIGNUP to NX27-GLITTERBOX. The summary reads Account: Electric NX (was Electric Bristol), Pixel …2763 (was …4093) and the Newcastle link. The draft that's made launches clean.
  • Dave Pearce: a draft holding PVD's link shows "Still points to Paul Van Dyk: paulvandyk-newcastle.com in {n} ads." and can't launch. After "Use the event's page" it launches.
  • Lewis Carroll: a copy left under the source's name shows the name check, and "Rename to …" clears it.
  • Sheffield: a Skiddle pixel set on the event shows as an override, and no check fires.
  • Templates: Audience templates on a target account with existing audiences makes zero Meta calls for the "Reused" items. The Audiences tab's behaviour is unchanged (CC-3's test).
  • Tree: "Add creatives to…" on On sale writes one campaign_additions row per campaign, and each shows in the tree with its date.
  • Captions: an artist caption with no recorded OK blocks the launch. Recording the OK clears it. Editing the caption afterwards blocks it again.
  • Words: every string on screen is in the word list, exactly.
