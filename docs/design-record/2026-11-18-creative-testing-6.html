Creative testing issue 6: a winner has to beat the show

Matas, 18 Nov, on #1339's flag: judge test variants against the show's benchmark, not just against each other. A variant that beats its rival but costs more per result than the show's main campaign isn't a winner. Nothing is promoted, the test runs 4 more days, and then it's stopped unless a variant has come close enough to earn 4 more (12 days at most).

What this amends: TF2's call. A winner now also needs the benchmark (TF5), and the day-4 "No clear winner" gains an extension and a stop (TF6). TF3's rows and TF-A / TF-C's words gain the benchmark (TF7). It also re-rules VP5, and amends VP6 and VP8, of the variant-pair record (#1339, merged as d8974e6) (TF8).

What stands: TF1 (the budget, always TF1's, never a fixed £10), TF2's read otherwise (daily from 48 h, 60 % of spend, 3 results, no cheaper judged variant, 10 % to be judged), TF3, TF4, TT8–TT11, R3's result, R5's cap and #1297's cap sentence. It applies to every test TF2 calls: CT6's tests and variant pairs (#1339), and any creative test after them.

The figures in the example are invented. The £3.00 benchmark is Matas's example figure.

Rulings

#Ruling
TF5 · The benchmark
  • The benchmark is the show's main campaign cost per result:
    • on the same result event as the test (R3: checkouts, else landing-page views, or signups on a signup campaign);
    • over the test's own days, from the London day its ad set starts to the read.
  • The main campaign is every live campaign of the show on that event except {code} · Tests (TT8), the Purchase campaign and Retargeting. With more than one, the benchmark is their summed spend ÷ their summed results.
  • A variant wins only when both hold:
    • it meets TF2's read;
    • its cost per result is at or under the benchmark.
    "Ready to call" waits for both, from the 48 h read on.
  • No benchmark. When the main campaign has no results on that event over the test's days, TF2 decides alone. The result line says "no show benchmark for these days".
TF6 · All above the benchmark: extend once, then stop
  • At day 4, if no variant is at or under the benchmark, there's no winner and nothing is promoted: no TT4 promotion lines.
  • The test is extended once, by 4 days.
    • Its ad set's end moves to 23:59 London on day 8, at the same daily budget.
    • The extra hold (the daily budget × 4) is checked against R5's 20 % cap first. If it doesn't fit, there's no extension: the test stops at day 4 with TF7's stop line.
    • The end-date write goes through the Tests campaign path (TT8–TT9), behind the same gate.
  • During the extension the daily read goes on, over all the test's days. A variant that meets TF2's read and the benchmark wins as usual.
  • At day 8 with no winner, the test is stopped: its ad set is paused, and nothing is promoted.
    • A variant has improved toward the benchmark when its cost per result over the 8 days is lower than at day 4's read, or it has results where it had none.
    • Neither improved: Matas's line, "Stopped: both above the show's £{benchmark} per {result}".
    • One improved but is still above: it gets one more 4-day extension (Matas, 18 Nov) only when both hold:
      • its cost per result over the 8 days is at least 20 % lower than at day 4's read;
      • it's within 30 % of the benchmark, at or under the benchmark × 1.3.
      A variant with no results at day 4 has nothing to be 20 % lower than, so it qualifies on the 30 % test alone.
    • Otherwise it stops at day 8: "Stopped: {label} came down to £{cost}, still above the show's £{benchmark} per {result}".
    • With more than two variants, "both" reads "all {n}".
  • The second extension moves the ad set's end to 23:59 London on day 12, at the same daily budget. Its hold is checked against R5's cap first, as every extension's is. With no room, the test stops at day 8 with the "came down to" line.
  • At day 12 with no winner, the test stops and its ad set is paused. That's the most it runs: 12 days.
    • The line is "Stopped: {label} came down to £{cost}, still above the show's £{benchmark} per {result}", with the variant's cost over the 12 days, while that's still lower than at day 4.
    • Otherwise it's Matas's "Stopped: both above the show's £{benchmark} per {result}".
  • A worked case (invented): a benchmark of £3.00 per checkout, and a variant at £5.00 at day 4 and £3.60 at day 8. That's 28 % lower and 20 % above the benchmark, so it's extended again. At £4.20 by day 8 it would be 16 % lower and 40 % above, so it stops.
TF7 · The words
  • Each variant's figure carries the benchmark, on the panel, the share link and the result: "£{cost} per {result} · show £{benchmark}". A variant with no results reads "— per {result} · show £{benchmark}".
  • The result line:
    • Panel: "Winner: {label} · {share} % of spend · £{cost} per {result} · show £{benchmark}".
    • Share link: "Winner: {label} · £{cost} per {result} · show £{benchmark}".
  • The state lines:
    • During the first extension: "Extended to {d Mon}: both above the show's £{benchmark} per {result}" ("all {n}" for more than two).
    • During the second: "Extended again to {d Mon}: {label} came down to £{cost}, within 30 % of the show's £{benchmark} per {result}".
    • After it: TF6's stop lines. Both stop lines show on the panel and on the share link.
  • Without a benchmark, the figure reads "£{cost} per {result}", and the result line ends " · no show benchmark for these days".
  • TF3's spend share stays the first figure on each row. The benchmark follows the cost.
TF8 · Variant pairs (#1339)

  • VP6: a pair's result lines carry TF7's " · show £{benchmark}". Its stop line is Matas's: "Stopped: both above the show's £{benchmark} per {result}".
  • VP8: an extended pair's hold grows by its daily budget × 4 for each extension. At P26-BDAY's £1.00 a day that's £4.00 to £8.00 a pair, and £12.00 after a second extension. The two pairs hold £16.00 with one extension each, and £24.00 at the most. Each extra hold is checked against the cap first (TF6).
  • VP-B and VP-C's words, with a £3.00 benchmark (invented):
    • The payoff drop's £0.84 is under it, so VP-B still reads "Ready to call".
    • VP-B's rows read "£0.84 per checkout · show £3.00" and "£1.48 per checkout · show £3.00".

Acceptance

  • No winner above the show: a variant above the benchmark never reads "Ready to call" or "Winner", and never gets a promotion line.
  • The extensions: one of 4 days when all variants are above at day 4, and a second only on TF6's two tests. Each happens only when its hold fits the cap. The end dates are 23:59 London on day 8 and day 12. No test runs more than 12 days.
  • The stop: at day 8 or day 12 with no winner, the ad set is paused and the right stop line shows.
  • The words: every variant figure carries " · show £{benchmark}", and TF7's lines are exact.
  • The example: it reads as above at day 4 and day 8.
