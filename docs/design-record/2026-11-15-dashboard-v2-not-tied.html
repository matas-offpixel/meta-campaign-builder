Rulings

#Ruling
PT1 · "Not tied to a show"

A send on the client's own account or audience that matches neither one show nor the client's whole list is not tied. Examples are a one-off list, a mix of shows, "all except the show's tag", or an audience that couldn't be read.

  • In the Show column it reads "Not tied to a show". That's on the client view and the venue view, internal and share link alike.
  • It counts in the client's channel totals and in no show. On a venue view it counts only when its Mailchimp audience is that venue's, as PI6 rules for whole-list sends.
  • The totals line names it. The line becomes "{client} · {n} sends · {first day} – {last day} · includes {w} whole-list sends and {t} sends not tied to a show". A clause with 0 is left out, and so is "and" with it. With one send, each clause is singular: "1 whole-list send", "1 send not tied to a show".
  • Internal only: when sends are not tied because their audience couldn't be read, add one review line: "{n} sends' audiences couldn't be read, so they aren't tied to a show." With one send it reads "1 send's audience couldn't be read, so it isn't tied to a show." That's missing data, not a send choice.
PT2 · A show's lists, compared exactly

A send is a show's when every list it targets is one of that show's:

  • CQ-{slug}, CQ-{slug}-Together or CQ-{slug}-opted-in, where {slug} is the show's CIRQLIN page slug. The tiers hold that show's signups only, so they're accepted.
  • A list or tag named exactly the show's tag or event code. A Mailchimp tag also has to sit on the show's audience when the event names one.

The comparison is exact: byte for byte, never trimmed, case-folded or otherwise normalised. Lists and tags are matched verbatim, as everywhere else in the pipeline. A list named "cq-kream" isn't "CQ-kream".

PT3 · The umbrella page
  • An umbrella event is never a show for attribution. That's events.is_umbrella, for example nx-loves-aw26 on NX26-GLOBAL.
  • Its lists (CQ-{slug} and its two tiers) are the client's whole list, so they read "Whole list". On a venue view they count when the umbrella event's venue is that venue, whether the send went by Bird or by Mailchimp.
  • This keeps D1's rule that the umbrella stays apart from shows.
PT4 · "Whole list" needs a positive match

Amends PI6. A send reads "Whole list" only when one of these holds:

  • it went to no list or segment at all;
  • it went only to the client's whole-list tags, for example "NX Loves" on Electric's NX Newcastle audience;
  • it went only to umbrella lists (PT3).

Anything else the client owns is not tied (PT1). The whole-list tags are compared exactly (PT2). Where they're stored is a data choice the dashboard doesn't draw.

PT5 · DF1 and DF1S

The sends table in both frames carries one row of each state:

  • a show send;
  • "Whole list";
  • "Not tied to a show", for example "Folamour - Presale" (the fever-105 opt-in list).

The totals line follows PT1. From #1250's read-only dry run on 15 Nov, Electric's 281 sends split into 53 show sends, 196 whole-list sends and 32 not tied (18 Bird and 14 Mailchimp, on the NX Newcastle audience). So the line reads "Electric Group · 281 sends · {first send} – 13 Nov · includes 196 whole-list sends and 32 sends not tied to a show". {first send} is the day of the earliest of the 281. "Folamour - Presale" went on 10 Sep, so it's 10 Sep or earlier, never a day taken from the rows the frame happens to show. The 196 holds once Electric's whole-list tags are set to "NX Loves". If PT3 moves any umbrella sends, the line uses the new counts.
