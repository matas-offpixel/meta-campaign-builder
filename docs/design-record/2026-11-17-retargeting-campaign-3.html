What the tier row says about a show's links

This amends RT13 of 2026-11-17-retargeting-campaign-2.html (#1313), from the build. Meta's pixel stats give origins only (https://host/), never paths, so visits can't be read link by link. This record says what the row shows instead (ops' ruling, confirmed).

Ruling

#Ruling
RT18 · Links in the tier row
  • Per link, its audience's size.
    • Each link is its own audience at its source's longest window: a ticket link's visitors at 30 days, a landing page's at 60.
    • Names: "{code} · Ticket-link visitors · {name} · 30 days" and "{code} · Page visitors · {name} · 60 days".
    • The tier takes these per-link audiences in place of one combined audience at that window, so who is in the tier doesn't change. RT14's shorter windows stay one audience across the links.
    • The row shows Meta's size band for each link. Before Apply it reads "By link: known after create".
  • Per third-party platform, whether the pixel is on it. Yes when the resolved pixel recorded any event from that platform's host in the last 28 days. This says whether the promoter's pixel is on that platform at all, not whether this show's link was visited.
  • Hosts. There's no pixel line for crqln.com, or for any other host that other shows' pages share, because a yes there says nothing about this show. A show's own domain does get the line, because its host is its own.
  • Names.
    • A ticket link is named by its host: Skiddle, RA, Fatsoma, Fixr, DICE, Eventbrite, Ticketmaster or See Tickets. Any other host is "Other ({host})".
    • Two links on one platform are told apart by their shop_name, else their label, in brackets: "Skiddle (Early bird)".
    • A landing page is named by its host and path: "xmpl-whse.com", "crqln.com/xmpl-whse-crowd".
    • The link shown first goes first, then the page's order.
  • The lines, under the tier's audiences, in ink-3:
    • Sizes: "{name} {band} · {name} {band} · …", for example "Skiddle 2,100–2,500 · RA 1,000–1,200 · Fixr below 1,000". Before Apply: "By link: known after create".
    • Pixel: "Pixel on {names}: yes", then " · {names}: no" when some are no. When all are no: "Pixel on {names}: no".
    • When any platform is no, a third line: "A ticket site counts visitors only when your pixel is on it there."
    • Phone: the same lines, joined as at 1280.
    Bottom shows its ticket links. Mid Bottom shows its landing pages, with a pixel line only for a show's own domain.
  • Judged per PR. Each build PR is judged only on the lines it builds. PR B's RB shot shows PR B's own lines, and the rest arrive with later PRs.

Acceptance

  • Names: each link is named as RT18 says.
  • Sizes: each link shows its own audience's size, and "By link: known after create" before Apply.
  • Pixel line: "Pixel on {platform}: yes / no" shows for each third-party platform and a show's own domain, read from the pixel's origins over 28 days, never for crqln.com or another shared host.
  • Per-link audiences: they replace the combined audience at the longest window, and the tier's people don't change.
  • Frames: RA's and RB's link lines at 1280 and 393.
