A small test budget and a fast call

This record carries two re-rulings from Matas (16 Nov) of numbers that 2026-11-16-creative-testing-2.html invented:

  • The budget: a test ad set gets about 10 % of the event's current daily spend, capped at £10 a day.
  • The call: Meta usually picks the winning ad within about 24 hours, so a test is called from its spend share. There's no 7-day wait and no 10-result floor. At £10 a day those would take weeks.

TF1–TF4 replace TT2 and TT3. Everything else in TT stands: the Tests campaign and its Video and Statics ad sets, one test at a time, Test 3's control, the Promote and Pause lines (TT4), the rights mute (TT5) and the surfaces (TT6). TT-A and TT-C are redrawn here as TF-A and TF-C. The example figures are invented.

Rulings

#Ruling
TF1 · The test ad set's budget (replaces TT2)
  • Current daily spend is the event's average Meta spend per day over the last 7 days, across its main campaigns. The Tests campaign isn't counted.
  • The test ad set's daily budget is 10 % of that, rounded down to the whole pound. Two limits apply:
    • it's never more than £10 a day;
    • it's never more than the daily budget of the event's median live main ad set, so a test is never bigger than the event's typical ad set.
  • Meta's minimum: when that comes in under Meta's minimum daily budget, the test uses the minimum.
    • That's £1.00 a day for these ad sets, which bill on impressions. It's the same floor WB8 uses.
    • The build reads Meta's own minimum from the ad account when it applies (min_daily_budget). If Meta's figure differs from £1.00, it uses Meta's.
  • No recent spend: with no Meta spend in the last 7 days, a test can't start, and the line reads "No spend in the last 7 days to size a test from."
  • The plan: a test holds 4 days of its daily budget against the plan (R5). That's the most it can run (TF2). Both test ad sets together still stay within the 20 % cap. Pausing returns what's unspent.
  • The panel line (Matas's words): "£{daily} a day · {pct} % of current daily spend (cap £10)".
    • {pct} is the real share, so a capped test reads lower than 10 %. For example, "£10 a day · 6 % of current daily spend (cap £10)".
    • At the minimum, it adds " · Meta's minimum", for example "£1.00 a day · 15 % of current daily spend (cap £10) · Meta's minimum".
TF2 · The fast call (replaces TT3)
  • Read daily. A running test is read every morning at 08:00 London, not only on refresh Mondays. The read covers spend and results since the test ad set first delivered.
  • The first read is at 48 hours after first delivery. Before that, the test reads "Running · first read at 48 h".
  • Spend share is the signal. It's each variant's share of the test ad set's spend since the start.
  • A variant wins when all three of these hold:
    • it has a clear majority, 60 % or more of the spend;
    • it has at least 3 results, so its cost per result is a number and not one lucky sale;
    • its cost per result isn't worse than any other judged variant's.
  • Barely served: a variant with under 10 % of the spend reads "Not enough spend to judge". It never reads "lost", and it isn't compared on cost.
  • Judged variants are the ones with 10 % or more of the spend. A judged variant with no results counts as worse than the leader once its spend passes the leader's cost per result.
  • Meta's favourite costs more. When a variant has the majority but another judged variant is cheaper per result, nothing wins yet. The test runs on and reads "Running · Meta favours {variant} ({share} % of spend), but {other} is cheaper per {result}".
  • Day 4: with no winner by the read at day 4 (96 hours), the call is "No clear winner". Nothing is promoted, TT4's pause line is offered, and the variants can go into a later test.
  • The states:
    • "Running · first read at 48 h";
    • "Running · {hours} h · {share} % of spend on {leader}";
    • "Running · Meta favours {variant} …" as above;
    • "Ready to call";
    • "Winner: {variant} · {share} % of spend · £{cost} per {result}";
    • "No clear winner by day 4";
    • "Waiting for Test {n−1}".
  • Withdrawn from TT3: the 10-result floor, the day-7 call, clear and narrow, and "Not enough results to call this test".
  • Unchanged: calling is Matas's click, "Call the winner", and a call is stored with its evidence. TT4's lines follow the call, and R6's test for calls stands.
TF3 · The spend-share view
  • Spend share is now the main signal, so on the event page it's the first figure column. Each bar has two marks:
    • a line at 60 %, the majority a winner needs;
    • a faint line at 10 %, below which a variant is barely served.
  • Rows are sorted by spend share, and the leader's bar is olive. Spend, results and cost per result follow.
  • On a phone, each row starts with the share: "{share} % of spend · {variant} · £{cost} per {result} · {results}".
  • The share link still never shows spend share (TT6). Its winner reads "Winner: {variant} · £{cost} per {result}", and barely served rows read "Not enough spend to judge".
TF4 · What carries over
  • Test 3 is still the combined winner against a copy of the best ad, called by the same TF2 rule.
  • A rights mute (TT5) takes the variant out of the share sums from the moment it's paused.
  • The Promote line's evidence now reads "{share} % of spend · £{cost} per {result} · {results} {result}s".

Frames

Expected figures

  • TF-A budget: current daily spend £160. 10 % of that is £16, so the test is capped at £10, and £10 ÷ £160 = 6.25 %, shown as 6 %.
  • TF-A at 48 h: £20.00 spent = £13.20 + £4.20 + £1.80 + £0.80, which is 66 %, 21 %, 9 % and 4 %.
  • TF-A's winner: Track two has 66 % of the spend (60 % or more) and 8 checkouts (3 or more). Its £1.65 per checkout (£13.20 ÷ 8) is no worse than Track one's £2.10 (£4.20 ÷ 2). Track one is the only other judged variant. Tracks five and three are under 10 %, so they're barely served.
  • TF-B budget: current daily spend £6.50. 10 % of that is £0.65, which is under Meta's £1.00, so the test uses £1.00, which is 15.4 % of £6.50.
  • TF-B at day 4: £4.00 spent = £1.92 + £1.64 + £0.44, which is 48 %, 41 % and 11 %. No variant has 60 %, so the call is "No clear winner by day 4".

Acceptance

  • The daily budget follows TF1: 10 % rounded down, at most £10, at most the median live main ad set's budget, and at least Meta's minimum, read from the account. The panel line follows TF1 exactly, with the suffix at the minimum.
  • Reads run daily at 08:00 London. Nothing can be called before 48 hours.
  • A win needs 60 % of spend, 3 results and no cheaper judged variant. A variant under 10 % is never a loser.
  • Day 4 with no winner ends as "No clear winner by day 4", offering the pause line and no promote line.
  • Frames TF-A at 1280 and 393, and TF-B and TF-C at 393, from fixtures.

Invented here

  • "Current daily spend" as the 7-day Meta average across the main campaigns, rounding down to the whole pound, the median-ad-set ceiling, and holding 4 days against the plan.
  • £1.00 as Meta's minimum, read from min_daily_budget, the " · Meta's minimum" suffix, and "No spend in the last 7 days to size a test from."
  • The fast call's numbers: 60 % for the majority (Matas said "clear majority"), 3 results, 10 % for barely served, the 08:00 daily read, the 48-hour first read and the day-4 end.
  • The states: "Running · first read at 48 h", "Running · {hours} h · {share} % of spend on {leader}", the "Meta favours … but … is cheaper" line, "Winner: {variant} · {share} % of spend · £{cost} per {result}" and "No clear winner by day 4".
  • The spend-share bar's 60 % and 10 % marks, and the phone row order.
