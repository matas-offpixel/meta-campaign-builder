On-sale refresh corrections: what counts as a purchase

This record corrects 2026-11-12-on-sale-refresh.html (#1204). That record is merged and stays as it is; where the two differ, this one rules. Everything here came out of the three review rounds on #1205 (P1, shadow proposals).

The main correction is to R3. #1204 proposed a Purchase campaign at 15 "pixel purchases" in 14 days. The purchases figure in ad_daily_insights is purchases attributed to this show's ads, for any show that shares the pixel. Pixels are shared as a rule: every client's shows share one, and julianfijma.com's pixel is shared by six. 22 of Julian Fijma's 24 attributed purchases landed before its presale opened, and Locklead's 21 are 16 from its Signup campaign plus 5 from On Sale. The figure is never this show's sales.

THE CORRECTIONS TO R3
CorrectionRule
C1 · A show's purchases

An event's purchases are the purchases on its own ticket URL, read from the pixel.

  • Trigger: 15 or more of them in 14 days. That proposes the Purchase campaign, and allows budget moves across campaigns on cost per purchase. This replaces #1204's "≥ 15 pixel purchases".
  • Ticketing data checks, it never triggers. When ticketing is linked (4thefans, Eventbrite, manual entries), the panel shows the pixel's share of real sales. A ticketing link doesn't make Meta optimise for this show, so it can't start a Purchase campaign.
  • No URL match, nothing proposed. If the show's purchases can't be told apart on the pixel, there's no Purchase campaign and no cross-campaign move, and the panel says why. Cases: Skiddle (no integration, and its confirmation page doesn't name the show); any ticket site whose confirmation page doesn't name the show; a show with no ticket URL.
  • P4 starts with a count. Its first job is a read-only count of URL-level purchases, 14 days, for every running on-sale event. Matas picks the first Purchase-piece event from that count, not from the attributed figure.
C2 · The attributed figure is labelled

Wherever the pixel-wide figure appears (the panel, the Slack post, line evidence), it is attributed, any show. It is never written as the show's purchases or as a signal.

  • Objective line: the purchase clause goes. "Initiate Checkout — 75 reached the pixel since 30 Oct. Last 14 days: 99 IC."
  • Purchase line until P4 reads URL-level purchases, one form at any count: "Purchase campaign not proposed — this show's own purchases aren't read yet. Attributed to these ads: {n} purchase{s} in 14 days, any show on this pixel."
  • At 393: "No Purchase campaign: {n} attributed purchase{s} in 14 days, any show."
  • The ticketing note stays: "No linked ticketing: the pixel's purchases can't be checked against real sales."
  • Evidence stores the figure as attributedPurchases with scope: "any_show_on_pixel", never as the event's purchases.
C3 · IC counted onceIC is omni_initiated_checkout only. #1204's worked example ("212 reached the pixel since 21 Oct") added initiate_checkout and omni_initiated_checkout, which are the same conversions. The true count is 106 on the On Sale campaign, 111 across the event.

P26-BDAY's shadow run for 12 Nov, data to Wed 11 Nov. Figures match the table (£650.38 · 75 IC since 30 Oct, 99 IC and 1 attributed purchase in 14 days).

F2 · The purchase line once P4 reads URL-level purchases (1280)

The five states the purchase line can take in P4. The counts are examples, not measured. The attributed line stays under each state as a second sentence. The ticketing check shows only when ticketing is linked.

At 393 each state shortens to its first clause plus the count, for example "Purchase campaign: 18 on this show's URL in 14 days." and "No Purchase campaign: Skiddle's confirmation page doesn't name the show."

CorrectionRule
C4 · Cluster bring-back (R2)live_evidence.cprIndex is cost over the baseline, so lower is better. #1204's "index ≥ 1.2" read it upside down. A cluster comes back when the event's client's own perClient entry is at most 0.83, at confidence ok or better. The pooled index across clients is never used. The line reads "Ran for {client} at {index}× its cost per registration · confidence {word} · not used on this event".
C5 · A rebuilt campaign's ad sets (R2)Within a campaign, an ad set is its name. The rebuild's same-named copy is the same ad set: totals combine, and "stopped" means no same-named set is delivering. #1204's F1 brought back the EDM set while its rebuild copy had been delivering since 6 Nov; that line was wrong.
C6 · Ticketing isn't a blocker (R4, Matas)The ticketing note moves out of "Before it can move money" into Objective, with no action shown when the provider has no integration (C2 has the wording). Only a missing budget blocks a line.
C7 · Timing (R1, HR1)The run is at 06:00 UTC on Mondays (06:00 London in winter, 07:00 in summer), after the 03:30 UTC learning-refresh, so it's ready by 08:00 all year. Slack's business hours start at 10:00, so posts the run couldn't send go out at 10:05 UTC on Mondays.
C8 · One "on sale" test (R1, R4)The run and the event page's panel use the same rule: not cancelled, the show still ahead, and either the status is on sale or the general sale date has passed. Brand campaigns are excluded at the page. With no general sale date, "since" counts from the window start.
C9 · Move thresholds (R2)#1204 didn't set them. The build's are accepted for shadow: from a delivering set at ≥ 1.5× the campaign's cost per result to one at ≤ 0.75× with ≥ 5 results in the week. Matas confirms them before Accept exists.
